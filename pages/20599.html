Bearings And Races

NOTE: When replacing or re-packing bearings, grease seal replacement is required. You are not required to replace these components in axle sets. Determine the need to replace based upon the individual component conditions that follow.
Condition Code Procedure
Bearing end-play exceeds specifications B Require adjustment of bearing, if possible
NOTE: If proper adjustment can't be obtained, require replacement of bearing assembly.
Bearing rollers, balls or races are worn, pitted, noisy, or feel rough when rotated as an assembly B Require replacement of bearing assembly
