Wireless Charger Symptom Troubleshooting Index (5-door) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021)

Symptom Diagnostic procedure Check for
Wireless charger indicators blink (green and amber) Check the wireless charger
  • Foreign matters between the wireless charger and electronic device
  • Misalignment of the electronic device
Wireless charger indicator blinks (amber) Check for DTCs with the HDS; if a DTC is indicated, troubleshoot the DTC first. Wireless charger
Wireless charger indicators do not come on (green and amber) Symptom troubleshooting 
  • Open in +B BACK UP wire
  • Open in GND wire or poor ground (G503)
  • Wireless charger
