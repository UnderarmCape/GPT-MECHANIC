DTC U0056-00 (With CAN gateway) (2019 2020 2021)

DTC U0056-00:  CAN Gateway F-CAN ch C Bus Off

General Description 

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

The CAN gateway performs self-diagnosis to detect the state where the CAN frame cannot be sent due to failure in the F-CAN line. If the bus-off occurs due to open and short circuit in the F-CAN line is detected for a prescribed time, the CAN gateway detects a malfunction and stores a DTC.

Monitor Execution, Sequence, Duration, DTC Type 

Execution Continuous
Sequence None
Duration 6 seconds or more
DTC Type One drive cycle, MIL on or off (CAN gateway does not output MIL on/off request)

Enable Conditions 

Condition Minimum Maximum
Elapsed time since the 12 volt battery voltage reaches to a specified value after the vehicle condition is turned to the ON mode 5 seconds -
12 volt battery voltage [BATTERY] 10 V -

[ ]: HDS Parameter

Malfunction Threshold 

Either of the condition is met:

  • The F-CAN C line bus-off continues for at least 6 seconds.
  • The CAN frame transmission to the F-CAN C line does not complete within 6 seconds.

Possible Cause 

NOTE:  The causes shown may not be a complete list of all potential problems, and it is possible that there may be other causes.

  • CAN gateway F-CAN C_H line open (include connector loose connection or disconnection)
  • CAN gateway F-CAN C_L line open (include connector loose connection or disconnection)
  • CAN gateway F-CAN C_H line short to CAN gateway F-CAN C_L line
  • CAN gateway F-CAN C_H line short to ground
  • CAN gateway F-CAN C_L line short to ground
  • CAN gateway internal circuit short
  • Unit communicating on F-CAN C failure

Diagnosis Details 

Conditions for setting the DTC 

When a malfunction is detected, a DTC and the freeze data are stored in the CAN gateway memory.

Conditions for clearing the DTC 

The DTC and the freeze data can be cleared with the scan tool Clear command.
