Moonroof System Description - Control/Function

Operation (Manual) 

The moonroof is activated by pushing the switch to the first detent of the open or close position.

Operation (Auto) 

The automatic operation of moonroof is activated by pushing the switch to the second detent of the open or close position. The moonroof will automatically open or close completely even if the switch is released.

Tilt function 

Auto tilt up operation 

During moonroof auto tilt operation, the moonroof will go to full tilt up position when the moonroof tilt switch is pushed and released.

Auto tilt down operation 

The moonroof is moved to the full closing position when the close switch is pushed to second detent position, and then released.

Manual operation (only for tilt down) 

In a tilted position, the moonroof is controlled to operate the tilt down when the close switch is pushed to first detent position.

Auto Reverse Function 

When the moonroof is closing and the stopping load is greater than the prescribed value, the moonroof will move in reverse direction momentarily then stop. The control unit detects "Pinching" by checking the pulses output from the pulse sensor in the moonroof motor.

Learning Function 

The learning function allows the moonroof to open and close with auto operation. If learning is not completed, the auto operation will not function.

Keyless/Key Cylinder Operation 

By pressing and holding the UNLOCK button of the keyless transmitter or the remote a second time, the windows and moonroof open. The windows stop moving when the UNLOCK button is released.

With the key inserted in the driver's door key cylinder, turn the key a second time holding it (counterclockwise to close). The windows and moonroof stop moving when the key is released.
