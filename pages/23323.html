DTC P003A (K20C1) (2017 2018 2019)

DTC P003A:  Turbocharger Wastegate Control Actuator Position Exceeded Learning Limit

General Description 

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

The turbocharger wastegate rod connection has a variation of hardware and software tolerances for each engine. Due to the significant temperature changes of the turbocharger, the turbocharger wastegate rod connection changes in length and size. Therefore, the mechanical stop position of the turbocharger wastegate control actuator changes accordingly. To provide an optimal approach to the actual mechanical stop position, the control system learns offset values. To reduce the influence of each tolerance for the closed position detection, two kinds of learning functions; long term position learning and short term position learning are provided.

  • Long term position learning

    During the position learning of the mechanical stop position, the turbocharger wastegate actuator control system shortly drives the actuator into the closed position. The position learning takes place when both the actual and desired positions of the turbocharger wastegate control valve are below a calibrated value, and the turbocharger wastegate control actuator presses wastegate valve to the closed stop with the constant duty cycle. The long term position learning provides correction for individual variation of hardware and software tolerances of engines, and deterioration over life time. A turbocharger wastegate actuator control system learns offset values once in a driving cycle. To ensure that the position learning will always take a place under similar temperature conditions, a long engine stop phase should be hold before. The long term position learning is limited within calibrated range. If the position learning value exceeds its limitation, the powertrain control module (PCM) detects a malfunction and stores a DTC.

  • Short term position learning

    Additionally to the long term position learning, the short term position learning will be done continuously during the engine operation. The short term position learning provides correction for tolerances which has temperature dependency, changes of environment conditions, or operating condition at every moment. Therefore, the turbocharger wastegate actuator control system learns offset values permanently under certain conditions (during fuel cut-off operation, low engine speeds and loads, or in case of the large air charge reduction). The short term position learning always starts from 'zero position' adapted at long term position learning and it is limited within calibrated range.

Malfunction Threshold 

Either of the conditions occurs:

  • The turbocharger wastegate control actuator position sensor voltage at closed position at first PCM initialization is less than 0.59326 V, or greater than 2.55981 V.
  • The turbocharger wastegate control actuator position sensor voltage at closed position during the long term position learning is less than 0.59326 V, or greater than 2.55981 V.

Possible Cause 

NOTE:  The causes shown may not be a complete list of all potential problems, and it is possible that there may be other causes.

  • Turbocharger wastegate control actuator position sensor failure
  • Turbocharger wastegate control actuator stuck
  • PCM internal circuit failure

Confirmation Procedure 

Operating Condition 

  • Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.
  • Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode, and leave the vehicle for at least 6 hours.
  • Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

With the HDS 

Select the Electric Waste Gate in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.

Diagnosis Details 

Conditions for setting the DTC 

When a malfunction is detected, the MIL comes on and a Pending DTC, a Confirmed DTC, and the freeze data are stored in the PCM memory.

Conditions for clearing the DTC 

The MIL is cleared if the malfunction does not return in three consecutive trips in which the diagnostic runs. The MIL, the Pending DTC, the Confirmed DTC, and the freeze data can be cleared with the scan tool Clear command.
