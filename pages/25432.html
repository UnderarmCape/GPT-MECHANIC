Automatic Dimming Mirrors Test (2/4-door)

1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode. Measure the voltage between terminal No. 7 and body ground.

  • If there is battery voltage, go to step 3.
  • If there is no voltage, check for:
    • -

      Blown No. B9 fuse

    • -

      An open or high resistance in the wire

Measure the voltage between terminal No. 6 and body ground.

  • If there is less than 0.2 V, go to step 4.
  • If there is voltage more than 0.2 V, check for:
    • -

      An open or high resistance in the ground wire

    • -

      Poor ground (G502)

Measure the voltage between terminal No. 5 and body ground with the shift lever in R position/mode.

  • If there is battery voltage, replace the rearview mirror .
  • If there is no voltage, check for:
    • -

      An open or high resistance in the wire

    • -

      Faulty under-dash fuse/relay box

    • -

      Faulty reverse relay circuit

    • -

      Faulty transmission range switch
