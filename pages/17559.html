MTF Inspection and Replacement (Except K20C1 (M/T)): Inspection

  3. MTF - Check
    Fig 1: Oil Check Bolt And Sealing Washer With Torque Specifications (Except K20C1 - M/T)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    1. Remove the oil check bolt (A) and the 6 mm sealing washer (B).

    2. Check the condition of the MTF and make sure it is at the proper level (C).

    NOTE:  If the MTF is dirty, replace the MTF.

    3. Install the oil check bolt with a new 6 mm sealing washer.
