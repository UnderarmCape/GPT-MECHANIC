Fuel Learn Procedure (K20C1) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Procedure

NOTE:  Do this procedure whenever you do any of these actions:

  • Replace the injector.
  • Replace the A/F sensor (Sensor 1).
  • Replace MAF sensor/IAT sensor 1.
  • Update the PCM.
  • Reset the PCM.
  1. HDS - Connect
  2. PCM - Reset
  3. PCM - Fuel Learn
    1. Start the engine. Keep the idling for 220 seconds.

    2. Keep the idling under the following conditions for 15 seconds:

    • Engine coolant temperature (ECT SENSOR 1) is 140 deg.F (60 deg.C) or more.
    • Intake air temperature (IAT SENSOR 1) is less than 176 deg.F (80 deg.C).

    3. Check the FUEL LEARNING in the DATA LIST with the HDS.

    • COMPLETE: This procedure is complete.
    • NOT COMPLETE: Do this procedure again.

    4. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode, then wait for 60 seconds.
