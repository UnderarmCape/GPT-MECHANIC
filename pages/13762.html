Rear Brake Pad Removal and Installation (2020 2021): Removal

NOTE: 

  • Review the Service Precautions before doing repairs or service .
  • Type-R: Do not remove the brake pads unless you are replacing it. If the brake pads are removed, the adhesive tape on the outer pad must be replaced. Refer to the installation service information for details.
  1. Brake Fluid - Remove
    1. Remove some brake fluid from the master cylinder.
  2. Vehicle - Lift
  3. Rear Wheels - Remove
  4. Brake Caliper Piston - Enter the MAINTENANCE MODE
    NOTE:  When not using the HDS, removing the electric parking brake actuator, and using a TORX socket, turn the spindle clockwise until it comes to a lock .

    1. Connect the HDS to the data link connector (DLC)

    2. Select ADJUSTMENT from ABS/TCS/VSA menu with the HDS, then enter ENTER BRAKE PAD MAINTENANCE MODE from BRAKE PAD MAINTENANCE MODE, and follow the screen prompts. Make sure TRANSITION TO PAD EXCHANGE MODE HAS BEEN COMPLETED appears on the screen.

    3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.
