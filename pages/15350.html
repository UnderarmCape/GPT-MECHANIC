Shift Arm Clearance Inspection (K20C1 (M/T)) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Shift Arm Clearance - Inspect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Measure the clearance between the shift arm (A) and the shift piece (B) with a feeler gauge (C). If the clearance exceeds the service limit, go to step 2.
    Standard: 
      0.65-0.95 mm (0.026-0.037 in) 
    Service Limit: 
      0.95 mm (0.037 in) 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Measure the width of the shift arm.
    • If the width is not within the standard, replace the shift arm.
    • If the width is within the standard, replace the shift piece.
    Standard: 
      13.2-13.4 mm (0.520-0.528 in)
