DTC P0461 (K20C1) (17-19)

DTC P0461  : Fuel Level Sensor (Fuel Gauge Sending Unit) Circuit Range/Performance Problem

NOTE:  Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
P0461 Fuel Level Sensor (Fuel Gauge Sending Unit) Circuit Range/Performance Problem    

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Fuel gauge sending unit performance check

    -1. Test the fuel gauge sending unit .

    Is the fuel gauge sending unit OK?

    YES 

    Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the fuel gauge sending unit and the gauge control module. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

    NO 

    Replace the fuel gauge sending unit .
