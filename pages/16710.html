DTC U3003-16 (Si (Adaptive Damper Control Unit)) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021)

DTC U3003-16  : Adaptive Damper Control Unit +B Voltage Low Failure

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • Check for PGM-FI DTCs with the HDS, and troubleshoot the PGM-FI DTCs first .
DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
U3003-16 Adaptive Damper Control Unit +B Voltage Low Failure    

DTC (Adaptive Damper System)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    Clear DTCs

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode, then to the ON mode.

    -4. Wait for 60 seconds or more.

    -5. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
    U3003-16 Adaptive Damper Control Unit +B Voltage Low Failure    

    Is DTC U3003-16 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting . If the freeze data/on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the freeze data/on-board snapshot.

  2. Fuse check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Check the following fuse.

    Fuse No. A33 (15 A) *1
    No. A31 (15 A) *2
    Location Under-hood fuse/relay box

    *1: Si

    *2: Type-R

    Is the fuse OK?

    YES 

    The fuse is OK. Reinstall the fuse, then go to step 3.

    NO 

    Replace the fuse. If the fuse blows again, repair a short to ground in the No. A33 (15 A) fuse circuit (Si) or the No. A31 (15 A) fuse circuit (Type-R)

  3. Open wire check (+B ADS line)

    -1. Disconnect the following connector.

    Adaptive damper control unit 24P connector

    -2. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Adaptive damper control unit 24P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Adaptive damper control unit 24P connector No. 8
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    The +B ADS wire is OK. Check for loose terminals and poor connections in the adaptive damper control unit 24P connector. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting. If they are OK, replace the adaptive damper control unit .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the wire between the adaptive damper control unit and the No. A33 (15 A) fuse (Si) or No. A31 (15 A) fuse (Type-R) in the under-hood fuse/relay box.
