DTC P2119 (K20C2) (18-21)

DTC P2119  : Throttle Actuator Control Throttle Body Range/Performance

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • If DTC P2119 is indicated alone, do the troubleshooting for DTC P1683 and P1684 using the on-board snapshot data for P2119.
  • If any of the DTCs listed below are indicated at the same time as DTC P2119, troubleshoot those DTCs first, then recheck for P2119.
