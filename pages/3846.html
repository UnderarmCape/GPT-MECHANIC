VSA System Symptom Troubleshooting - VSA cannot be turned OFF

  1. VSA OFF switch check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Check the VSA OFF switch .

    Is the VSA OFF switch OK?

    YES 

    Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Replace the VSA OFF switch .

  2. Determine possible failure area (VSA OFF SW line, GND line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
    VSA OFF switch 4P connector * 1: disconnected
    VSA OFF switch 5P connector * 2: disconnected
      VSA OFF switch 8P connector *3: disconnected
    Test point 1 VSA OFF switch 4P connector (female terminals) No. 4 *1:
    VSA OFF switch 5P connector (female terminals) No. 1 *2 :
      VSA OFF switch 8P connector (female terminals) No. 8 *3 :
    Test point 2 Body ground

    *1: VSA OFF switch 4P connector type

    *2: VSA OFF switch 5P connector type

    *3: VSA OFF switch 8P connector type

    VSA OFF switch 4P connector type 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    VSA OFF switch 5P connector type 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    VSA OFF switch 8P connector type 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    Repair an open in the wire between the VSA OFF switch and body ground (G501).

    NO 

    Go to step 3.

  3. Shorted wire check (VSA OFF SW line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    Gauge control module A (32P) connector

    -3. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    VSA OFF switch 4P connector * 1: disconnected
    VSA OFF switch 5P connector * 2: disconnected
    VSA OFF switch 8P connector *3: disconnected
    Gauge control module A (32P) connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 VSA OFF switch 4P connector (female terminals) No. 4 *1:
    VSA OFF switch 5P connector (female terminals) No. 1 *2 :
      VSA OFF switch 8P connector (female terminals) No. 8 *3 :
    Test point 2 Body ground

    *1: VSA OFF switch 4P connector type

    *2: VSA OFF switch 5P connector type

    *3: VSA OFF switch 8P connector type

    VSA OFF switch 4P connector type 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    VSA OFF switch 5P connector type 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    VSA OFF switch 8P connector type 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there 1 MΩ or more ?

    YES 

    The VSA OFF SW wire is not shorted. Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Repair a short to body ground in the wire between the gauge control module and the VSA OFF switch.

  4. Open wire check (VSA OFF SW line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    VSA OFF switch 4P connector * 1: disconnected
    VSA OFF switch 5P connector * 2: disconnected
    VSA OFF switch 8P connector *3: disconnected
    Gauge control module A (32P) connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 VSA OFF switch 4P connector (female terminals) No. 4 *1:
    VSA OFF switch 5P connector (female terminals) No. 1 *2 :
      VSA OFF switch 8P connector (female terminals) No. 8 *3 :
    Test point 2 Gauge control module connector A (32P) No. 9

    *1: VSA OFF switch 4P connector type

    *2: VSA OFF switch 5P connector type

    *3: VSA OFF switch 8P connector type

    Is there 1.0 Ω or less Ω

    YES 

    Replace the gauge control module .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the wire between the gauge control module and the VSA OFF switch.
