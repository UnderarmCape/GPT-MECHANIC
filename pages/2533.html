Seat Heaters System Description - Components

Seat Heater Switches 

The front seat heater switches are installed in the climate control panel, and the rear seat heater switches are installed the rear of the center console.

Thermostat and Breaker (Rear Seat) 

The seat cushion heater has a thermostat and a breaker. The thermostat and the breaker protect the seat heater circuit from overheat.
