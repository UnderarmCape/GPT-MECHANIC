CVT SETS DTC P2817

CVT SETS DTC P2817

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 19-047, Date of Issue:  July 18, 2019
HONDA: 2017-18 CR-V; 2016-18 Civic (Includes 5-Door); 2018-2019 Accord
Superceded Bulletin(s): 19-047, Date of Issue:  April 25, 2019

REVISION SUMMARY

Under AFFECTED VEHICLES, information was updated.

BACKGROUND

The shift position indicator ("D" light) starts flashing due to DTC P2817, (Shift solenoid valve O/P [pressure control solenoid H] stuck off) being set.

When the TCM checks the drive pulley oil pressure, it misreads the pressure as high even though oil pressure is within specification.

CORRECTIVE ACTION

Update the PGM-FI and TCM software.

The normal warranty applies.

SOFTWARE INFORMATION

NOTE: Unnecessary or incorrect repairs resulting from a failure to update the i-HDS are not covered under warranty.

i-HDS Software Version: 1.004.064 or later 

J2534 Software Information:

  • PC Application Version 1.1.0.6 or later 
  • Database update 06-APR-2019 or later 

Before beginning the repair, make sure that both the i-HDS and J2534 software are updated as listed above. 

Do only the update listed in this service bulletin. 

You cannot apply the updates with the MVCI as a standalone tool.  To update the software you must use the MVCI or the DST-i interface in conjunction with the J2534 Rewrite PC application on the i-HDS.

For more information about updating the i-HDS, the MVCI, and vehicle systems, refer to service bulletin 01-023, Updating Control Units/Modules.

REPAIR PROCEDURE

Update the PGM-FI and TCM software using the i-HDS J2534 rewrite software with the MVCI or DST-i. Refer to Service Bulletin 01-023, Updating Control Units/Modules. 

NOTE:
  • Connect a fully charged jumper battery, and leave it connected during the entire procedure to maintain steady voltage.
  • Do not use the MongoosePro VCI tool.
NOTE:
  • Never turn the ignition to OFF or ACCESSORY during the update. If there is a problem with the update, leave the ignition turned to ON.
  • To prevent control unit damage, do not operate anything electrical (headlights, audio system, brakes, A/C power windows, door locks, etc.) during the update.
