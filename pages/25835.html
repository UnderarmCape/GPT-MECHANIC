DTC U3000-51 (2/4-door: Multipurpose Camera Unit) (2016 2017 2018)

DTC U3000-51  : Multipurpose Camera Unit Update Incomplete

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • This DTC is indicated when the multipurpose camera unit update is not completed properly.
  • Do not turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode while updating the multipurpose camera unit. If you turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode before completion, the multipurpose camera unit will be damaged.
DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
U3000-51 Multipurpose Camera Unit Update Incomplete    

DTC (IDAS)

  1. Multipurpose camera unit check (update)

    -1. Update the multipurpose camera unit .

    NOTE:  This DTC is erased and no history is recorded if update is successfully completed.

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, then wait for at least 2 seconds.

    -4. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
    U3000-51 Multipurpose Camera Unit Update Incomplete    

    Is DTC U3000-51 indicated?

    YES 

    Check for loose terminals in the multipurpose camera unit 20P connector. If the multipurpose camera unit was updated and the symptom/indication is still present, replace the multipurpose camera unit .

    NO 

    Troubleshooting is complete. If any other DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.
