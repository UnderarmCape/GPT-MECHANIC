DTC B2649

DTC B2649  : Rear Sensor Signal Short to Ground

NOTE: 

  • If you are troubleshooting multiple DTCs, be sure to follow the instructions in B-CAN System Diagnosis Test Mode A .
  • The connection order of the sensor is 1st -> 2nd -> 3rd -> 4th from the side near the control unit.
DTC Description DTC
B2649 Rear Sensor Signal Short to Ground  

DTC (Parking and Back-Up Sensor Control Unit)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode and then the ON mode.

    -3. Wait for at least 6 seconds.

    -4. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
    B2649 Rear Sensor Signal Short to Ground  

    Is DTC B2649 indicated?

    YES 

    Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections.

  2. Shorted wire check (SOR SIDE L, E2 SIDE L lines) 1

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    Parking and back-up sensor control unit 16P connector

    -3. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    Parking and back-up sensor control unit 16P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Parking and back-up sensor control unit 16P connector No. 14
    Test point 2 Parking and back-up sensor control unit 16P connector No. 12

    Is there 1 MΩ or more?

    YES 

    Go to step 10.

    NO 

    Go to step 3.

  3. Shorted wire check (SOR SIDE R, E2 SIDE R lines) 1

    -1. Disconnect the following connector.

    Right rear center sensor 6P connector

    -2. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    Parking and back-up sensor control unit 16P connector: disconnected
    Right rear center sensor 6P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Right rear center sensor 6P connector No. 3
    Test point 2 Right rear center sensor 6P connector No. 1
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there 1 MΩ or more?

    YES 

    Go to step 5.

    NO 

    Go to step 4.

  4. Shorted wire check (SOR SIDE R, E2 SIDE R lines) 2

    -1. Disconnect the following connector.

    Right rear corner sensor 6P connector

    -2. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    Parking and back-up sensor control unit 16P connector: disconnected

    Right rear corner sensor 6P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Right rear center sensor 6P connector No. 3
    Test point 2 Right rear center sensor 6P connector No. 1
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there 1 MΩ or more?

    YES 

    Replace the right rear corner sensor .

    NO 

    Repair a short in the SOR SIDE R and E2 SIDE R wires between the right rear center sensor and the right rear corner sensor.
