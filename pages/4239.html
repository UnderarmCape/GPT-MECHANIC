Brake Fluid Level Switch Test: Test

NOTE: 

  • If both the ABS/VSA indicator and the brake system indicator (red) come on at the same time, check the VSA system for DTCs first .
  • Unless otherwise indicated, illustrations used in the procedure are for M/T.
  1. Brake Fluid Level Switch - Test
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Disconnect the connector (A).

    2. Check for continuity between the terminals (1) and (2) with the float in the down position and in the up position.

    • Remove the brake fluid completely from the reservoir. With the float down, there should be continuity.
    • Fill the reservoir with brake fluid to the MAX (upper) level (B). With the float up, there should be no continuity.

    NOTE:  If the brake fluid level switch is OK, but the brake system indicator (red) does not function, do the gauge control module self-diagnostic function .

    3. Connect the connector.
