Front Door Panel 

  1. Power Window Switch Panel - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Pry out the front edge of the power window switch panel (A) with the trim tool (B), then remove the power window switch panel.

    2. Disconnect the connectors (C).
  2. Front Door Panel - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the screw (A) and the clips (B, C).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. While holding the front door panel (A) away from the door, disconnect the connector (B), then remove the front door panel.
  3. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
