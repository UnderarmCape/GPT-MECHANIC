Fuel Tank Unit Removal and Installation (Except L15BY): Installation

WARNING: 

Flammable - Keep Fire Away

NOTE:  Refer to the Fuel and Emissions Systems Service Precautions before disconnecting/connecting the fuel line.

  1. Fuel Tank Unit - Install (USA/Canada/California Models)

    1. Install a new O-ring (A).

    NOTE: 

    • Be careful not to damage the new O-ring.
    • Do not coat the O-ring with oil.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Grab the plug lanyard (B) and pull the inner fuel vent tube (C) up until it is close to the fuel tank unit opening.

    3. Move the fuel tank unit (D) aside, allowing space to remove the ORVR service plug (E) from the inner fuel vent tube.

    NOTE:  Do not allow the inner fuel vent tube to fall inside the fuel tank. If fuel enters the inner fuel vent tube, a DTC will set and the inner fuel vent tube will need to be replaced.

    4. Hold the plugged inner fuel vent tube while reinstalling the fuel tank unit.

    5. Remove the ORVR service plug (A) away from the tank opening and connect the inner fuel vent tube (B) to the fuel tank unit (C).

    NOTE:  Be careful not to bend or twist the fuel level sensor arm (D) excessively.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    6. Remove the ORVR service plug from the child safety seat hook (E) and install the provided protective cap (F).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 7. Align the tab (A) of the fuel tank unit as shown, and temporarily insert the new fuel tank unit cam lock ring (B).
  2. Fuel Tank Unit - Install (Mexico Models)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 NOTE:  Be careful not to bend or twist the fuel level sensor arm (A) excessively.

    1. Insert the fuel tank unit (B) partially into the fuel tank with a new O-ring (C).

    NOTE: 

    • Be careful not to damage the new O-ring.
    • Do not coat the O-ring with oil.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Align the tab (A) of the fuel tank unit as shown, and temporarily insert the new fuel tank unit cam lock ring (B).
  3. Fuel Vent Tube and Fuel Tank Vapor Recirculation Tube - Connect (USA/Canada/California Models)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Connect the fuel vent tube (A) and fuel tank vapor recirculation tube (B) by rotating the fuel tank unit (C) counterclockwise slightly.
    NOTE:  Do not bend the fuel vent tube and fuel tank vapor recirculation tube excessively.
  4. Fuel Tank Unit Cam Lock Ring - Lock
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Rotate the fuel tank unit cam lock ring (A) and slide the fuel tank unit cam lock tabs (B) into the positioning edge (C) slightly as shown by hand.
    NOTE:  Confirm the fuel cam lock ring is not floating.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Using the fuel sender wrench (A), turn the fuel tank unit cam lock ring (B) clockwise.
    NOTE: 
    • Try to keep the fuel sender wrench from floating.
    • Securely set the positioning edge (C) of the fuel tank at the fuel tank unit cam lock ring tabs (D) on the fuel tank unit cam lock ring as shown.
  5. Fuel Leak - Check
    USA/Canada/California Models 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Mexico Models 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Connect the fuel line quick-connect fitting (A)

    2. Connect the connector (B).

    3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, but do not start the engine. After the fuel pump runs for about 2 seconds, the fuel line will be pressurized. Repeat this two or three times, then check for fuel leakage.

    4. USA/Canada/California Models: If equipped, install the fuel connector cover (C).

    5. Install the access panel (D) to the floor.
