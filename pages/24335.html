Fuel Tank Unit Removal and Installation (L15BY) (2018 2019 2020): Installation

WARNING: 

Flammable - Keep Fire Away

NOTE:  Refer to the Fuel and Emissions Systems Service Precautions before disconnecting the fuel line.

  1. Fuel Tank Unit - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 NOTE:  Be careful not to bend or twist the fuel level sensor arm (A) excessively.

    1. Insert the fuel tank unit (B) partially into the fuel tank with a new O-ring (C).

    NOTE: 

    • Be careful not to damage the new O-ring.
    • Do not coat the O-ring with oil.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Align the tab (A) of the fuel tank unit with the mark (B) on the fuel tank as shown, and temporarily insert the new fuel tank unit cam lock ring (C).
  2. Fuel Tank Unit Cam Lock Ring - Lock
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Rotate the fuel tank unit cam lock ring (A) and slide the fuel tank unit cam lock tabs (B) into the positioning edge (C) slightly as shown by hand.
    NOTE:  Confirm the fuel cam lock ring is not floating.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Using the fuel sender wrench, turn the fuel tank unit cam lock ring (A) clockwise.
    NOTE: 
    • Try to keep the fuel sender wrench from floating.
    • Securely set the positioning edge (B) of the fuel tank at the fuel tank unit cam lock ring tabs (C) on the fuel tank unit cam lock ring as shown.
  3. Fuel Leak - Check
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Connect the fuel line quick-connect fitting (A)

    2. Connect the connector (B).

    3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, but do not start the engine. After the fuel pump runs for about 2 seconds, the fuel line will be pressurized. Repeat this two or three times, then check for fuel leakage.

    4. Install the access panel (C) to the floor.
