Calibration - Models With TPMS Switch

  1. Make sure the tires are inflated to the specified tire pressure listed on the doorjamb label. Vehicle must be stopped with the transmission in neutral (M/T) or P or N (A/T, CVT).
  2. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II), or press the engine start/stop button to select the ON mode.
  3. Press the TPMS switch (lower dash, left of steering wheel) for 3 seconds.
  4. When the calibration successfully begins, the TPMS indicator (instrument cluster, center left) blinks twice.
  5. Calibration is completed after driving 31 to 62 mph (50 to 100 km/h), steadily without much acceleration or deceleration for about 30 minutes.
