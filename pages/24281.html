Fuel Supply System Description - Fuel Tank

When the fuel tank is full, there is a fuel tank feature which is the fuel level is higher than the lower end of the opening for the fuel tank unit at the vehicle horizontal state. However, even if the same amount of the fuel, this fuel tank has a structure which is the fuel level is lower than the lower end of the opening at the vehicle inclination state as shown.
