AFFECTED VEHICLES

American Honda sells several types of refrigerant oil for A/C systems. Using the wrong refrigerant oil can cause compressor damage. This guide shows you which refrigerant oil you need for servicing A/C systems in 1994 and later Honda cars and trucks.
