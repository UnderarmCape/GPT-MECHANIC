Cable Reel Removal and Installation: Installation

SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations and the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.

NOTE: 

  • Before installing the steering wheel, make sure the front wheels are aligned straight ahead.
  • The new cable reel is locked by a yellow tab for center adjustment. When installing a new cable reel there is no need to rotate the cable. After the tab is removed, discard it after installing the cable reel.
  • If the driver's airbag is deployed, cable reel cannot be reused because of thermal damage.
