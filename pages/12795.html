Manual Transmission Removal and Installation (Except K20C1 (M/T)): Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

NOTE: 

  • Engine support hanger AAR-T1256 must be used with the side engine mount installed.
  • Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  1. Vehicle - Lift Set
  2. Front Grille Cover - Remove
  3. Air Cleaner - Remove
  4. 12 Volt Battery - Remove
  5. 12 Volt Battery Base - Remove
  6. Intake Air Duct E - Remove (1.5L Engine)
  7. Intake Air Duct F - Remove (1.5L Engine)
  8. Slave Cylinder - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the clutch line brackets (A).

    2. Remove the slave cylinder (B).

    3. Carefully move the slave cylinder out of the way to avoid bending the clutch line.

    NOTE: 

    • Do not press the clutch pedal after the slave cylinder has been moved.
    • Do not disconnect the clutch line joints.
  9. Intake Air Duct - Remove
  10. EVAP Canister Purge Valve - Remove (2.0L Engine)
  11. Engine Wire Harness (Transmission Side) - Remove

    1. Disconnect the connectors (A).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Remove the connector (B) from the bracket.

    3. Remove the brackets (C).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Disconnect the connector (A).

    5. Remove the harness bracket (B).
  12. Engine Wire Harness (Engine Side) - Remove (1.5L Engine)

    1. Disconnect the connectors (A).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Remove the ground cables (B).

    3. Remove the harness covers (C) and the harness brackets (D), then swing the engine wire harness (E) out of the way.

  13. Engine Wire Harness (Engine Side) - Remove (2.0L Engine)

    1. Disconnect the connectors (A).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Remove the ground cable (B).

    3. Remove the harness clamps (C), then swing the engine wire harness (D) out of the way.

  14. Change Wire Bracket - Remove

    1. Remove the lock pins (A).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Carefully remove the change wire and the change wire bracket (B) together to avoid bending the change wire.

    NOTE:  Do not wipe off the grease of the change wire ends (C).

  15. Intake Manifold Bracket - Remove (1.5L Engine)
  16. Engine Support Hanger - Install

    1. Remove the front damper caps.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Install the universal lifting eyelet with an about 50 mm (2.00 in) commercially available spacer (A).

    3. Install the engine support hanger onto the vehicle as shown.

    4. Attach the hook (B) to the slotted hole in the universal lifting eyelet.

    5. Tighten the wing nut (C) by hand, and lift and support the engine/transmission.

  40. Intermediate Shaft - Remove (With Intermediate Shaft)
  41. Lower Transmission Assembly Mounting Bolt - Remove

    NOTE:  Refer to the Exploded View as needed during this procedure.

  42. Transmission - Remove
    1. Pull the transmission away from the engine until the transmission mainshaft clears the clutch pressure plate.

    2. Slowly lower the transmission about 150 mm (5.91 in).

    3. Check once again that all hoses and harness are disconnected and free from the transmission.

    4. Lower the transmission.
