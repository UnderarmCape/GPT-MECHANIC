Fuel Tank Unit Removal and Installation (Except L15BY): Removal

WARNING: 

Flammable - Keep Fire Away

NOTE:  Refer to the Fuel and Emissions Systems Service Precautions before disconnecting/connecting the fuel line.

  1. Fuel Pressure - Relieve
  2. Vehicle - Lift (Front Side)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 NOTICE: 

    If the front of the vehicle is not raised with the fuel level more than 90%, fuel in the fuel tank will spill .

    1. When the fuel indicator points at F segment or more, or you cannot confirm the fuel level, apply the parking brake, block the rear wheels, use the jack to raise the front of the vehicle more than 345 mm (13.58 in) height (A) as shown, then make sure it is securely supported.
  3. Rear Seat-Back and Rear Seat-Cushion - Remove
  4. Access Panel - Remove
    USA/Canada/California Models 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Mexico Models 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the access panel (A) from the floor.

    2. Disconnect the connector (B).
  5. Fuel Line Quick-Connect Fitting - Disconnect
    USA/Canada/California Models 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Mexico Models 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. USA/Canada/California Models: If equipped, remove the fuel connector cover (A).

    2. Disconnect the quick-connect fitting

    (B) from the fuel tank unit.
  6. Fuel Tank Unit Cam Lock Ring - Unlock
    USA/Canada/California Models 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Mexico Models 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Set the fuel sender wrench (A) as shown.

    2. Turn the fuel tank unit cam lock ring (B) counterclockwise.

    NOTE:  Try to keep the fuel sender wrench from floating.
  7. Fuel Vent Tube and Fuel Tank Vapor Recirculation Tube - Disconnect (USA/Canada/California Models)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 NOTE:  Do not bend the fuel vent tube (A) and fuel tank vapor recirculation tube (B) excessively.

    1. Disconnect the fuel vent tube by doing the following procedure.

    • Hold the fuel vent tube with one hand, and squeeze the two retainer tabs (C) with the other hand to release them from the locking tabs.

    2. Disconnect the fuel tank vapor recirculation tube by doing the following procedure.

    • Push in the fuel tank vapor recirculation tube towards the fuel tank unit, push down the retainer tab (D), then disconnect it.

    3. Pull the tubes off by slightly rotating the fuel tank unit (E) clockwise.
  8. Fuel Tank Unit Cam Lock Ring - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  9. Fuel Tank Unit - Remove (USA/Canada/California Models)

    1. Hook the plug's lanyard (A) on the child safety seat hook (B) as shown. Set the ORVR service plug (C) near the fuel tank unit opening.

    NOTE:  Do not damage any part of the ORVR service plug's surface. Damage to the ORVR service plug may cause fuel leaks into the inner fuel vent tube (D). Use the provided protective cap (E) when the ORVR service plug is not in use. If there is damage, discard the ORVR service plug and order a new one.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Disconnect the inner fuel vent tube (A) from the fuel tank unit (B) and hold it. Do not allow the inner fuel vent tube to drop into the fuel tank.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    3. Install the ORVR service plug (C) onto the inner fuel vent tube. Make sure the ORVR service plug is fully seated by holding the inner fuel vent tube and gently pulling on the plug's lanyard.

    NOTE:  The ORVR service plug should not come off.

    4. Carefully lower the inner fuel vent tube (A) into the fuel tank (B), making sure the plug lanyard (C) is free and away from the fuel tank unit (D).

    NOTE:  It is OK for the tube to be submerged in the fuel.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    5. Remove the fuel tank unit with the O-ring (E).

    NOTE:  Be careful not to bend or twist the fuel level sensor arm (F) excessively.

  10. Fuel Tank Unit - Remove (Mexico Models)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the fuel tank unit (A) with the O-ring (B).
    NOTE:  Be careful not to bend or twist the fuel level sensor arm (C) excessively.
