    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Attach a wrench to the drive belt auto-tensioner (A) and move it to the direction arrow shown. With the drive belt auto-tensioner compressed, secure the drive belt auto-tensioner by aligning the water passage hole with the drive belt auto-tensioner hole and inserting a 6 x 45 mm pin (B) into the hole.
    NOTE:  This is a hydraulic type auto-tensioner, so you must turn the wrench slowly for at least 3 seconds.
