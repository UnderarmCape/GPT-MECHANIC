DTC P2119 (K20C1) (17-21)

DTC P2119  : Throttle Actuator Range

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • If some of the DTCs listed below are stored at the same time as DTC P2119, troubleshoot them first, then recheck for DTC P2119.

    P0121, P0122, P0123, P0221, P0222, P0223: Throttle position (TP) sensor

    P060C, P060D: PCM internal control module

    P2101, P2176: Electronic throttle control system (ETCS)

DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
P2119 Throttle Actuator Range    

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    Clear DTC

    -3. Check for Pending or Confirmed DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
    P2119 Throttle Actuator Range    

    Is DTC P2119 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Replace the throttle body .

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the throttle body and the PCM, then clean the throttle body . If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .
