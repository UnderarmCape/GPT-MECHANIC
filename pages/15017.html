DTC P0138 (L15B7/L15BA/L15BY) (20-21)

DTC P0138  : Secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) Circuit High Voltage

NOTE:  Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
P0138 Secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) Circuit High Voltage    

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Start the engine, and let it idle without load (CVT in P or N, M/T in neutral) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.

    -4. Check for Pending or Confirmed DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
    P0138 Secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) Circuit High Voltage    

    Is DTC P0138 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) and the PCM. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

  2. Determine possible failure area (short to power in SO2SG/SO2 SG line, others)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    Secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) 4P connector

    -3. Connect terminals A and B with a jumper wire.

    Terminal A Secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) 4P connector (male terminals) No. 1:
    Terminal B Secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) 4P connector (male terminals) No. 2:
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -5. Check the parameter (s) below with the HDS.

    Signal Threshold Current conditions
    Values Unit Values Unit
    HO2S S2 More than 3.28 V    

    Do the current condition (s) match the threshold?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Go to step 5.

  3. Open wire check (SO2SG/SO2 SG line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Remove the jumper wire from the secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) 4P connector.

    -3. Connect terminals A and B with a jumper wire.

    Terminal A Secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) 4P connector (male terminals) No. 1:
    Terminal B Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -5. Check the parameter (s) below with the HDS.

    Signal Threshold Current conditions
    Values Unit Values Unit
    HO2S S2 More than 3.28 V    

    Do the current condition (s) match the threshold?

    YES 

    The SO2SG/SO2 SG wire is not open. Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the SO2SG/SO2 SG wire between the PCM (A48) and secondary HO2S (Sensor 2).

  4. Open wire check (SO2 line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector A (50P)

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) 4P connector: disconnected
      Secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) 4P connector No. 1: jumped to body ground
      PCM connector A (50P): disconnected
    Test point 1 PCM connector A (50P) No. 47
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there 1.0 Ω or less?

    YES 

    The SO2 wire is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P0138 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the SO2 wire between the PCM (A47) and secondary HO2S (Sensor 2).

  5. Shorted wire check (SO2SG/SO2 SG line to power)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Remove the jumper wire from the secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) 4P connector.

    -3. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -4. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector A (50P)

    -5. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -6. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      Secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) 4P connector: disconnected
      PCM connector A (50P): disconnected
    Test point 1 PCM connector A (50P) No. 48
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there about 5 V or more?

    YES 

    Repair a short to power in the SO2SG/SO2 SG wire between the PCM (A48) and secondary HO2S (Sensor 2).

    NO 

    The SO2SG/SO2 SG wire is OK. Replace secondary HO2S (Sensor 2) .
