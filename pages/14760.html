DTC P0335 (K20C1) (17-21)

DTC P0335  : CKP Sensor No Signal

NOTE:  Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
P0335 CKP Sensor No Signal    

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Start the engine.

    -4. Check for Pending or Confirmed DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
    P0335 CKP Sensor No Signal    

    Is DTC P0335 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the CKP sensor and the PCM. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

  2. Determine possible failure area (VCC1 CRK line, others)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    CKP sensor 3P connector

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      CKP sensor 3P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 CKP sensor 3P connector (female terminals) No. 1:
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there about 5.0 V?

    YES 

    The VCC1 CRK wire is OK. Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Go to step 3.

  3. Open wire check (VCC1 CRK line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector No. 1 (96P)

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      CKP sensor 3P connector: disconnected
      PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    Test point 1 CKP sensor 3P connector (female terminals) No. 1:
    Test point 2 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 80
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The VCC1 CRK wire is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P0335 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the VCC1 CRK wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 80 and the CKP sensor.

  4. Determine possible failure area (CRK line, others)

    -1. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      CKP sensor 3P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 CKP sensor 3P connector (female terminals) No. 2:
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there about 5.0 V?

    YES 

    Go to step 5.

    NO 

    Go to step 7.

  5. Determine possible failure area (CKP sensor, others)

    -1. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      CKP sensor 3P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 CKP sensor 3P connector (female terminals) No. 1:
    Test point 2 CKP sensor 3P connector (female terminals) No. 3:
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there about 5.0 V?

    YES 

    The SG CRK wire is OK. Replace the CKP sensor .

    NO 

    Go to step 6.

  6. Open wire check (SG CRK line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector No. 1 (96P)

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      CKP sensor 3P connector: disconnected
      PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    Test point 1 CKP sensor 3P connector (female terminals) No. 3:
    Test point 2 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 76
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The SG CRK wire is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P0335 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the SG CRK wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 76 and the CKP sensor.

  7. Shorted wire check (CRK line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector No. 1 (96P)

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      CKP sensor 3P connector: disconnected
      PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    Test point 1 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 75
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Repair a short in the CRK wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 75 and the CKP sensor.

    NO 

    The CRK wire is not shorted. Go to step 8.

  8. Open wire check (CRK line)

    -1. Connect terminals A and B with a jumper wire.

    Terminal A CKP sensor 3P connector (female terminals) No. 2:
    Terminal B Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    -2. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      CKP sensor 3P connector: disconnected
      CKP sensor 3P connector No. 2: jumped to body ground
      PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    Test point 1 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 75
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The CRK wire is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P0335 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the CRK wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 75 and the CKP sensor.
