SYMPTOM

Fuel leaks or overflows from the filler neck while refueling.

PROBABLE CAUSE

The auto shut-off feature at the gas pump may be delayed and cause fuel to leak at the filler neck. The capless fuel shutter may also allow excess fuel to leak at the filler neck.

CORRECTIVE ACTION

Replace the capless fuel shutter set. Install the fuel filler lid cover and apply the fuel lid label (if applicable). Refer the customer to page 112 of the Owner's Guide for refueling precautions.

The normal warranty applies.

REPAIR PROCEDURE

  1. Remove the left rear inner fender as needed.
  2. Remove the fuel fill door lock actuator.
  3. Remove the fuel fill door adapter.
  4. Remove the mis-fuel inhibit device by breaking the 6 lock tabs.
  5. Pry up the lock tab and remove the mis-fuel inhibit device.
  6. Install the new mis-fuel inhibit device.
  7. Re-install all removed parts.

Civic 4-door 

  1. On Si models only  : Apply the fuel lid label to the new fuel lid cover as shown.
    NOTE: Make sure the new fuel lid cover is clean and grease-free, especially on the surface where the label will be applied.
  2. Align the hooks on the top edge of the fuel lid cover to slots on the fuel door as shown.
  3. Press the bottom of the fuel lid cover against the fuel door until both locking tabs click. This indicates the fuel lid cover is securely and correctly installed.
  4. Make sure the fuel door closes and latches without contact or interference from the mis-fuel inhibit device and surrounding area. The gap between the door and the surrounding quarter panel should remain consistent.
  5. Make sure the fuel door release works correctly when using the driver's door switch.

Civic 2-door 

  1. On Si models only  : Apply the fuel lid label to the new fuel lid cover as shown.
    NOTE: Make sure the new fuel lid cover is clean and grease-free, especially on the surface where the label will be applied.
  2. Align the hook on the right side edge of the fuel lid cover to the fuel door as shown.
  3. Press the left of the fuel lid cover against the fuel door until both locking tabs click. This indicates the fuel lid cover is securely and correctly installed.
  4. Make sure the fuel door closes and latches without contact or interference from the mis-fuel inhibit device and surrounding area. The gap between the door and the surrounding quarter panel should remain consistent.
  5. Make sure the fuel door release works correctly when using the driver's door switch.

Civic 5-door 

  1. On Sport, Sport Touring, or Type R models only  : Apply the fuel lid label to the new fuel lid cover as shown.
    NOTE: Make sure the new fuel lid cover is clean and grease-free, especially on the surface where the label will be applied.
  2. Align the clips on the back of the fuel lid cover to the holes in the fuel door.
  3. Press the left of the fuel lid cover against the fuel door until both clips click. This indicates the fuel lid cover is securely and correctly installed.
  4. Make sure the fuel door closes and latches without contact or interference from the mis-fuel inhibit device and surrounding area. The gap between the door and the surrounding quarter panel should remain consistent.
  5. Make sure the fuel door release works correctly when using the driver's door switch.
