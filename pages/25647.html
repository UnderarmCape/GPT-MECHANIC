    		 1. Test the washer motor (A) by connecting 12 volt battery power to terminal No. 1 and ground to terminal No. 2 of the washer motor. The motor should run.
    • If the washer motor does not run or fails to run smoothly, replace the windshield washer motor.
    • If the washer motor runs smoothly, but little or no washer fluid is pumped, check for a disconnected or blocked washer hose, or a clogged washer motor outlet.
