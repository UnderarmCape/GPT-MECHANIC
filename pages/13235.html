    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Disconnect the connector (A).

    2. Check for continuity between driver's power window motor connector terminal No. 4 and body ground. There should be continuity.

    • If there is continuity, connect the motor connector, then go to step 3.
    • If there is no continuity, check for an open or high resistance in the BLK wire or poor ground (G501).
