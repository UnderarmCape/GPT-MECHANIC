Wireless Charger System Description - Overview

Some models come equipped with the wireless charger. The wireless charger can charge the battery like a smartphone that was placed on top of the wireless charger, without the charging cable. The wireless charger is compliant with the "Qi". The Qi is international standard for the wireless charging below 5 watts. This system uses the law of electromagnetic induction.
