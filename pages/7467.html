DTC P0441 (K20C2)

DTC P0441  : EVAP System Purge Flow Malfunction

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • If DTC P0441 is indicated alone, do the troubleshooting for DTC P04DF and P04F1 using the on-board snapshot for P0441.
  • If DTC P04F1 and P0441 are stored at the same time, check for a poor connection, a blockage, or damage at the EVAP canister purge line between the EVAP canister purge valve and the EVAP canister. Also check for a stuck closed EVAP canister purge valve.
  • If any of the DTCs listed below are indicated at the same time as DTC P0441, troubleshoot those DTCs first, then recheck for P0441.
