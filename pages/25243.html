DTC C0062-62: Longitudinal Acceleration Sensor Failure (Signal Compare Failure)

Confirmation Procedure Operating Condition:
  1. Start the engine.
  2. Test-drive the vehicle at 32 mph (50 km/h) or more for 6 minutes and press the brake pedal and stop the vehicle.
  3. Repeat step 2 for four times.

    (Drive the vehicle on the road, not on a lift. Be careful of the surrounding traffic conditions.)
Execution Initial Diagnosis/Regular Diagnosis
Duration About 3 seconds
DTC Type
  • 1 drive cycle
  • Brake system indicator (amber) on
  • VSA indicator on
Fail Safe Action
  • Stop hill start assist function
  • Stop automatic brake hold function
  • Stop drive away assist function
  • Stop ignition OFF apply function* 1
Possible Cause
  • SRS unit internal failure

*1: M/T
