Millimeter Wave Radar Removal and Installation (5-door) (2017 2018): Removal

NOTE: How to read the torque specifications .

  1. Front Bumper - Remove
  2. Millimeter Wave Radar - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Millimeter Wave Radar Assembly Removal 

    1. Disconnect the connector (A).

    2. Remove the harness clip (B).

    3. Remove the millimeter wave radar assembly (C).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Millimeter Wave Radar Subassembly Removal 

    NOTE:  The millimeter wave radar cannot be disassembled.

    1. Rotate the three clips (A) counterclockwise.

    2. Remove the millimeter wave radar subassembly (B) from the clips.

    3. Remove the clips from the radar bracket (C).
