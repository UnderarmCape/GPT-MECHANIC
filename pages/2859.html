Removal and Installation

Front Door Panel 

  1. Front Door Panel Grip Cover - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Pry out the bottom edge of the front door panel grip cover (A) with the trim tool (B), then remove the front door panel grip cover.
  2. Front Door Panel - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the screws (A, B).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Remove the clips (A, B).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 3. While holding the front door panel (A) away from the door, disconnect the connectors (B), and remove the harness clips (C), then remove the front door panel.
  3. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
