Removal and Installation

WARNING: 

Flammable - Keep Fire Away

NOTE:  Refer to the Fuel and Emissions Systems Service Precautions before disconnecting the fuel line.

  1. FTP Senor - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Disconnect the connector (A) and the hose (B).

    2. Remove the retainer (C) and the FTP sensor (D) with the O-ring (E).
  2. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal with a new retainer and a new O-ring.
