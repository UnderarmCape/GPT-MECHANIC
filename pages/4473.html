Removal and Installation

NOTE: How to read the torque specifications.

  1. Engine Coolant - Drain
  2. Air Cleaner - Remove
  3. Intake Air Duct E - Remove
    Fig 1: Intake Air Duct E Components With Torque Specifications (1.5L Engine)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    1. Disconnect the connectors (A).

    2. Remove the harness clamps (B).

    3. USA and Canada models: Disconnect purge tube D.

    4. Remove intake air duct E.

    NOTE: Make sure the end of hose band is aligned with the mark on the hose band after intake air duct E is installed.
  4. Thermostat - Remove
    Fig 2: Exploded View Of Thermostat Components With Torque Specifications (1.5L Engine)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    1. CVT: Remove the harness clamp (A).

    2. Remove the thermostat cover (B).

    3. Remove the thermostat (C).

    NOTE:  Be sure to install the thermostat in the direction shown.
  5. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
