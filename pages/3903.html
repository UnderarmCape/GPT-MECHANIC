Removal and Installation

NOTE:  Unless otherwise indicated, illustrations used in the procedure are for 1.5L engine.

  1. Vehicle - Lift
  2. Transmission Fluid - Drain
    • M/T
    • CVT
  3. Right Front Wheel - Remove
  4. Right Driveshaft - Remove 
  5. Intermediate Shaft Assembly - Remove
    Fig 1: Intermediate Shaft Assembly Components With Torque Specifications (Except K20C1 (M/T)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    NOTE:  Hold the intermediate shaft horizontal until it is clear of the differential to prevent damaging the oil seal (A).

  6. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Clean the areas where the intermediate shaft contacts the differential thoroughly with solvent, then dry them with compressed air.

    NOTE:  Do not wash the rubber parts with solvent.

    2. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
  7. Wheel Alignment - Check
  8. Test Drive - Check
