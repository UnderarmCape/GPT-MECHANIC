Piston, Ring, Pin, and Connecting Rod Removal and Installation (K20C2): Installation

  1. Piston Pin - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install a snap ring (A) only on one side.

    2. Coat the piston pin bore in the piston, the bore in the connecting rod, and the piston pin with new engine oil.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 3. Heat the piston to about 158°F (70°C).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Assemble the piston (A) and the connecting rod (B).

    NOTE:  Align the arrow (C) with the groove (D) direction on the connecting rod as shown.

    5. Install the piston pin (E).

    6. Install the remaining snap ring (F).

    7. Turn the snap rings in the ring grooves until the end gaps are positioned at the bottom of the piston.
  2. Piston Ring - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the top ring and the second ring as shown. The top ring (A) has a 1R mark, and the second ring (B) has a 2R mark. The manufacturing marks (C) must be facing upward.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Rotate the rings in their grooves to make sure they do not bind.

    3. Position the ring end gaps as shown.

    4. After installing a new set of rings, measure the ring-to-groove clearances:

    Top Ring Clearance 
    Standard (New):  0.060-0.085 mm (0.003 in) 
    Service Limit:  0.11 mm (0.004 in) 
    Second Ring Clearance 
    Standard (New):  0.035-0.060 mm (0.002 in) 
    Service Limit:  0.08 mm (0.003 in) 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  3. Piston/Connecting Rod Assembly - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the connecting rod bearing caps, then install the ring compressor. Check that the bearing is securely in place.

    2. Apply new engine oil to the piston, the inside of the ring compressor, and the cylinder bore, then attach the ring compressor to the piston/connecting rod assembly.

    3. Position the mark (A) to face the cam chain side of the engine.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Position the piston/connecting rod assembly in the cylinder, and tap it in using the wooden handle of a hammer (A). Push down on the ring compressor (B) to prevent the rings from expanding before entering the cylinder bore.

    5. Stop after the ring compressor pops free, and check the connecting rod-to-rod journal alignment before pushing the piston into place.
