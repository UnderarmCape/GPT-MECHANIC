Millimeter Wave Radar Removal and Installation (2/4-door) (2016 2017 2018): Removal

  1. Front Bumper - Remove
  2. Millimeter Wave Radar Assembly - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 NOTE:  Do not disassemble the millimeter wave radar.

    1. Disconnect the connector (A).

    2. Remove the millimeter wave radar assembly (B).
  3. Component of Millimeter Wave Radar - Remove (if necessary)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 NOTE:  Do not disassemble the millimeter wave radar body.

    1. Remove the TORX screws (A) using a TORX T20 bit.

    2. Remove the millimeter wave radar (C) from the bracket (D).

    3. Remove the vertical radar aiming bolt (E).

    4. Remove these items:

    • Spacer B
    • Aiming bolt (E)
    • Grip (F)
