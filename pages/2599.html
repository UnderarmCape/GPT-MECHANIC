Pairing a Cell Phone (Display Audio Type (7-inch Screen))

NOTE: 

  • To pair a cell phone, the vehicle has to be parked by applying the parking brake.
  • The screens detail indicated may change depending on equipment or specification.

1. Make sure the cell phone you are pairing is on and in its discovery mode.

2. Turn the vehicle to the ACCESSORY mode, and select OK on the disclaimer screen.

3. Press the HOME button, then select the Phone icon from the HOME screen.

NOTE:  Before pairing a cell phone, set the Automatic Phone Sync to ON or OFF.

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

4. If the Bluetooth power in the audio unit is off, a confirmation screen appears on the display. Select Yes to turn the Bluetooth power on.

5. A cell phone connection confirmation screen appears on the display. Select Yes to add a new cell phone.

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

6. Select the your Bluetooth Device from the device list. If the device name is not found in the list, select Reflash to recheck the Bluetooth cell phone.

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

7. If the HFL does not find the cell phone, select the Phone Not Found icon. When the 4-digit PIN appears on the display, enter the PIN into the cell phone.

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

8. Once the cell phone is discovered by the HFL, Confirm the connection on the cell phone.

NOTE:  The confirmation screen may vary by the cell phone. Depending on the Bluetooth version of cell phone, it may be necessary to operate the cell phone.

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

9. Once the cell phone is successfully paired, the connected device is added on the screen.

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

10. If the HondaLink Assist Setting screen appears on the display, select On or Off, then press the BACK button to exit the screen.

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
