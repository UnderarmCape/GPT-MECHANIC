DTC C0072-00: Brake Pad Temperature too High

Confirmation Procedure Operating Condition:
  • Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.
Execution Initial Diagnosis/Regular Diagnosis
Duration After setting temperature detection (immediate)
DTC Type
  • 1 drive cycle
  • Brake system indicator (amber) on*
Fail Safe Action
  • Stop driving support system's brake function

*: Without ACC

Possible Cause
  • Temporary brake pad high temperature condition

*: Without ACC
