DTC C1100-48

DTC C1100-48  : Electric Parking Brake Abnormal Operation Monitoring

NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description DTC
C1100-48 Electric Parking Brake Abnormal Operation Monitoring  

DTC (VSA)

  1. DTC check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode, then to the ON mode.

    -4. Wait for 30 seconds or more.

    -5. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
       

    Is DTC C1100-48 indicated?

    YES (only DTC C1100-48 is indicated)

    The failure is duplicated. Check for loose terminals and poor connections in the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting. If they are OK, replace the VSA modulator-control unit .

    YES (also other DTCs is indicated)

    Go to indicated DTC's troubleshooting.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting .
