Failure Reproduction Technique

NOTE:  Follow these rules while the vehicle is raised on a lift for the test-drive

  • In case of the vehicle speed below 31 mph (50 km/h): Disable the VSA by pressing the VSA OFF button.
  • In case of the vehicle speed above 31 mph (50 km/h): Enter the VSA maintenance mode .
  • VSA DTC(s) may come on when test-driving on a lift. If the VSA system sets a DTC(s), clear the DTC(s) with the HDS.
