DTC (Adaptive Damper System)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    Clear DTCs

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode, then to the ON mode.

    -4. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
    C1640-14 Left Front Damper Solenoid Circuit Open Failure    
    C1641-14 Right Front Damper Solenoid Circuit Open Failure    
    C1642-14 Left Rear Damper Solenoid Circuit Open Failure    
    DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
    C1643-14 Right Rear Damper Solenoid Circuit Open Failure    

    Is DTC C1640-14, C1641-14, C1642-14, or C1643-14 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting . If the freeze data/on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the freeze data/on-board snapshot.

  2. Open wire check (ADS COIL OUT lines)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connectors.

    Adaptive damper control unit 24P connector
    Appropriate damper actuator 2P connector

    -3. Check for continuity between the adaptive damper control unit 24P connector terminals and appropriate damper actuator 2P connector terminals individually (see table).

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Adaptive damper control unit 24P connector: disconnected
      Appropriate damper actuator 2P connector: disconnected
    DTC Circuit Name Adaptive Damper Control Unit 24P Connector Damper Actuator 2P Connector (female terminals):

    Is there continuity?

    1. YES 

      The ADS COIL OUT wires are OK. Replace the appropriate damper unit.

      • Rear

      NO 

      Repair an open in the wire between the adaptive damper control unit and the appropriate damper actuator.
