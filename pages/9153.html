DTC P0571 (L15B7/L15BY)

DTC P0571  : Brake Pedal Position Switch Circuit Malfunction

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • If the accelerator pedal is pressed with pressing the brake pedal lightly and continuously for 5 minutes or more, this DTC will be stored.
DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
P0571 Brake Pedal Position Switch Circuit Malfunction    

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Brake lights check 1

    -1. Check the brake lights without the brake pedal pressed.

    Do the brake lights stay on?

    YES 

    Go to step 9.

    NO 

    Go to step 2.

  2. Brake lights check 2

    -1. Check the brake lights with the brake pedal pressed.

    Do the brake lights come on?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Go to step 5.

  3. Brake pedal position switch signal check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Check the parameter (s) below with the HDS.

    Signal Current conditions
    Values Unit
    BRAKE SWITCH    

    Does the signal change ON and OFF when the brake pedal is pressed and released?

    YES 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the brake pedal position switch and the PCM. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

    NO 

    Go to step 4.

  4. Open wire check (STOP SW line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector A (50P)

    -4. Press the brake pedal, and measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    PCM connector A (50P): disconnected
    Brake pedal: pressed
    Test point 1 PCM connector A (50P) No. 26
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    The STOP SW wire is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P0571 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the STOP SW wire between the PCM (A26) and the brake pedal position switch.

  5. Brake pedal position switch check

    -1. Test the brake pedal position switch .

    Is the brake pedal position switch normal?

    YES 

    Go to step 6.

    NO 

    Replace the brake pedal position switch .

  6. Fuse check

    -1. Check the following fuse.

    Fuse No. A9 (10 A)
    Location Under-hood fuse/relay box

    Is the fuse OK?

    YES 

    Go to step 7.

    NO 

    Replace the No. A9 (10 A) fuse. Also check for a short to ground on the No. A9 (10 A) fuse circuit if needed.

  7. Open wire check (+B STOP line)

    -1. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    Test point 1 Brake pedal position switch 4P connector No. 2
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    The +B STOP wire is OK. Go to step 8.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the +B STOP wire between the No. A9 (10 A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box and the brake pedal position switch.

  8. Open wire check (STOP SW line)

    -1. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector A (50P)

    -3. Press the brake pedal, and measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    PCM connector A (50P): disconnected
    Brake pedal: pressed
    Test point 1 PCM connector A (50P) No. 26
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    The STOP SW wire is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P0571 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the STOP SW wire between the PCM (A26) and the brake pedal position switch.

  9. Brake pedal position switch installation check

    -1. Remove the brake pedal position switch with the connector is connected .

    -2. Check the brake lights with the rod of the brake pedal position switch pressed.

    Do the brake lights go off?

    YES 

    The brake pedal position switch installation is incorrect, install the brake pedal position switch and adjust the pedal height .

    NO 

    Go to step 10.

  10. Brake pedal position switch check

    -1. Test the brake pedal position switch .

    Is the brake pedal position switch normal?

    YES 

    The brake pedal position switch is OK, repair a short to power in the STOP SW wire between the PCM (A26) and the brake pedal position switch.

    NO 

    Replace the brake pedal position switch .
