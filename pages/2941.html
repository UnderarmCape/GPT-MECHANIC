    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE: 
    • Clean the spoiler surfaces where the seals are applied with a shop towel dampened with isopropyl alcohol. After cleaning, keep oil, grease, and water from getting on the surface.
    • Except Seal C: If you are reusing the original seals, apply adhesive primer to the areas where the new double-sided adhesive tape will be applied, and then attach the new double-sided adhesive tapes to the seals as shown.
    Adhesive tape requirements (A): Thickness 0.6 mm (0.024 in)
