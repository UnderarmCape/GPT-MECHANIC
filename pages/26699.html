BEFORE INSTALLING THE ACCESSORY AMBIENT LIGHTING KIT . . . READ THIS!

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): A16040B Version 1, Date of Issue:  April, 2016
HONDA: 2016 Civic

AFFECTED VEHICLES

2016 Civic

About to install the accessory Ambient Lighting Kit (P/N 08E10-TBA-100)? For a trouble-free installation, make sure you follow the installation instructions to the letter! 

This is especially important when doing step 35  . Make sure you plug the taped ambient light harness 12-pin male connector into the A/C control unit. Never  try plugging it into the audio unit! If you do, you'll burn out the gauge control module and your dealership will be responsible for its replacement!

35. Remove the tape from the ambient light harness 12-pin male connector in step 30 and plug it into the A/C control unit.

By the way, this also applies if you're replacing the audio unit and A/C control unit in a vehicle that already has this accessory. So, just remember what you read here for a trouble-free repair.
