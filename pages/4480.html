Cooling Fan Controls Symptom Troubleshooting Index (K20C1) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021)

Symptom  Diagnostic Procedure  Also check for 
Engine overheats
  • Check the coolant level .
  • Check for any engine coolant leakage (from gaskets, hoses, O-rings, etc.).
  • Check for dirt, leaves, or insects on the radiator and the A/C condenser.
  • Check for deteriorated coolant.
  • Check for a damaged or deformed fan shrouds.
  • Check the fan motors are operating properly (air should be blowing toward the engine).
  • Inspect the fan motors or fan relays .
  • Check the expansion tank cap .
  • Check the thermostat .
  • Inspect the water pump .
  • Check for plugged or deteriorated radiator hoses.
  • Check for clogged radiator.
  • Check for a plugged heater core or hoses.
  • Check for a damaged cylinder head gasket.
 Check the radiator and A/C condenser fans are operating properly
The radiator fan does not run at high speed Radiator fan high speed circuit troubleshooting . Check the connectors are properly connected and the terminals are making good contact
Both the radiator fan and the A/C condenser fan do not run at low speed Symptom troubleshooting . Check the connectors are properly connected and the terminals are making good contact
The A/C condenser fan does not run at high speed Symptom troubleshooting . Check the connectors are properly connected and the terminals are making good contact
