Side Curtain Airbags

The A-pillar trim, quarter pillar trim (2-door), and/or C-pillar trim (4/5-door) with side curtain airbags have a "SIDE CURTAIN AIRBAG" mark.

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

The side curtain airbag module assembly is a long jointed part containing an inflator, a flexible bag, and brackets. When removing or installing the side curtain airbag assembly, never:

  • Handle the flexible bag.
  • Drop the airbag assembly.
  • Cut, tear, or unwrap the tape strips.
