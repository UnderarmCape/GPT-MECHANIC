*1 1.5L Engine
  1. Detent Bolt - Remove
  2. 26.0 mm Detent Ball Spring - Remove
  3. 7.94 mm Steel Ball - Remove
  4. Detent Bolt - Remove
  5. 23.4 mm Detent Ball Spring - Remove
  6. 7.94 mm Steel Ball - Remove
  7. Breather Cap - Remove
  8. Change Lever Assembly - Remove
  9. Detent Bolt - Remove
  10. 26.0 mm Detent Ball Spring - Remove
  11. 7.94 mm Steel Ball - Remove
  12. Transmission Hanger C - Remove
  13. Filler Plug - Remove
  14. Back-Up Light Switch - Remove
  15. Output Shaft (Countershaft) Speed Sensor - Remove
  16. Neutral Position Sensor - Remove
  17. Detent Bolt - Remove
  18. 26.0 mm Detent Ball Spring - Remove
  19. 7.94 mm Steel Ball - Remove
  20. Oil Check Bolt - Remove
  21. 8 mm Flange Bolt - Remove
  22. Drain Plug - Remove
  23. 32 mm Sealing Cap - Remove
  24. 8 mm Flange Bolt - Loosen

    NOTE:  Loosen the 8 mm flange bolts in a crisscross pattern in several steps, then remove them.

  25. Clutch Line Bracket - Remove
  26. Transmission Hanger B - Remove
  27. Transmission Housing - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. While expanding the 72 mm snap ring (A) on the countershaft ball bearing with the snap ring pliers, lift the transmission housing (B). Release the snap ring pliers, and remove the transmission housing with the 14 x 20 mm dowel pins (C).
  28. Oil Seal - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    NOTE:  Be careful not to damage the transmission housing while removing the oil seal.

  29. Oil Guide Plate M - Remove
  30. 72 mm Shim - Remove
  31. Oil Gutter Plate A - Remove
  32. Oil Gutter Plate B - Remove
  33. 90 mm Shim - Remove
  34. 72 mm Snap Ring - Remove
  35. Oil Guide Plate C - Remove
  36. Reverse Shift Holder - Remove
  37. Baffle Plate - Remove
  38. Reverse Idler Gear Shaft Assembly/Reverse Shift Fork - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the reverse idler gear shaft assembly (A), the 33 mm spring washer (B), the thrust washer (C), and the reverse shift fork (D) as an assembly.
  39. Shift Piece Shaft - Remove
  40. Shift Piece - Remove
  41. Interlock - Remove
  42. Select Return Spring - Remove
  43. Shift Piece Collar - Remove
  44. 8 x 50 mm Pin - Remove
  45. Mainshaft, Countershaft, and Shift Fork Assembly - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Apply a thin layer of tape to the mainshaft splines (A) to protect the oil seal.

    NOTE:  Do not apply too much tape as it will damage the oil seal.

    2. Remove the mainshaft assembly (B) and the countershaft assembly (C) with the shift fork assembly (D).
  46. 64 mm Spring Washer - Remove
  47. Mainshaft Oil Seal - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    NOTE:  Be careful not to damage the clutch housing while removing the mainshaft oil seal.

  48. Bearing Set Plate - Remove
  49. Countershaft Bearing - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the bearing (A) using the 45-75 mm adjustable bearing puller and a commercially available 3/8"-16 UNF slide hammer (B).
  50. Differential Assembly - Remove
  51. Magnet - Remove

    NOTE:  Clean the magnet and the clutch housing.

  52. Oil Gutter Plate C - Remove (1.5L Engine)
  53. Oil Seal - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    NOTE:  Be careful not to damage the clutch housing while removing the oil seal.
