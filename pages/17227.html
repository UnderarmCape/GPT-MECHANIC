12 Volt Battery Sensor Removal and Installation: Removal

NOTE:  Canada models: When turning the vehicle to the ON mode for the first time after the 12 volt battery is reconnected, turn the vehicle to the ON mode while depressing the brake pedal for the immobilizer mutual authentication between the body control module and the VSA modulator-control unit.

  1. 12 Volt Battery Sensor - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Make sure the vehicle is in the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    2. Disconnect the connector (A).

    3. Disconnect the cable (B) from the 12 volt battery sensor (C).

    4. Remove the 12 volt battery sensor from the 12 volt battery.

    NOTE:  To protect the connector (D) from damage, do not hold it when removing the terminal.
