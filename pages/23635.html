Cam Chain Removal and Installation (1.5L Engine): Removal

NOTE:  Keep the cam chain away from magnetic fields.

  1. Right Front Wheel - Remove
  2. Engine Undercover - Remove
  3. Drive Belt - Remove
  4. No. 1 Piston at Top Dead Center - Set (Crank Side)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Turn the crankshaft so its white mark (A) on the crankshaft pulley lines up with the pointer (B) on the cam chain case.
  5. Crankshaft Pulley - Remove 
  6. Drive Belt Auto-Tensioner - Remove
  7. Dipstick - Remove
  8. Cylinder Head Cover - Remove
  9. Expansion Tank - Move

    NOTE:  Do not disconnect the water bypass hoses.

  10. Side Engine Mount - Remove
  11. Connector (VTC Oil Control Solenoid Valve A) - Disconnect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Disconnect the connector (A).

    2. Remove the harness clamp (B).
  12. VTC Oil Control Solenoid Valve A - Remove 
  13. Cam Chain Case - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  14. No. 1 Piston at Top Dead Center - Set (Cam Side)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Set the No. 1 piston at top dead center (TDC). The "UP" mark (C) on VTC actuator A should be at the top. Align the TDC marks (D) on VTC actuator A and VTC actuator B.
    NOTE:  If the marks are not aligned, rotate the crankshaft 360 degrees, and recheck VTC actuator A and VTC actuator B marks.
  15. Cam Chain Auto-Tensioner - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Loosely install the crankshaft pulley.

    2. Turn the crankshaft counterclockwise to compress the cam chain auto-tensioner.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 3. Rotate the crankshaft counterclockwise to align the holes on the lock (A) and the cam chain auto-tensioner (B).

    4. Insert a 1.2 mm (3/64 in) diameter pin (C) into the holes.

    5. Turn the crankshaft clockwise to secure the pin.

    NOTE:  If the holes in the lock and the cam chain auto-tensioner do not align, continue to rotating the crankshaft counterclockwise until the holes align, then install the pin.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 6. Remove the cam chain auto-tensioner (A) and the cam chain auto-tensioner filter (B).

    NOTE:  Check the cam chain auto-tensioner filter for damage. If filter is damaged, replace it.

    7. Remove the crankshaft pulley.
  16. Cam Chain Guide B - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  17. Cam Chain - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the cam chain guide (A), the cam chain tensioner arm (B), and the cam chain tensioner sub arm (C).

    2. Remove the cam chain.
