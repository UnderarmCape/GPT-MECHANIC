CVT Oil Seal Replacement (K20C2, CVT model): Replacement

NOTE:  Keep all foreign particles out of the transmission.

Left Differential Oil Seal 

  1. Vehicle - Lift
  2. Engine Undercover - Remove
  3. Transmission Fluid - Drain 
  4. Left Driveshaft Inboard Joint - Disconnect
  5. Left Differential Oil Seal - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  6. Left Differential Oil Seal - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the left differential oil seal (A) flush with the transmission housing using the 15 x 135L driver handle and the 58 mm oil seal driver attachment.
    NOTE:  Before installation, apply a light coat of clean transmission fluid to the surface both of the press-fit and the lip that are on the oil seal.
  7. Left Driveshaft Inboard Joint - Connect
  8. Transmission Fluid - Refill 
  9. Engine Undercover - Install

Right Differential Oil Seal 

  1. Vehicle - Lift
  2. Engine Undercover - Remove
  3. Transmission Fluid - Drain 
  4. Right Driveshaft Inboard Joint - Disconnect
  5. Right Differential Oil Seal - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  6. Right Differential Oil Seal - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the right differential oil seal (A) flush with the torque converter housing using the 65 mm oil seal driver.
    NOTE:  Before installation, apply a light coat of clean transmission fluid to the surface both of the press-fit and the lip that are on the oil seal.
  7. Right Driveshaft Inboard Joint - Connect
  8. Transmission Fluid - Refill 
  9. Engine Undercover - Install

Control Shaft Oil Seal 

  1. Air Cleaner - Remove
  2. Shift Cable (Transmission Side) - Disconnect
  3. Control Lever - Remove
  4. Transmission Range Switch - Remove
  5. Control Shaft Oil Seal - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  6. Control Shaft Oil Seal - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the control shaft oil seal (A) flush with the transmission housing using the 22 x 24 mm attachment.
    NOTE:  Before installation, apply a light coat of clean transmission fluid to the surface both of the press-fit and the lip that are on the oil seal.
  7. Transmission Range Switch - Install
  8. Control Lever - Install
  9. Shift Cable (Transmission Side) - Connect
  10. Air Cleaner - Install
