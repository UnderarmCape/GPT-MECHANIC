REVISION SUMMARY

AFFECTED VEHICLES was updated, and WARRANTY CLAIM INFORMATION was updated.

SYMPTOM

There is a rattling coming from the front of the vehicle.

PROBABLE CAUSE

The rubber damper cap(s) are faulty.

CORRECTIVE ACTION

Replace the rubber damper caps.

PARTS INFORMATION

PARTS INFORMATION

Part Name Part Number Quantity
Rubber Damper Cap (Shock Absorber Mounting Cap) 51608-TJB-A00 2

The normal warranty applies.

Skill Level: Repair Technician

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

Remove the rubber damper caps, then test-drive the vehicle.
  • If you no longer hear a rattling from the front of the vehicle with the caps removed, go to REPAIR PROCEDURE .
  • If you still hear a rattling from the front of the vehicle with the caps removed, this bulletin does not apply. Continue with normal system troubleshooting.

REPAIR PROCEDURE

Install the new rubber damper caps on both sides.
