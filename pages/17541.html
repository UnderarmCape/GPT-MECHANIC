Reverse Lockout System Symptom Troubleshooting - Driver can select reverse gear when vehicle speed is 12 mph (20 km/h) or more (L15B7/L15BA/K20C1/K20C2 (M/T))

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

NOTE: 

  • Refer to How to Troubleshoot Circuits at the PCM connectors in the Fuel and Emissions Systems section before troubleshooting this circuit .
  • Information marked with an asterisk (*1) applies to the L15BA/L15B7 engine, an asterisk (*2) applies to the K20C1 engine, and an asterisk (*3) applies to the K20C2 engine
  1. PGM-FI system DTC check

    -1. Test-drive the vehicle.

    -2. Check for Pending or Confirmed DTCs in the PGM-FI SYSTEM with the HDS.

    DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
         

    Are any DTCs stored?

    YES 

    Go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting. DTC TROUBLESHOOTING INDEX

    NO 

    DTC is not indicated. Go to step 2.

  2. Open wire check (IG1 (RVSLCK line) *1/IG1(RVS LCK SOL) line *2/IG1(RVS LCK) line *3):

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    Reverse lockout solenoid 2P connector

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      Reverse lockout solenoid 2P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Reverse lockout solenoid 2P connector (female terminals) No. 1:
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    The IG1 (RVSLCK) wire *1/IG1(RVS LCK SOL) wire *2/IG1(RVS LCK) wire *3 is OK. Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the IG1(RVSLCK) wire *1/IG1(RVS LCK SOL) wire *2/IG1(RVS LCK) wire *3 between the No. B21 (10 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box and the reverse lockout solenoid.

  3. Open wire check (RVSLCK line *1/RVSLCK/REVERSE LOCK SOL line *2/RVS LCK line *3):

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector No. 2 (58P) *2
    PCM connector E (80P) *1*3

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    PCM connector No. 2 (58P) *2: disconnected
    PCM connector E (80P) *1*3: disconnected
    Reverse lockout solenoid 2P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 PCM connector No. 2 (58P) No. 35*2
    PCM connector E (80P) No. 29*1*3
    Test point 2 Reverse lockout solenoid 2P connector (female terminals) No. 2:
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The RVSLCK wire *1/RVSLCK/REVERSE LOCK SOL wire *2/RVS LCK wire *3 is OK. Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the RVSLCK wire *1/RVSLCK/REVERSE LOCK SOL wire *2/RVS LCK wire *3 between the reverse lockout solenoid and the PCM.

  4. Reverse lockout solenoid check

    -1. Do the reverse lockout solenoid test .

    Is the reverse lockout solenoid OK?

    YES 

    The reverse lockout solenoid is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Repair or replace the reverse lockout solenoid .
