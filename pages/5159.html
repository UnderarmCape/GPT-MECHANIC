Removal and Installation: Procedure

NOTE:

Numbered call-outs in figure pertain to list numbers in following procedure.

Fig 1: Exploded View Of EVAP Canister Components With Torque Specifications
Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
Torque: N.m (kgf.m, lbf.ft)
  1. Front Floor Undercover - Remove
  2. Fuel Vent Tube, Hose, and Connector (EVAP Canister Vent Shut Valve and FTP Sensor) - Disconnect
  3. Quick-Connect Fitting - Disconnect
  4. EVAP Canister Assembly - Remove
  5. EVAP Canister Vent Shut Valve - Remove 
  6. FTP Sensor - Remove 
  7. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal with a new retainer and new O-rings.
    NOTE: Make sure the hose clamps are properly positioned and tightened .
