Steering Joint Disconnection and Reconnection: Removal

  1. Steering Wheel Holder Tool - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Center the steering wheel spoke angle.

    2. Install a commercially available steering wheel holder tool (A).
  2. Steering Joint - Disconnect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the steering joint cover (A).

    2. Hold the spline shaft (B) on the column with a piece of wire (C) between the joint yoke (D) of the spline shaft and the joint yoke (E) of the spline tube to prevent the spline shaft from pulling out.

    3. Remove the steering joint bolt (F).

    4. Disconnect the steering joint (G) by sliding the steering joint into the column shaft (H).

    NOTE: 

    • If the center guide (I) is in place and has not moved, leave it in place.
    • If the center guide has come off, discard it.
