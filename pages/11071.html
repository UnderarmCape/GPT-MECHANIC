Calibrate TPMS (With TPMS Switch)

  1. Make sure the tires are inflated to the specified tire pressure listed on the doorjamb label.
  2. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.
    NOTE: Vehicle must be stopped with the transmission in P or N position.
  3. Press the TPMS switch for 3 seconds. See Fig 1.
  4. When the calibration successfully begins, the indicator blinks twice.
NOTE: If the indicator does not blink, check the TPMS switch.
