Engine Oil Replacement (1.5L Engine): Replacement

NOTE: How to read the torque specifications.

  1. Engine - Warm Up
    1. Start the engine. Hold the engine speed at 3, 000 rpm with no load (M/T in neutral, CVT in P or N) until the cooling fan comes on, then turn off the engine.
  2. Vehicle - Lift
  3. Engine Undercover Plate - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  4. Engine Oil - Drain
    Fig 1: Engine Oil Drain Bolt With Torque Specifications
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    1. Remove the drain bolt (A), and drain the engine oil.

    2. Reinstall the drain bolt with a new washer (B) and torque to specification.
  5. Engine Oil - Refill

    1. Refill the engine with the recommended engine oil .

    Capacity 
    At Oil Change:  3.2 L (3.4 US qt) 
    At Oil Change including Filter:  3.5 L (3.7 US qt) 
    After Engine Overhaul:  4.0 L (4.2 US qt) 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  6. Engine Oil Level - Check
    1. Run the engine for a minimum of 3 minutes, then check the oil level  and for any leaks.
  7. Engine Undercover Plate - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  8. Maintenance Minder - Reset (With Maintenance Minder System)
    1. If the Maintenance Minder required to replace the engine oil, reset the Maintenance Minder with the gauge (see "Resetting the Maintenance Minder"). If the Maintenance Minder did not require to replace the engine oil, reset the Maintenance Minder with the HDS (see "Resetting the Individual Maintenance Items").
