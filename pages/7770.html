DTC P219C, P219D, P219E, P219F (K20C1) (17-21)

DTC P219C  : No. 1 Cylinder Air-Fuel Ratio Variation

DTC P219D  : No. 2 Cylinder Air-Fuel Ratio Variation

DTC P219E  : No. 3 Cylinder Air-Fuel Ratio Variation

DTC P219F  : No. 4 Cylinder Air-Fuel Ratio Variation

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • If any of the DTCs listed below are indicated at the same time as DTC P219C, P219D, P219E, and/or P219F, troubleshoot those DTCs first, then recheck for P219C, P219D, P219E, and/or P219F.

    P0131, P0132, P0133, P2195, P2237, P2238, P2243, P2251: A/F sensor (Sensor 1)

    P0134, P0135: A/F sensor (Sensor 1) heater

    P0201, P0202, P0203, P0204: No. 1-No. 4 cylinder injector (s)

    P0300: Random misfire

    P0301, P0302, P0303, P0304: No. 1-No. 4 cylinder misfire

    P0351, P0352, P0353, P0354: No. 1-No. 4 cylinder ignition coil (s)

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Record all the on-board snapshots with the HDS.

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -3. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    Clear DTC

    -4. Start the engine.

    -5. Hold the engine speed at 3, 000 rpm without load (in neutral) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.

    Test-drive the vehicle for several minutes in the range of these recorded on-board snapshot parameters:

    • Engine Speed
    • Vehicle Speed
    • ECT Sensor 1
    • MAP Sensor (Hi Res)
    • REL TP Sensor

    Is DTC P219C, P219D, P219E, and/or P219F indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

  2. Engine compression check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Do the cylinder compression test .

    Is there a cylinder with lower compression than the other cylinders?

    YES 

    Repair the engine compression problem.

    NO 

    Go to step 3.

  3. Valve clearance check

    -1. Inspect the valve clearance .

    Is the valve clearance OK?

    YES 

    Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Adjust the valve clearance .

  4. Injector check

    -1. Substitute a known-good injector (s) of the problem cylinder (s) .

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -3. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    Clear DTC

    -4. Start the engine.

    -5. Hold the engine speed at 3, 000 rpm without load (in neutral) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.

    Test-drive the vehicle for several minutes in the range of these recorded on-board snapshot parameters:

    • Engine Speed
    • Vehicle Speed
    • ECT Sensor 1
    • MAP Sensor (Hi Res)
    • REL TP Sensor

    Is the DTC (s) of the problem cylinder (s) indicated?

    YES 

    The injector (s) is OK. Go to step 5.

    NO 

    Replace all injectors as a set .

  5. Intake and exhaust manifold visual check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Check for carbon build-up in the intake manifold and the exhaust manifold.

    Is there carbon build-up in the intake manifold and/or exhaust manifold?

    YES 

    Check the engine for oil leaks. If needed, repair the engine, also remove the carbon.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check the engine for oil leaks.
