Engine Lubrication System Symptom Troubleshooting - Low oil pressure indicator does not come on with the vehicle in the ON mode

  1. Gauge control module test

    -1. Connect the HDS to the DLC .

    -2. Check for DTCs . If a DTC is present, diagnose, and repair the cause before continuing with this test.

    DTC (PGM-FI)

    -3. Check the parameter (s) below with the HDS.

    Signal Threshold Current conditions
    Values Unit Values Unit
    OIL PRESSURE SWITCH ON      

    Do the current condition (s) match the threshold?

    YES 

    Replace the gauge control module .

    NO 

    Go to step 2.

  2. Oil pressure switch test

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Check the oil pressure switch .

    Is the oil pressure switch OK?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Replace the oil pressure switch .

  3. Open wire check (ENG OP SW line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    NOTE:  This step must be done to protect the PCM from damage.

    -3. Disconnect the following connectors.

    PCM connector E (80P)
    Oil pressure switch 1P connector

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    PCM connector E (80P): disconnected
    Oil pressure switch 1P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 PCM connector E (80P) No. 58
    Test point 2 Oil pressure switch 1P connector No. 1
     
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the ENG OP SW wire between the oil pressure switch and the PCM.
