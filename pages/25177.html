DTC C1110-13, C1111-13

DTC C1110-13  : Left Rear Electric Parking Brake Actuator Circuit Open

DTC C1111-13  : Right Rear Electric Parking Brake Actuator Circuit Open

NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description DTC
C1110-13 Left Rear Electric Parking Brake Actuator Circuit Open  
C1111-13 Right Rear Electric Parking Brake Actuator Circuit Open  

DTCs (VSA)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode, then to the ON mode.

    -4. Apply and release the parking brake several times.

    -5. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
    C1110-13 Left Rear Electric Parking Brake Actuator Circuit Open  
    C1111-13 Right Rear Electric Parking Brake Actuator Circuit Open  

    Is DTC C1110-13 or C1111-13 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting .

  2. Power source check

    -1. Check the parameter(s) below with the HDS.

    Signal Current conditions
    Values Unit
    Left Parking Brake Actuator Motor Supply Voltage    
    Right Parking Brake Actuator Motor Supply Voltage    

    Is there battery voltage indicated?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Go to step 5.

  3. Open wire check (EPB L ACT+, EPB L ACT-, EPB R ACT+, or EPB R ACT- lines)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connectors.

    Appropriate electric parking brake actuator 2P connector
    VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector

    -3. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Appropriate electric parking brake actuator 2P connector: disconnected
      VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector: disconnected
    DTC Test point 1 Test point 2
    C1110-13 Left electric parking brake actuator 2P connector (female terminals) No. 1: VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector No. 8
    Left electric parking brake actuator 2P connector (female terminals) No. 2: VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector No. 7
    C1111-13 Right electric parking brake actuator 2P connector (female terminals) No. 1: VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector No. 5
    Right electric parking brake actuator 2P connector (female terminals) No. 2: VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector No. 6
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit and the appropriate electric parking brake actuator.

  4. Electric parking brake actuator check

    -1. Substitute a known-good electric parking brake actuator .

    -2. Reconnect the following connectors.

    VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector
    Appropriate electric parking brake actuator 2P connector

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -5. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode, then to the ON mode.

    -6. Apply and release the parking brake several times.

    -7. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
    C1110-13 Left Rear Electric Parking Brake Actuator Circuit Open  
    C1111-13 Right Rear Electric Parking Brake Actuator Circuit Open  

    Is DTC C1110-13 or C1111-13 indicated?

    YES 

    Check for loose terminals and poor connections in the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting. If they are OK, replace the VSA modulator-control unit .

    NO 

    Replace the appropriate electric parking brake actuator .

  5. Fuse check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Check the following fuse.

    Fuse No. A3-2 (40 A)
    Location Under-hood fuse/relay box

    Is the fuse OK?

    YES 

    The fuse is OK. Reinstall the fuse. Repair an open in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit and the under-hood fuse/relay box.

    NO 

    Replace the fuse. If the fuse blows again, repair a short to body ground in the No. A3-2 (40 A) fuse circuit.
