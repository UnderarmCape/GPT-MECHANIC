Engine Oil Replacement Reminder Reset - Procedure 13

  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
    NOTE: Models with the smart entry system have an ENGINE START/STOP button instead of an ignition switch.
  2. Press the DISPLAY/INFORMATION button repeatedly until the Wrench Icon appears. See Fig 1.
  3. Press the ENTER button.
  4. Press and hold the ENTER button for about 10 seconds to enter the reset mode.
  5. Press the UP/DOWN ARROW buttons to select a maintenance item to reset, or to select ALL DUE ITEMS (You can also select CANCEL to end the process).
  6. Press the ENTER button to reset the selected item.
NOTE: Repeat from step 4 for any other items needing to be reset.
