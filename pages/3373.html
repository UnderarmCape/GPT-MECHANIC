DTC U3003-16: Electric Parking Brake Control Unit Power Source Circuit (+B) Low Voltage

Confirmation Procedure Basic Condition:
  • 12 volt battery voltage 9 - 16 V

Operating Condition:

  • Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, then wait for 10 seconds or more.
Execution Initial Diagnosis
Duration About 10 seconds
DTC Type
  • 1 drive cycle
  • Brake system indicator (red) flash
  • Brake system indicator (amber) on
Fail Safe Action
  • Electric parking brake actuator shall be interrupted or not be activated
Possible Cause
  • Low 12 volt battery voltage
  • VSA modulator-control unit internal failure
