Drive Belt Inspection (K20C1) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Inspection

  1. Drive Belt - Inspect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Inspect the belt for cracks or damage. If the belt is cracked or damaged, replace it .
    2. Check the position of the drive belt auto-tensioner indicator pointer (A) is within the standard range (B) as shown. If it is out of the standard range, replace the drive belt .
    NOTE:  When replacing a new drive belt, check the position of the drive belt auto-tensioner indicator pointer is within the standard range (C) as shown.
