    1. Disconnect the connector (A).

    Fig 1: Exploded View Of Turbocharger Joint Components With Torque Specifications (1.5L Engine)

    2. Remove the harness holder (C) and the hose joint (D).

    NOTE: Make sure the end of hose band is aligned with the mark on the hose band after intake air duct G is installed.

    4. Remove the turbocharger joint (H).

    5. If necessary, remove the turbocharger joint bracket (I), turbocharger joint bracket B, the harness clamp bracket (J), and the turbocharger joint heat insulator (K).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
