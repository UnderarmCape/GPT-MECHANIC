DTC P2583-92 (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021)

DTC P2583-92  : CMBS or FCW Over Working

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • This DTC is recorded when the CMBS is activated several times in a single vehicle ON mode cycle.
  • If this DTC is recorded, the CMBS function does not return to normal until the DTC is cleared with the HDS.
  • If this DTC is recorded, the following is suspected:
    • -

      The millimeter wave radar is installed improperly and close-range obstacles are detected frequently.

    • -

      The millimeter wave radar installing portion is deformed by a minor collision or strong impact.

    • -

      The beam axis of the millimeter wave radar is out of alignment due to abnormal wheel alignment, abnormal tire pressure, etc.

    • -

      The CMBS is activated several times in a row intentionally by the driver.

  • After inspecting, repairing, or adjusting a possible cause of the failure, be sure to perform millimeter wave radar aiming . If the DTC cannot be deleted after millimeter wave radar aiming, replace the millimeter wave radar .
  • The functions of the integrated driver support system that will be temporarily canceled when this DTC is recorded can be confirmed with the DTC troubleshooting index .
DTC Description DTC
P2583-92 CMBS or FCW Over Working  

DTC (IDAS)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, then wait for at least 6 seconds.

    -5. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
    P2583-92 CMBS or FCW Over Working  

    Is DTC P2583-92 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting .

  2. Millimeter wave radar installation check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Remove the front bumper .

    -3. Check that the millimeter wave radar and radar bracket are installed properly and check them for deformation and damage.

    Are the millimeter wave radar and the radar bracket installed properly, and/or is there any no damage?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Repair or replace faulty parts, then aim the millimeter wave radar .

  3. Millimeter wave radar check (aiming)

    -1. Install the front bumper .

    -2. Aim the millimeter wave radar .

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -5. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -6. Test-drive the vehicle in a speed range where the CMBS is activated .

    -7. Check the operation of the CMBS.

    Is the CMBS activated frequently?

    YES 

    If the millimeter wave radar is installed properly but the DTC is recorded again after millimeter wave radar aiming, replace the millimeter wave radar .

    NO 

    The system is OK at this time.
