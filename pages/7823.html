DTC P2626

DTC P2626  : A/F Sensor (Sensor 1) Circuit (LAF-CA, LAF-CP) Open Malfunction

NOTE:  Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
P2626 A/F Sensor (Sensor 1) Circuit (LAF-CA, LAF-CP) Open Malfunction    

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    Clear DTC

    -3. Start the engine.

    -4. Hold the engine speed at 3000 RPM without load (in neutral) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.

    -5. Check for Pending or Confirmed DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
    P2626 A/F Sensor (Sensor 1) Circuit (LAF-CA, LAF-CP) Open Malfunction    

    Is DTC P2626 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the A/F sensor (Sensor 1) and the PCM. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

  2. Open wire check (LAF CA line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connectors.

    A/F sensor (Sensor 1) 6P connector
    PCM connector No. 1 (96P)

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      A/F sensor (Sensor 1) 6P connector: disconnected
      PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    Test point 1 A/F sensor (Sensor 1) 6P connector (female terminals) No. 6:
    Test point 2 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 55
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The LAF CA wire is OK. Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the LAF CA wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 55 and the A/F sensor (Sensor 1).

  3. Open wire check (LAF CP line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      A/F sensor (Sensor 1) 6P connector: disconnected
      PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    Test point 1 A/F sensor (Sensor 1) 6P connector (female terminals) No. 4:
    Test point 2 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 37
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The LAF CP wire is OK. Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the LAF CP wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 37 and the A/F sensor (Sensor 1).

  4. A/F sensor (Sensor 1) check

    -1. Substitute a known-good A/F sensor (Sensor 1) .

    -2. Reconnect all connectors.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    Clear DTC

    -5. Start the engine.

    -6. Hold the engine speed at 3000 RPM without load (in neutral) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.

    -7. Check for Pending or Confirmed DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
    P2626 A/F Sensor (Sensor 1) Circuit (LAF-CA, LAF-CP) Open Malfunction    

    Is DTC P2626 indicated?

    YES 

    The A/F sensor (Sensor 1) is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P2626 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Replace the original A/F sensor (Sensor 1) .
