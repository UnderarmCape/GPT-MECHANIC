DTC U1281, U1286, U128D, U128E (5-door with Wireless Charger) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021)

DTC U1281  : Wireless Charger Unit Lost Communication with MICU (Body Control Module)

DTC U1286  : Wireless Charger Unit Lost Communication with Keyless Access Control Unit (Body Control Module)

DTC U128D  : Wireless Charger Unit Lost Communication with Gauge Control Module

DTC U128E  : Wireless Charger Unit Lost Communication with Audio-Navigation Unit

NOTE: 

  • According to the detected DTC(s), check for the power circuit and the ground circuit of the control unit which cannot communicate with the wireless charger.
  • Refer to the DTC shown on the display, then inspect the connectors and terminals based on the instructions.
  • If you are troubleshooting multiple DTCs, be sure to follow the instructions in B-CAN System Diagnosis Test Mode A .

DTC (Wireless Charger)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode and then the ON mode.

    -4. Wait for at least 6 seconds.

    -5. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
    U1281 Wireless Charger Unit Lost Communication with MICU (Body Control Module)  
    U1286 Wireless Charger Unit Lost Communication with Keyless Access Control Unit (Body Control Module)  
    U128D Wireless Charger Unit Lost Communication with Gauge Control Module  
    U128E Wireless Charger Unit Lost Communication with Audio-Navigation Unit  

    Are any DTCs indicated?

    YES 

    Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the transmitting control units and the wireless charger.

  2. Open wire check (B-CAN_H line, B-CAN_L line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the receiving control unit connector.

    Wireless charger 7P connector

    -3. Disconnect the transmitting control unit connector(s).

    Refer to the DTC shown on the display, then disconnect the connector(s) based on the instructions (see table).

    DTC Connector
    U1281
    U1286    		 Body control module connector B (36P)
    U128D Gauge control module connector A (32P)
    U128E Audio-navigation unit connector A (24P)

    -4. Check for continuity between the receiving control unit and the transmitting control unit on the B-CAN_H circuit and the B-CAN_L circuit.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The B-CAN_H wire and the B-CAN_L wire are OK.

    Refer to the DTC shown on the display, then substitute or replace the control unit based on the instructions (see table).

    DTC Operation for transmitting control unit
    U1281
    U1286    		 Substitute a known-good body control module , then recheck. If DTC U1281/U1286 goes away after substitution, replace the original body control module .
    U128D Substitute a known-good gauge control module , then recheck. If DTC U128D goes away after substitution, replace the original gauge control module .
    U128E Substitute a known-good audio-navigation unit , then recheck. If DTC U128E goes away after substitution, replace the original audio-navigation unit .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the B-CAN_H wire and/or the B-CAN_L wire between the wireless charger and the transmitting control unit.
