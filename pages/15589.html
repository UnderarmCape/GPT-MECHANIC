Fuel Pump Circuit Troubleshooting (L15B7/L15BA/L15BY)

If you suspect a problem with the fuel pump, check that the fuel pump actually runs; when it is on, you will hear some noise if you listen to the fuel fill port. The fuel pump should run for 2 seconds when the vehicle is turned to ON mode. If the fuel pump does not make noise, check as follows:

  1. Determine possible failure area (FI MAIN RLY OUT line, others)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Remove PGM-FI main relay 2 .

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      PGM-FI main relay 2: disconnected
    Test point 1 PGM-FI main relay 2 4P socket No. 3
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Go to step 2.

  2. Open wire check (FI MAIN RLY OUT line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    Relay circuit board connector C (18P)

    -3. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    PGM-FI main relay 2: disconnected
    Relay circuit board connector C (18P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Relay circuit board connector C (18P) No. 17
    Test point 2 PGM-FI main relay 2 4P socket No. 3
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Remove and test the relay circuit board , and replace it if needed.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the FI MAIN RLY OUT wire between the relay circuit board and PGM-FI main relay 2.

  3. Open wire check (IG1 FUEL PUMP line)

    -1. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      PGM-FI main relay 2: disconnected
    Test point 1 PGM-FI main relay 2 4P socket No. 1
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Check the No. B8 (15 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box. If the fuse is OK, repair an open in the IG1 FUEL PUMP wire between the No. B8 (15 A) fuse and PGM-FI main relay 2. If needed, replace the under-dash fuse/relay box .

  4. Open wire check (FUEL PUMP RLY CL- line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector A (50P)

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      PGM-FI main relay 2: disconnected
      PCM connector A (50P): disconnected
    Test point 1 PGM-FI main relay 2 4P socket No. 4
    Test point 2 PCM connector A (50P) No. 11
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The FUEL PUMP RLY CL- wire is OK. Go to step 5.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the FUEL PUMP RLY CL- wire between the PCM (A11) and PGM-FI main relay 2.

  5. Determine possible failure area (PGM-FI main relay 2, others)

    -1. Reinstall PGM-FI main relay 2.

    -2. Connect terminals A and B with a jumper wire.

    Terminal A PCM connector A (50P) No. 5
    Terminal B Body ground

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      PCM connector A (50P): disconnected
      PCM connector A (50P) No. 5: jumped to body ground
    Test point 1 PCM connector A (50P) No. 11
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    Go to step 6.

    NO 

    Replace PGM-FI main relay 2.

  6. PCM operation check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Remove PGM-FI main relay 2 .

    -3. Remove the jumper wire from PCM connector A (50P).

    -4. Reconnect PCM connector A (50P).

    -5. Exit the SCS mode with the HDS.

    -6. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, and measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Within 2 seconds after turning vehicle to ON mode
      PGM-FI main relay 2: disconnected
    Test point 1 PGM-FI main relay 2 4P socket No. 3
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Go to step 7.

  7. Determine possible failure area (FUEL PUMP RLY OUT line, GND line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    Fuel tank unit 4P connector

    -3. Reinstall PGM-FI main relay 2 .

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, and measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Within 2 seconds after turning vehicle to ON mode
      Fuel tank unit 4P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Fuel tank unit 4P connector No. 1
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    Go to step 9.

    NO 

    Go to step 8.

  8. Open wire check (FUEL PUMP RLY OUT line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Remove PGM-FI main relay 2 .

    -3. Connect terminals A and B with a jumper wire.

    Terminal A PGM-FI main relay 2 4P socket No. 1
    Terminal B PGM-FI main relay 2 4P socket No. 2
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -5. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      Fuel tank unit 4P connector: disconnected
      PGM-FI main relay 2 :disconnected
      PGM-FI main relay 2 4P socket No. 1 and No. 2: jumped
    Test point 1 Fuel tank unit 4P connector No. 1
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    The FUEL PUMP RLY OUT wire is OK. Replace PGM-FI main relay 2.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the FUEL PUMP RLY OUT wire between PGM-FI main relay 2 and the fuel tank unit.

  9. Open wire check (GND line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Fuel tank unit 4P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Fuel tank unit 4P connector No. 3
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The GND wire is OK. Replace the fuel pump .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the GND wire between the fuel tank unit and G601.
