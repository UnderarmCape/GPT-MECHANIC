Trunk Lid Weatherstrip Removal and Installation: Installation

  1. Trunk Lid Weatherstrip - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:  Locate the painted alignment mark (A) on the trunk lid weatherstrip (B). Align the painted mark in the center of the trunk lid opening, and install the weatherstrip all the way around in the direction shown. Make sure there are no wrinkles in the weatherstrip.
