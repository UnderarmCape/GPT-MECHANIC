DTC P0135 (K20C1) (2019 2020 2021)

DTC P0135:  Air/Fuel Ratio (A/F) Sensor (Sensor 1) Heater Circuit Malfunction

General Description 

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

A heater for the sensor element is embedded in the air/fuel ratio (A/F) sensor (sensor 1), and it is controlled and monitored by the powertrain control module (PCM). It is activated and heats the sensor to stabilize and speed up the detection of oxygen content when the exhaust gas temperature is cold. If the A/F sensor (sensor 1) heater output current or voltage is a specified value, the PCM detects a malfunction and stores a DTC.

Monitor Execution, Sequence, Duration, DTC Type 

Execution Continuous
Sequence None
Duration 0.5 second or more
DTC Type Two drive cycles, MIL on

Enable Conditions 

Condition Minimum Maximum
12 volt battery voltage [Battery] 8 V 16 V
A/F sensor (sensor 1) heater duty* 4 % -

[ ]: HDS Parameter

Malfunction Threshold 

  • Short circuit to power

    The A/F sensor (sensor 1) heater output current is 24 A or more while the power stage is on.

  • Short circuit to ground

    The A/F sensor (sensor 1) heater output voltage is 2.74 V or less while the power stage is off.

  • Open circuit

    The A/F sensor (sensor 1) heater output voltage is between 3.26 - 4.7 V while the power stage is off.

Possible Cause 

NOTE:  The causes shown may not be a complete list of all potential problems, and it is possible that there may be other causes.

  • A/F sensor (sensor 1) AFHT line open
  • A/F sensor (sensor 1) AFHT line short to ground
  • A/F sensor (sensor 1) AFHT line short to power
  • A/F sensor (sensor 1) power supply line open
  • A/F sensor (sensor 1) power supply line short to ground
  • PGM-FI subrelay FI SUB RLY CL- line open
  • A/F sensor (sensor 1) failure
  • PCM internal circuit failure

Confirmation Procedure 

Operating Condition 

Start the engine, and let it idle.

Diagnosis Details 

Conditions for setting the DTC 

When a malfunction is detected during the first drive cycle, a Pending DTC is stored in the PCM memory. If the malfunction returns in the next (second) drive cycle, the MIL comes on and a Confirmed DTC and the freeze data are stored.

Conditions for clearing the DTC 

The MIL is cleared if the malfunction does not return in three consecutive trips in which the diagnostic runs. The MIL, the Pending DTC, the Confirmed DTC, and the freeze data can be cleared with the scan tool Clear command.
