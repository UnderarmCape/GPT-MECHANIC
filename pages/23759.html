Piston, Ring, Pin, and Connecting Rod Removal and Installation (1.5L Engine): Installation

  1. Piston Pin - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install a snap ring (A) only on one side.

    2. Coat the piston pin bore in the piston, the bore in the connecting rod, and the piston pin with new engine oil.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 3. Heat the piston to about 158°F (70°C).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Assemble the piston (A) and the connecting rod (B).

    NOTE:  Align the arrow (C) with the groove (D) direction on the connecting rod as shown.

    5. Install the piston pin (E).

    6. Install the remaining snap ring (F).

    7. Turn the snap rings in the ring grooves until the end gaps are positioned at the bottom of the piston.
  2. Piston Ring - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the piston rings as shown. The top ring (A) has a 1RF mark, and the second ring (B) has a 2RF mark. The manufacturing marks (C) must be facing upward.

    2. After installing a new set of rings, measure the ring-to-groove clearances:

    NOTE:  Make sure of the mark (A) on the piston.

    Mark:  59B or 5PA 
    Top Ring Clearance (59B) 
    Standard (New):  0.065-0.090 mm (0.003 in) 
    Service Limit:  0.130 mm (0.005 in) 
    Top Ring Clearance (5PA) 
    Standard (New):  0.040-0.065 mm (0.002 in) 
    Service Limit:  0.130 mm (0.005 in) 
    Second Ring Clearance 
    Standard (New):  0.030-0.055 mm (0.002 in) 
    Service Limit:  0.120 mm (0.004 in) 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 3. Rotate the rings in their grooves to make sure they do not bind.

    4. Position the ring end gaps as shown.
  3. Piston/Connecting Rod Assembly - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the connecting rod caps. Check that the bearing is securely in place.

    2. Apply new engine oil to the piston, the inside of the ring compressor, and the cylinder bore, then attach the ring compressor to the piston/connecting rod assembly.

    3. Position the piston/connecting rod assembly with the arrow (A) facing the cam chain side of the engine block.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Position the piston/connecting rod assembly in the cylinder, and tap it in using the wooden handle of a hammer. Push down on the ring compressor (A) to prevent the rings from expanding before entering the cylinder bore.

    5. Stop after the ring compressor pops free, and check the connecting rod-to-rod journal alignment before pushing the piston into place.
