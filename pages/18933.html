DISPLAY AUDIO SCREEN PROBLEMS?

DISPLAY AUDIO SCREEN PROBLEMS?

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): A15120G Version 1, Date of Issue:  December 8, 2015
HONDA: 2016 Civic

AFFECTED VEHICLES

2016 Civic

If the Display Audio (DA) screen has display-related problems, they're coming from the center display assembly, not the audio unit or audio-navigation unit.

Typical display-related problems include the following:

  • Blank screen
  • Flickering or flashing screen
  • Discolored screen
  • No back lighting
  • No touch response

To fix such problems, don't  order a reman audio unit or audio-navigation unit. A replacement center display assembly is all you need.
