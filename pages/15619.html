Acceleration Sensor Removal, Installation, and Inspection (Type-R) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Inspection

  1. HDS - Connect
  2. Acceleration sensor - Inspection
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    2. Connect the acceleration sensor connector.

    3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    4. With the HDS, check the following signal in the ADAPTIVE DAMPER SYSTEM DATA LIST by slowly moving the acceleration sensor as shown in the illustration.

    5. If the voltages do not change or out of specification, replace the acceleration sensor.
    Signal Specified value
    FRONT ACCELERATION SENSOR VOLTAGE About 0.39-4.77 V
    RIGHT REAR ACCELERATION SENSOR VOLTAGE
    LEFT REAR ACCELERATION SENSOR VOLTAGE
