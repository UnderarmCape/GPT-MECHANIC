Yaw Rate-Acceleration Neutral Position Memorization: Procedure

NOTE: 

  • The VSA modulator-control unit receives the yaw rate and the acceleration information from the SRS unit. This information is corrected by the neutral position learning of VSA modulator-control unit, performs the VSA control. Follow the procedures of the VSA system for more details.
  • When you replace or update the SRS unit, memorize the yaw rate-acceleration neutral position.
