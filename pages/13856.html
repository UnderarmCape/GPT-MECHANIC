Idle speed fluctuates (MIL works OK, no DTCs set) (K20C2: USA/Canada models)

Diagnostic procedure

  • Do the PCM idle learn procedure .
  • Check the idle speed .
  • Do the carbon accumulation check .
  • With A/C: Troubleshoot the A/C signal circuit .
  • With A/C: Check the A/C refrigerant amount .

    NOTE:  Turning the A/C to ON under the conditions that the A/C refrigerant amount is out of specification (excessive or too little) may cause the idle speed fluctuation.

Also check for

  • Incorrect valve adjustment
  • Intake air leaks
