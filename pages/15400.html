Mainshaft Disassembly, Reassembly, and Inspection (K20C1 (M/T)) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Mainshaft - Inspect

    1. Inspect the oil passage, gear, and bearing contact areas for wear and damage, then measure the mainshaft at points A, B, C, D, E, and F. If any part of the mainshaft is less than the service limit, replace it.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
      Measurement Standard Service Limit
    A  Ball Bearing Contact Area (Transmission Housing Side)  38.984-39.000 mm (1.53480-1.53543 in)  38.984 mm (1.53480 in) 
    B  6th Gear Distance Collar Contact Area  38.984-39.000 mm (1.53480-1.53543 in)  38.984 mm (1.53480 in) 
    C  4th/5th Gear Distance Collar Contact Area  41.984-42.000 mm (1.65291-1.65354 in)  41.984 mm (1.65291 in) 
    D  Needle Bearing Contact Area  47.984-48.000 mm (1.88913-1.88976 in)  47.984 mm (1.88913 in) 
    E  Ball Bearing Contact Area (Clutch Housing Side)  28.002-28.015 mm (1.10244-1.10295 in)  28.002 mm (1.10244 in) 
    F  Flywheel Pilot Bearing Contact Area  19.967-19.980 mm (0.78610-0.78661 in)  19.967 mm (0.78610 in) 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Inspect the runout by supporting both ends of the mainshaft. Then rotate the mainshaft two complete turns while measuring with a dial gauge. If the runout exceeds the standard, replace the mainshaft.
    Standard:  0.02 mm (0.0008 in) max.
