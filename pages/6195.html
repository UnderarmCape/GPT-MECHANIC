Fuel Tank Removal and Installation (Except L15BY): Removal

WARNING: 

Flammable - Keep Fire Away

NOTE:  Refer to the Fuel and Emissions Systems Service Precautions before disconnecting/connecting the fuel line.

  1. Fuel Pressure - Relieve
  2. Fuel Tank - Drain
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Drain the fuel tank, then reinstall the fuel tank unit and the fuel tank unit cam lock ring without connecting the fuel tank unit connector, the quick-connect fitting, the fuel vent tube, and fuel tank vapor recirculation tube .
    NOTE:  When installing the fuel tank unit cam lock ring (A) using the fuel sender wrench (B), temporarily set the positioning edge (C) of the fuel tank at the fuel tank unit cam lock ring tabs (D) on the fuel tank unit cam lock ring as shown.
  3. Muffler - Remove
  4. Front Floor Undercover - Remove
  5. Fuel Tank Heat Baffle - Remove (Under Fuel Tank)
  6. Fuel Vent Tube B - Disconnect (Mexico Models)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  7. Rear Wheel - Remove
  8. Left Rear Inner Fender - Remove
  9. Fuel Fill Door Adapter - Remove
  10. Connector (Adaptive Damper System) - Disconnect (If Equipped)
  11. Rear Brake Caliper Assembly, Rear Subframe, and Rear Trailing Arm - Remove

    NOTE: 

    • Do not disconnect the brake lines and the brake hoses.
    • Do not twist the brake hose excessively.

    1. Remove the rear subframe with the rear trailing arms, do the following procedures while removing the indicated part(s) from both sides.

    • The wheel speed sensor/electric parking brake harness bracket bolt (A) and the rear brake hose bracket bolt (B).
    • The rear upper arm bolt (C), the rear upper arm nut (D), and the rear wheel speed sensor bolt (E).
    • The rear brake caliper bracket bolts (F).

      NOTE:  To prevent damage to the rear brake caliper assembly (G), the rear brake hose, the electric parking brake harness, and the rear wheel speed sensor, use a short piece of wire to hang the rear brake caliper assembly from the undercarriage.

    • The rear damper upper bolts (H).
    • Disconnect the connector(s) (J).

      NOTE:  Pay attention to prevent damage to the connector.

    • Remove the bracket(s) (K).
    • The rear subframe bolts (L) and the rear trailing arm bolts (M).

      NOTE:  Place a transmission jack or other support under the rear subframe.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  12. Fuel Tank Assembly - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the fuel fill pipe bracket (A), the fuel fill pipe mounting bolt (B), and the ground cable terminal bolt (C).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Place a transmission jack or other support under the fuel tank (A).

    3. Remove the strap (B) and the fuel tank carefully.

    4. If needed, remove the fuel tank unit .
