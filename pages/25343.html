Removal and Installation

  1. Front Door Plastic Cover - Remove
  2. Front Door Glass - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Lower the front door glass (A) until you can see the bolts (B), then remove the bolts.

    2. Carefully pull out the front door glass through the window slot.
  3. Front Door Regulator - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Disconnect the connector (A).

    2. Remove the bolts (B).

    3. Release the hooks (C), then remove the front door regulator (D).
  4. All Removed Parts - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:  Apply multipurpose grease to all sliding surfaces of the front door regulator (A) where shown.
  5. Door Glass - Adjust
