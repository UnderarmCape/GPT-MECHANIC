DTC U0029-00 (Multipurpose Camera Unit) (2016 2017 2018)

DTC U0029-00  : F-CAN Malfunction (BUS-OFF)

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the how to troubleshooting information .
  • This DTC is stored by communication failure between the F-CAN line and the multipurpose camera unit. When DTC U0029-00 is indicated, the F-CAN line has already returned to normal. Check for loose terminals and poor connections at the connectors. If the connectors are OK, clear the DTC and following this troubleshooting.
