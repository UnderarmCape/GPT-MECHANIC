Removal and Installation: Procedure

  1. Ignition Coil - Remove
  2. Spark Plug - Remove
  3. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:  When installing the spark plugs, apply a small amount of anti-seize compound to the spark plug threads, and screw the spark plugs into the cylinder head, finger-tight, then tighten the spark plugs to the specified torque.
  4. Maintenance Minder - Reset (With Maintenance Minder System)
    1. If the Maintenance Minder required to replace the spark plugs, reset the Maintenance Minder with the gauge (see "Resetting the Maintenance Minder").
