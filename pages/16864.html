DTC B0095-4A, B0095-92

DTC B0095-4A, B0095-92  : Internal Failure of the Right Front Impact Sensor

NOTE: 

  • Before doing this troubleshooting procedure, find out if the vehicle was in a collision. If so, verify that all the required components were replaced with new components of the correct part number and were properly installed .
  • Before doing this troubleshooting procedure, review SRS Precautions and Procedures and General Troubleshooting Information .
DTC Description DTC
B0095-4A Internal failure of the right front impact sensor  
B0095-92 Internal failure of the right front impact sensor  

DTC (SRS)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Clear the DTCs with the HDS.

    Clear DTCs

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, then wait for 10 seconds.

    -3. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
    B0095-4A Internal failure of the right front impact sensor  
    B0095-92 Internal failure of the right front impact sensor  

    Is DTC B0095-4A or B0095-92 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Replace the right front impact sensor , then clear the DTC, and retest. If the DTC is still present, check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting or replace the SRS unit .

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Go to Troubleshooting Intermittent Failures. If another DTC is indicated, troubleshoot the DTC.
