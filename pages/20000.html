Engine Oil Replacement Reminder Reset - Procedure 11

Maintenance Minder messages begin appearing on the i-MID when engine oil life is less than 15%. The system message indicator comes on along with the Maintenance Minder message.

  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  2. Press the MENU button to go to VEHICLE INFORMATION. See Fig 1.
  3. Select VEHICLE INFORMATION with the PLUS (+) button, then press the SOURCE button.
  4. Press the SOURCE button again to go to the MAINTENANCE INFO screen. The engine oil life appears on the i-MID.
  5. Select YES with the MINUS (-) button, then press the SOURCE button. The displayed maintenance items disappear, and the engine oil life display returns to 100%. Any maintenance items that are necessary for this vehicle at the next maintenance service will appear.
  6. To cancel the oil life reset mode, select No, then press the SOURCE button.
