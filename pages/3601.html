Front Brake Pad Inspection (Type-R) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Inspection

NOTE: 

  • Review the Service Precautions before doing repairs or service .
  • Due to the high performance nature of the brake system, the brake disc and pads may wear faster than on the other models. Be sure to inspect the front brake disc thickness anytime the front pads are replaced.
  1. Vehicle - Lift
  2. Front Wheels - Remove
  3. Brake Pad - Inspect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Check the thickness (A) of the inner pad (B) and the outer pad (C). Do not include the thickness of the backing plate.

    2. If any part of the brake pad thickness is less than the service limit, replace the brake pads as a set .
    Brake pad thickness: 
      Standard:  10.0 mm (0.394 in) 
      Service limit:  2.0 mm (0.079 in)
