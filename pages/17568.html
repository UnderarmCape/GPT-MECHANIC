Countershaft Disassembly, Reassembly, and Inspection (Except K20C1 (M/T)): Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Countershaft - Inspect

    1. Inspect the oil passage, gear, and bearing contact areas for wear and damage, then measure the countershaft at points A, B, C, and D. If any part of the countershaft is less than the service limit, replace it.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
      Measurement Standard Service Limit
    A  Ball Bearing Contact Area (Transmission Housing Side)  30.002-30.015 mm (1.18118-1.18169 in)  30.002 mm (1.18118 in) 
    B  2nd Gear Distance Collar Contact Area  42.937-42.950 mm (1.69043-1.69094 in)  42.937 mm (1.69043 in) 
    C  Countershaft Reverse Gear Contact Area  42.975-42.991 mm (1.69193-1.69256 in)  42.975 mm (1.69193 in) 
    D  Needle Bearing Contact Area (Clutch Housing Side)  40.000-40.015 mm (1.57480-1.57539 in)  40.000 mm (1.57480 in) 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Inspect the runout by supporting both ends of the countershaft. Then rotate the countershaft two complete turns while measuring with a dial gauge. If the runout exceeds the standard, replace the countershaft.
    Standard:  0.02 mm (0.0008 in) max.
