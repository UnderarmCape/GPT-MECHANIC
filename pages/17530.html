Oil Pump Clearance Inspection (K20C1 (M/T)) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Oil Pump Clearance - Inspect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Measure the thrust clearance between the oil pump rotors (A) and the clutch housing (B) with a straight edge (C) and a feeler gauge (D).
    Standard (new): 
      0.03-0.13 mm (0.002-0.005 in) 
    Service Limit: 
      0.13 mm (0.005 in) 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Measure the side clearance between the oil pump outer rotor (A) and the clutch housing (B) with a feeler gauge (C).
    Standard (new): 
      0.1-0.2 mm (0.004-0.008 in) 
    Service Limit: 
      0.2 mm (0.008 in) 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 3. Measure the side clearance between the oil pump outer rotor (A) and the oil pump inner rotor (B) with a feeler gauge (C).

    4. If any clearance is not within the standard, replace the oil pump outer and inner rotors as a set.
    Standard (new): 
      0.1-0.4 mm (0.004-0.016 in) 
    Service Limit: 
      0.4 mm (0.016 in)
