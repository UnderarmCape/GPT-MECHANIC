M/T Differential Carrier Bearing Replacement (K20C1 (M/T)) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Replacement: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

NOTE: 

  • Where icon is shown, for further information see below.
  • The bearing outer race and the tapered roller bearing should be replaced as a set.
  • Whenever the tapered roller bearing is replaced, do the carrier bearing preload adjustment while reassembling the transmission .
