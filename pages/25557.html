Exterior Seal Removal and Installation: Installation

Front Bumper Seal 

  1. Front Bumper Seal - Install

    1. From under the front bumper (A), place a mark on the middle of the front bumper.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Clean the front bumper bonding surface with a sponge dampened with isopropyl alcohol. After cleaning, keep oil, grease, and water from getting on the surface.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    3. Install the front bumper seal (A) to the front bumper (B) from the mark placed in step 1 as shown.

    NOTE: 

    • Do not use an adhesive primer.
    • Do not run off the edges (C). If necessary, cut the excess front bumper seal.
    • After attaching the front bumper seal, press the front bumper seal firmly.
