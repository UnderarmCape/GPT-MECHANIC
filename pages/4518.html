Drive Belt Auto-Tensioner Air Bleeding (K20C2): Bleeding

  1. Drive Belt - Remove
  2. Drive Belt Auto-Tensioner - Air Bleed
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Attach a socket wrench to the drive belt auto-tensioner from above the engine. Slowly (at least 3 seconds) compress the auto-tensioner in the direction shown, the full length of its stroke, then slowly (at least 3 seconds) move the auto-tensioner the opposite direction the full length of its stroke. Repeat this operation three times.
  3. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
