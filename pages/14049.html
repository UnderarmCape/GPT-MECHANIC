M indicator does not come on even though the paddle shifter is operated in the S-paddle shift mode. (With paddle shifter) (K20C2 (CVT))

Probable cause(s)

  • F-CAN communication line error
  • Gauge control module defective
  • TCM defective

NOTES: 

  • Check the F-CAN communication line .
  • Check the F-CAN communication line by using the gauge control module self-diagnostic function .
