Transmission Housing Driven Pulley Shaft Thrust Clearance Adjustment (L15B7/L15BA/L15BY (CVT)): Adjustment

  1. Secondary Drive Gear - Install
  2. Driven Pulley Shaft Bearing (Transmission Housing Side) - Install
  3. Driven Pulley Shaft Thrust Clearance - Adjust
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Clean the cotter groove of the driven pulley shaft.

    2. Install the 25.5 mm cotters (A).

    3. Measure the clearance between the 25.5 mm cotters and the driven pulley shaft bearing (B) using a feeler gauge (C).

    NOTE:  Take measurements in at least three places, and use the average as the actual clearance.

    4. If the clearance is out of the standard, remove the 25.5 mm cotters, and measure its thickness.

    5. Select new 25.5 mm cotters.

    25.5 mm Cotters 

    6. Install the selected 25.5 mm cotters, then recheck the clearance.
    No.  Thickness 
    A 2.9 mm (0.114 in)
    B 3.0 mm (0.118 in)
    C 3.1 mm (0.122 in)
    D 3.2 mm (0.126 in)
    Standard:  0-0.13 mm (0-0.005 in)
