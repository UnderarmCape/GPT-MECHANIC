Automatic Lighting/Rain Sensor Removal and Installation (With Automatic Wiper): Removal

  1. Camera Cover - Remove
  2. Automatic Lighting/Rain Sensor - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Release the lock (A).

    2. Remove the automatic lighting/rain sensor (B) from the bracket (C).

    3. Disconnect the connector (D).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Remove the silicon sheet (A).
    NOTE: 
    • Do not reuse the silicon sheet.
    • If the silicon sheet is not on the back of the rain sensor after removal, it will remain on the glass surface. Make sure to peel it off prior to installation.
