Replace
  1. Engine Coolant - Drain
  2. A/C Condenser Fan Shroud Assembly and Radiator Fan Shroud Assembly - Remove 
  3. A/C Condenser Mounting Bolt - Remove
  4. Radiator - Remove
  5. Lower Cushion - Remove
  6. Drain Plug - Remove
  7. ECT Sensor 2 - Remove (if necessary)
  8. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
