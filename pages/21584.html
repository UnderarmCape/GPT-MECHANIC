DTC P0134 (K20C1) (2017 2018 2019)

DTC P0134:  Air/Fuel Ratio (A/F) Sensor (Sensor 1) Heater System Malfunction

General Description 

The powertrain control module (PCM) monitors the air/fuel ratio (A/F) sensor (sensor 1) heater control circuit for malfunctions. The diagnosis are divided into start, permanent, and low temperature diagnosis. If the A/F sensor (sensor 1) heater temperature is abnormal during any of the diagnosis, the PCM detects a malfunction and stores a DTC.

Malfunction Threshold 

Any of the conditions is met:

  • Start diagnosis

    The temperature of A/F sensor (sensor 1) heater ceramic does not reach 1, 327 deg.F (719.991 deg.C) after 35 to 87 seconds the start diagnosis is executed.

  • Permanent diagnosis

    The temperature of A/F sensor (sensor 1) heater ceramic is less than 1, 327 deg.F (719.991 deg.C) for at least 60 seconds.

  • Low temperature diagnosis

    The temperature of A/F sensor (sensor 1) heater ceramic is less than 1, 183 deg.F (639.998 deg.C) for at least 10 seconds.

Possible Cause 

NOTE:  The causes shown may not be a complete list of all potential problems, and it is possible that there may be other causes.

  • A/F sensor (sensor 1) heater failure
  • A/F sensor (sensor 1) element failure

Confirmation Procedure 

Operating Condition 

Start the engine. Hold the engine speed [Engine Speed] at 3, 000 rpm without load (in neutral) until the radiator fan comes on.

With the HDS 

None.

Diagnosis Details 

Conditions for setting the DTC 

When a malfunction is detected during the first drive cycle, a Pending DTC is stored in the PCM memory. If the malfunction returns in the next (second) drive cycle, the MIL comes on and a Confirmed DTC and the freeze data are stored.

Conditions for clearing the DTC 

The MIL is cleared if the malfunction does not return in three consecutive trips in which the diagnostic runs. The MIL, the Pending DTC, the Confirmed DTC, and the freeze data can be cleared with the scan tool Clear command.
