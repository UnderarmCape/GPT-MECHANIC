DTC P2127 (K20C1) (17-21)

DTC P2127  : APP Sensor B (TP Sensor E) Circuit Low Voltage

NOTE:  Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
P2127 APP Sensor B (TP Sensor E) Circuit Low Voltage    

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    Clear DTC

    -3. Check for Pending or Confirmed DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
    P2127 APP Sensor B (TP Sensor E) Circuit Low Voltage    

    Is DTC P2127 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the APP sensor and the PCM. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

  2. Determine possible failure area (VCC5 line, others)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    APP sensor 6P connector

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      APP sensor 6P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 APP sensor 6P connector (female terminals) No. 1:
    Test point 2 APP sensor 6P connector (female terminals) No. 2:
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there about 5.0 V?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Go to step 6.

  3. Shorted wire check (APS2 line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    SCS Short

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector No. 2 (58P)

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      APP sensor 6P connector: disconnected
      PCM connector No. 2 (58P): disconnected
    Test point 1 APP sensor 6P connector (female terminals) No. 3:
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Repair a short in the APS2 wire between PCM connector No. 2 terminal No. 45 and the APP sensor.

    NO 

    The APS2 wire is not shorted. Go to step 4.

  4. Open wire check (APS2 line)

    -1. Connect terminals A and B with a jumper wire.

    Terminal A APP sensor 6P connector (female terminals) No. 3:
    Terminal B Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    -2. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      APP sensor 6P connector: disconnected
      PCM connector No. 2 (58P): disconnected
      APP sensor 6P connector No. 3: jumped to body ground
    Test point 1 PCM connector No. 2 (58P) No. 45
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The APS2 wire is OK. Go to step 5.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the APS2 wire between PCM connector No. 2 terminal No. 45 and the APP sensor.

  5. PCM internal circuit check

    -1. Remove the jumper wire from the APP sensor 6P connector.

    -2. Reconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector No. 2 (58P)

    -3. Exit the SCS mode with the HDS.

    SCS Open

    -4. Connect terminals A and B with a jumper wire.

    Terminal A APP sensor 6P connector (female terminals) No. 1:
    Terminal B APP sensor 6P connector (female terminals) No. 3:
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    -5. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -6. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    Clear DTC

    -7. Check for Pending or Confirmed DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
    P2127 APP Sensor B (TP Sensor E) Circuit Low Voltage    

    Is DTC P2127 indicated?

    YES 

    Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P2127 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Replace the accelerator pedal module .

  6. Open wire check (VCC5 line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    SCS Short

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector No. 2 (58P)

    -4. Connect terminals A and B with a jumper wire.

    Terminal A APP sensor 6P connector (female terminals) No. 1:
    Terminal B Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    -5. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      APP sensor 6P connector: disconnected
      PCM connector No. 2 (58P): disconnected
      APP sensor 6P connector No. 1: jumped to body ground
    Test point 1 PCM connector No. 2 (58P) No. 46
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The VCC5 wire is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P2127 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the VCC5 wire between PCM connector No. 2 terminal No. 46 and the APP sensor.
