Grab Handle Removal and Installation: Removal

  1. Grab Handle - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Lower the grab handle (A).

    2. Insert the tips of the push pin/grab rail cap pliers (B) into the notch.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 3. Gently squeeze the handles of the push pin/grab rail cap pliers (A), and pull the cap (B) straight out.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Lower the grab handle (A).

    5. Squeeze the clips (B) with pliers, then remove the grab handle.
