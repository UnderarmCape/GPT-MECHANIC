Millimeter Wave Radar Aiming (2019 2020 2021): Adjustment

NOTE: 

  • The millimeter wave radar must be re-aimed if the millimeter wave radar is removed or replaced. The ACC indicator comes on if the aiming process is not completed correctly:
    • -

      The millimeter wave radar has a self-diagnosis function and if the mounting angle of the millimeter wave radar is outside of the horizontal auto-aiming range, it sends the failure information to the multipurpose camera unit.

  • If DTCs are indicated, do the indicated DTCs first. If the DTCs are not repaired, you cannot complete the millimeter wave radar aiming procedure. However, if you have the following DTC, proceed with the aiming procedure.
    • -

      Millimeter Wave Radar Aiming Incomplete: DTC P2583-54

  • If the vehicle you are aiming was involved in a collision or if you suspect the alignment is out of specifications, you must perform a wheel alignment prior to aiming the millimeter wave radar.
  • Conditions for the aiming environment:
    • -

      Aiming should be on level ground in a wide open space to avoid errors caused by reflecting objects.

    • -

      Amount of space require in front of the vehicle:

      10 m (33 ft) measured from the front bumper

      5 m (16 ft) in width

      3 m (10 ft) in height, measured from the floor

  • The vehicle must be parked on a horizontal floor in order to adjust the vertical radar aiming correctly. Check the levelness of the floor surface, but if there is a continuous slope, then adjust the angle of the millimeter wave radar so it is parallel to the floor surface. Lay a ruler or other flat straight object on the floor surface and put the level on top to confirm the levelness of the floor surface.
  • When marking on the floor surface, use suitable tape to mark the exact locations.

Prepare these items:

  • HDS
  • Tape measure and ruler
  • Felt-tip pen
  • Tape
  • String
  • Plumb bob

  2. Millimeter Wave Radar - Aim
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Connect the HDS .

    NOTE:  Keep the aiming target away from the vehicle until you are instructed in a later step.

    2. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    NOTE:  Troubleshoot any DTCs first before proceeding. If DTC P2583-54 is indicated, proceed with the aiming procedure.

    3. In the DRIVING SUPPORT menu, select INTEGRATED DRIVER SUPPORT system, then ADJUSTMENT, then select Radar Aiming.

    4. Press ENTER on the HDS to enter the aiming mode.

    NOTE:  The ACC, LKAS, and RDM indicators (amber) blink when aiming mode is activated.

    5. The MID will display NO TARGET.

    6. Make sure the MID indicates NO TARGET on the display.

    NOTE:  If NO TARGET is not indicated, the stand set is placed in front of the vehicle or there is an unwanted radar reflection in front of the vehicle in the range of the radar. Make sure the stand set is set aside away from the range of the radar until you are instructed to place it in front of the vehicle. If you are still getting an unwanted radar reflection, stack tires about 1.5 m (4.9 ft) in height in front of either the immovable object, such as a pole, or in front of any object causing the unwanted reflections. The tires will block the radar reflections. Make sure NO TARGET appears on the MID. If it still does not appear, move the vehicle to a different location until NO TARGET appears on the MID.

    7. Place the stand set on the point (E).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    8. Press ENTER on the HDS to calculate the alignment angles.

    9. Check the vertical and horizontal alignment angles on the HDS display. If the angles displayed on the HDS are out of the specified angle range, turn the adjustment bolt using the 3.5 mm hex socket driver (A) as necessary (counterclockwise or clockwise) to bring the displayed angle to the specified angle range. Watch the HDS display while performing the adjustment as the values will change.

    Specified Angle: 

    Millimeter wave angle Specified angle range
    Horizontal 0 0.1 deg.
    Vertical 0 0.1 deg.

    Adjustment Method: 

    Angle shown on the HDS display Rotate the 3.5 mm hex socket driver as follows
    More than +0.1 deg. Counterclockwise
    Less than -0.1 deg. Clockwise
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    NOTE: 

    • The values on the HDS display will fluctuate as the adjustment is being performed. Wait until the values stabilize to confirm the adjustment.
    • When checking the angle on the HDS screen and recording the calculation angle, do not enter the zone where it will affect the radar angle calculation.
    • A value of 12.8 degrees on the HDS display indicates that the millimeter wave radar is being obstructed and cannot receive the radar wave.
    • If the value of 12.8 degrees remains on the HDS screen for 15 seconds continuously, the aiming will stop.
    • If the specified angle cannot be obtained with the adjustment bolts, confirm the millimeter wave radar mounting position.

    NOTE:  If the failure code and the message in the following list are displayed, solve the problem, and then perform the millimeter wave radar aiming again.

    Failure codes and messages: 

    Failure code (on the MID) Failure message (on the HDS) How to solve
    120 Multiple Aiming Target Detected Remove all reflective objects except the correct special tools.
    122 The Distance between Radar and Aiming Target is Out of Specification Set the specified distance to the aiming target.
    124 Detected Some Obstacles Remove all obstacles.
    125 There is no Aiming Target Set the aiming target at the correct position.
    129 A Vehicle Speed is not 0 km/h Stop the vehicle completely.
    132 Internal Software Error Update the millimeter wave radar .
    134 High Temperature Error Cool down the millimeter wave radar to the proper operating temperature.
    135 Internal Hardware Error Replace the millimeter wave radar .

    10. When the angle is at the specified value, press ENTER to record the angle.

    NOTE:  The aiming information obtained is stored in the millimeter wave radar.

    11. Confirm THE RADAR AIMING HAS BEEN COMPLETED on the HDS.

    12. Press ENTER on the HDS, and turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    13. Reinstall the radar cover.

    NOTE:  If the radar cover is damaged or cracked, replace it.
