DTC P003A (K20C1) (2019)

DTC P003A:  Turbocharger Wastegate Control Actuator Position Exceeded Learning Limit

General Description 

The closed mechanical stop position of the turbocharger wastegate control actuator is learned by pressing the turbocharger wastegate control actuator with a constant duty cycle against the closed mechanical stop. A range check of the mechanical stop voltage of the turbocharger wastegate control actuator position sensor is performed at first initialization and during the driving cycle. If the turbocharger wastegate control actuator position is abnormal, the powertrain control module (PCM) detects a malfunction and stores a DTC.

Monitor Execution, Sequence, Duration, DTC Type 

Execution Once per driving cycle
Sequence None
Duration 0.5 second or more
DTC Type One drive cycle, MIL on

Enable Conditions 

Condition Minimum Maximum
Engine off time 6 hours -
12 volt battery voltage [Battery] 10 V -
Vehicle ON mode
Other Not to start the engine

[ ]: HDS Parameter

Malfunction Threshold 

Any of the conditions occurs:

  • The actual learned turbocharger wastegate control actuator position sensor voltage at mechanical stop at first PCM initialization is less than 0.59 V or higher than 2.56 V.
  • The actual learning step 1 is timed out.
  • The actual turbocharger wastegate control actuator position is more than -98.5 - 101.5 % for at least 2.5 seconds.
  • The actual learned turbocharger wastegate control actuator position sensor voltage at lower mechanical stop at actual learning step 3 is higher than 2.56 V.
  • The actual learned turbocharger wastegate control actuator position sensor voltage at lower mechanical stop at actual offset learning step 1 is less than 0.59 V.

Possible Cause 

NOTE:  The causes shown may not be a complete list of all potential problems, and it is possible that there may be other causes.

  • Turbocharger wastegate control actuator position sensor failure
  • Turbocharger wastegate control actuator stuck
  • PCM internal circuit failure

Confirmation Procedure 

Operating Condition 

  • Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.
  • Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode, and leave the vehicle for at least 6 hours.
  • Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

With the HDS 

  • 1. Start the engine. Hold the engine speed [Engine Speed] at 3, 000 rpm without load (in neutral) until the radiator fan comes on.
  • Select the Electric Waste Gate in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.

Diagnosis Details 

Conditions for setting the DTC 

When a malfunction is detected, the MIL comes on and a Pending DTC, a Confirmed DTC, and the freeze data are stored in the PCM memory.

Conditions for clearing the DTC 

The MIL is cleared if the malfunction does not return in three consecutive trips in which the diagnostic runs. The MIL, the Pending DTC, the Confirmed DTC, and the freeze data can be cleared with the scan tool Clear command.
