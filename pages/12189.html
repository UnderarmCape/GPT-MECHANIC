Adaptive Damper System Description - Components (Type-R) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021)

Damper Unit 

The adaptive damper passes the electric current that has been adjusted by the adaptive damper control unit, to the coil to operate the valves. The solenoid valves open depending on the amount of electric current. Since the amount of oil changes depending on the driving condition, an optimal damping force can be generated.

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

Acceleration Sensor 

The acceleration sensor detects acceleration value of vertical direction on each position.

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

Suspension Stroke Sensor 

Each suspension stroke sensors are located on the suspension and detects the stroke amount.
