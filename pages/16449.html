Millimeter Wave Radar Mounting Position Inspection (5-door) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Inspection

NOTE:  If millimeter wave radar aiming cannot be completed, follow the procedure below to check that the millimeter wave radar is installed properly and the radar bracket is not deformed. Based on the check results, replace faulty parts. After that, perform millimeter wave radar aiming again.

  1. Front Bumper - Remove
  2. Millimeter Wave Radar Mounting Position - Inspect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 NOTE: 
    • When the millimeter wave radar is adjusted on a floor surface that has more than one degree of horizontal slope (drainage slope), the vertical radar aiming cannot be adjusted correctly. Check the levelness of the floor surface, but if there is a continuous slope, then adjust the angle of millimeter wave radar so it is parallel to the floor surface. Lay a ruler or other flat straight object on the floor surface and put the level on top to confirm the levelness of the floor surface.
    • Make sure the steering wheel is pointed straight ahead, and do not turn it after setting up the vehicle.

    1. Put the level on the millimeter wave radar.

    NOTE: 

    • Use the level included in the alignment set.
    • To correctly confirm the bubble position in the level, have a look from the side.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Confirm if the bubble in the level is between the lines.

    NOTE:  If the bubble is not within the lines, adjust the mounting position using the vertical adjustment bolt (A).

    3. Remove the level.
