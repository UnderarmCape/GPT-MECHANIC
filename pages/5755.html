VTC Oil Control Solenoid Valve Removal, Installation, and Test (L15B7/L15BA/L15BY): Test

  1. VTC Oil Control Solenoid Valve - Test
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Check for any blockage in the ports of the VTC oil control solenoid valve.

    2. Connect VTC oil control solenoid valve 2P connector terminal No. 1 to the 12 volt battery positive terminal with a jumper wire. Connect VTC oil control solenoid valve 2P connector terminal No. 2 to the 12 volt battery negative terminal with a jumper wire.

    3. It is normal to a click sound by the valve function when turning on electricity. If the no sound heard, replace the VTC oil control solenoid valve because of a functional malfunction.
