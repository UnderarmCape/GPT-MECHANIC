LaneWatch System Description - System Diagram

LaneWatch Camera 

LaneWatch may reduce the passenger's side blind spot by displaying the LaneWatch camera image mounted under the passenger's side power mirror. The image is displayed on the center display unit when the turn signal is activated to the passenger's side or the LaneWatch switch is pressed.

The LaneWatch obtains information from the external sensors and the switches that are connected to their respective control units.
