A/C REFRIGERANT TYPES GUIDE

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): AJA09914, Date of Issue:  August 24, 2018
HONDA: All Models

AFFECTED VEHICLES

American Honda sells two different types of refrigerant for A/C systems. Be careful with what type of refrigerant is put in the vehicles. This guide shows you which refrigerant you need for servicing A/C systems in 2000 and later Honda vehicles.
