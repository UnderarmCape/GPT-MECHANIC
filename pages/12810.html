  4. Back-Up Light Switch - Remove
  5. Neutral Position Sensor - Remove
  6. Input Shaft (Mainshaft) Speed Sensor - Remove
  7. Output Shaft (Countershaft) Speed Sensor - Remove
  8. Detent Bolt - Remove

  22. Transmission Case Cap - Remove
  23. 10 mm Flange Bolt - Loosen
  24. Transmission Hanger B - Remove
  25. Clutch Pipe Bracket - Remove
  26. Transmission Housing - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Expand the 75 mm snap ring (A) on the countershaft ball bearing, and remove it from the groove using the snap ring pliers.

    2. Remove the transmission housing (B) and the 14 x 20 mm dowel pins (C).
  27. 87 mm Shim - Remove
  28. 8 mm Special Bolt - Remove
  29. Spring Washer - Remove
  30. Shift Arm - Remove
  31. Change Lever - Remove
  32. 16 x 23 x 5 mm Dust Seal - Remove
  33. 75 mm Snap Ring - Remove
  34. 42 x 58 x 12.5 mm Oil Seal - Remove (Transmission Housing Side)
  35. 50 x 81 x 18 mm Bearing Outer Race, 46 x 81 x 1 mm Spacer and 81 mm Shim - Remove (Transmission Housing Side)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the 50 x 81 x 18 mm bearing outer race (A), the 46 x 81 x 1 mm spacer (B), and the 81 mm shim (C) by heating the transmission housing to about 212°F (100°C) with a heat gun.
  36. Oil Guide Pipe A - Remove
  37. Oil Guide Pipe B - Remove

  61. Interlock - Remove
  62. Return Spring - Remove
  63. 5 x 22 mm Spring Pin and 3 x 22 mm Spring Pin - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the 5 x 22 mm spring pin and the 3 x 22 mm spring pin together as shown.
  64. Select Lever and Select Arm - Remove
  65. 14 x 20 x 4 mm Dust Seal - Remove
  66. Mainshaft, Countershaft, and Shift Fork Assembly - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Apply a thin layer of tape to the mainshaft splines to protect the oil seal.

    NOTE:  Do not apply too much tape as it will damage the oil seal.

    2. Remove the mainshaft assembly (A) and the countershaft assembly (B) with the shift fork assembly (C).
  67. Spring Washer - Remove
  68. 28 x 43 x 7 mm Oil Seal - Remove
  69. Oil Pump Shaft - Remove
  70. Differential Assembly - Remove
  71. Oil Gutter Plate - Remove
  72. Bearing Set Plate - Remove
  73. Countershaft Bearing - Remove (Clutch Housing Side)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the countershaft bearing using the 45-75 mm adjustable bearing puller, the bearing remover shaft, and the remover weight.
  74. Oil Pump Plate - Remove
  75. Oil Pump Inner Rotor - Remove
  76. Oil Pump Outer Rotor - Remove
  77. 16.1 mm Detent Ball Spring - Remove
  78. Steel Ball - Remove
  79. 37 x 56 x 8 mm Oil Seal - Remove (Clutch Housing Side)
  80. 45 x 80 x 21 mm Bearing Outer Race and 46 x 80 x 1 mm Spacer - Remove (Clutch Housing Side)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the 45 x 80 x 21 mm bearing outer race (A) and the 46 x 80 x 1 mm spacer (B) by heating the clutch housing to about 212°F (100°C) with a heat gun.
