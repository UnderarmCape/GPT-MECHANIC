VSA System Description - Brake Assist Control

Overview 

The brake assist control shortens the braking distance by making increasing applied brake force by the driver during emergency braking.

Control 

VSA modulator-control unit constantly monitors brake operation through the pressure sensor. If the pedal stroke speed and pedal stroke amount exceed the threshold, it is considered to be emergency braking. When emergency braking is determined to be taking place, the VSA modulator-control unit increases braking force compared to normal braking and shortens the braking distance.
