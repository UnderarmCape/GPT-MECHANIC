VSA Modulator-Control Unit Removal and Installation (5-door) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Removal

NOTE: 

  • Review the Service Precautions before doing repairs or service .
  • Do not damage or drop the VSA modulator-control unit as it is sensitive.
  • Do not use power tools when removing the VSA modulator-control unit.
  • When replacing the VSA modulator-control unit, do the immobilizer system registration.
  1. 12 Volt Battery Terminal - Disconnect
  2. Connector Bracket - Remove (2.0 L Engine)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the connectors (A) from the bracket (B).

    2. Remove the clip (C).

    3. Remove the bracket.
  3. VSA Modulator-Control Unit - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Disconnect the 10 mm brake lines (A).

    2. Disconnect the 12 mm brake lines (B).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 3. Disconnect the connector (A) by pushing the lock (B) and pulling up the lever (C); the connector disconnects itself.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Remove the VSA modulator-control unit (A) with the bracket (B).
  4. VSA Modulator-Control Unit Bracket - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the modulator bracket (A) from the modulator sub bracket (B).

    2. Remove the modulator sub bracket from the VSA modulator-control unit (C).
