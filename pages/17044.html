Fuel Pressure Relieving: Procedure

WARNING: 

Flammable - Keep Fire Away

NOTE: 

  • Refer to the Fuel and Emissions Systems Service Precautions  before disconnecting the fuel line.
  • Before disconnecting fuel lines or hoses, relieve pressure from the system by disabling the fuel pump and disconnecting the fuel line/quick connect fitting in the engine compartment.
  1. Fuel Tank Pressure - Relieve
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Insert the emergency fuel funnel (A) into the fuel filler neck to relieve the pressure in the fuel tank.

    NOTE:  The emergency fuel funnel is stored in the trunk tool box.
  2. Fuel Pressure - Relieve
    1. Remove PGM-FI main relay 2 .

    2. Start the engine, and let it idle until it stalls.

    NOTE:  If any DTCs are stored, clear and ignore them.

    3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    4. Install PGM-FI main relay 2.
  3. 12 volt Battery Terminal - Disconnect
