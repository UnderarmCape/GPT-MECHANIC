Brake system indicator (amber) does not go off

Diagnostic Procedure

  • With multipurpose camera unit: Check for collision mitigation braking system (CMBS) DTCs .
  • Check for VSA System DTCs .
  • With multipurpose camera unit: Check for collision mitigation braking system (CMBS) Symptom Troubleshooting (CMBS indicator does not go off) .
  • Check for VSA System Symptom Troubleshooting (ABS indicator, brake system indicator (red), VSA indicator, and brake system indicator (amber) do not go off) .
  • Do the gauge control module self-diagnostic function .
