Removal and Installation: Procedure

NOTE:

Numbered call-outs in figure pertain to list numbers in following procedure.

Fig 1: Exploded View Of Throttle Body Components With Torque Specifications (K20C1 - 2017-21)
Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
Detailed information, notes and precautions
Torque: N.m (kgf.m, lbf.ft)
Replace
  1. Intercooler Pipe Bracket - Remove 
  2. 12 Volt Battery Base - Remove
  3. Throttle Body Inlet Pipe and Throttle Body Connector Tube - Remove 
  4. Connector (Throttle Body) - Disconnect
  5. Harness Bracket - Remove
  6. Water Bypass Hose - Disconnect and Plug
  7. Throttle Body and Throttle Flange Plate - Remove

    NOTE:  Be careful not to drop the throttle flange plates to prevent other parts from damage.

    Note for installation 

    • When tighten the throttle body mounting bolts in a cross pattern in three steps;
      • Tighten the bolts until they sit on the throttle body.
      • Tighten the bolts until the gasket is compressed and both parts are contacted.
      • Tighten the bolts to specified torque.
  8. Throttle Body Cover - Remove (If Necessary)
    Fig 2: Throttle Body Cover And Mounting Screws With Tightening Sequence (K20C1 - 2017-21)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Note for installation 

    Tighten the mounting screws to the specified torque in the numbered sequence shown.

  9. Throttle Body Lower Case - Remove (If Necessary)
  10. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
  11. Engine Coolant - Refill
  12. Throttle Position - Learn
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Do the throttle position learn procedure.
