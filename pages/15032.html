DTC P0201, P0204 (L15BA) (17-21)

DTC P0201  : No. 1 Cylinder Injector Circuit Malfunction

DTC P0204  : No. 4 Cylinder Injector Circuit Malfunction

NOTE:  Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
P0201 No. 1 Cylinder Injector Circuit Malfunction    
P0204 No. 4 Cylinder Injector Circuit Malfunction    

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Start the engine (If the engine does not start, crank the engine for 20 seconds).

    -4. Check for Pending or Confirmed DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
    P0201 No. 1 Cylinder Injector Circuit Malfunction    
    P0204 No. 4 Cylinder Injector Circuit Malfunction    

    Is DTC P0201 and/or P0204 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the injector and the PCM. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

  2. Engine starting check

    -1. Try to start the engine.

    Does the engine start?

    YES 

    Go to step 9.

    NO 

    Go to step 3.

  3. Relay check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Remove the injector relay .

    -3. Test the injector relay .

    Is the relay OK?

    YES 

    The relay is OK. Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Replace the injector relay.

  4. Open wire check (+B INJ line)

    -1. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    Injector relay: disconnected
    Test point 1 Injector relay 4P socket (female terminals) No. 3 and No. 7:
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    The +B INJ wire is OK. Go to step 6.

    NO 

    Go to step 5.

  5. Fuse check

    -1. Check the following fuse.

    Fuse No. A10 (15 A)
    Location Under-hood fuse/relay box

    Is the fuse OK?

    YES 

    Repair an open in the +B INJ wire between the No. A10 (15 A) fuse and the injector relay.

    NO 

    Repair a short in the +B INJ wire between the injector relay and the No. A10 (15 A) fuse. Also replace the No. A10 (15 A) fuse.

  6. Determine possible failure area (FI MAIN RLY CL- line, others)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to ON mode.

    -2. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
    Injector relay: disconnected
    Test point 1 Injector relay 4P socket (female terminals) No. 7:
    Test point 2 Injector relay 4P socket (female terminals) No. 8:
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    Go to step 8.

    NO 

    Go to step 7.

  7. Open wire check (FI MAIN RLY CL- line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector A (50P)

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    Injector relay: disconnected
    PCM connector A (50P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Injector relay 4P socket (female terminals) No. 8:
    Test point 2 PCM connector A (50P) No. 5
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The FI MAIN RLY CL- wire is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P0201 and/or P0204 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the FI MAIN RLY CL- wire between the PCM (A5) and the injector relay.

  8. Open wire check (INJ RLY OUT line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Connect terminals A and B with a jumper wire.

    Terminal A Injector relay 4P socket (female terminals) No. 3:
    Terminal B Injector relay 4P socket (female terminals) No. 4:
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    -3. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -4. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector A (50P)

    -5. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    Injector relay: disconnected
    Injector relay 4P socket No. 3 and No. 4: jumped
    PCM connector A (50P): disconnected
    Test point 1 PCM connector A (50P) No. 1 and No. 2
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    The INJ RLY OUT wire is OK. Go to step 9.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the INJ RLY OUT wire between the PCM (A1, A2) and the injector relay.

  9. DTC check

    -1. Check the indicated DTC in step 1.

    Is DTC P0201 indicated?

    YES 

    Go to step 10.

    NO 

    Go to step 18.

    Test point 1 PCM connector E (80P) No. 3
    Test point 2 PCM connector E (80P) No. 15
