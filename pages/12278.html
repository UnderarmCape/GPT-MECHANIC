TPMS Symptom Troubleshooting Index (TPMS)

Symptom Diagnostic procedure Also check for
HDS does not communicate with the VSA modulator-control unit or the vehicle Troubleshoot the DLC circuit .  
Without TPMS switch: Deflation warning system cannot be Initialized
  • Check for VSA system DTCs .
  • Check for the VSA indicator. If VSA indicator turns on, do the VSA system symptom troubleshooting "ABS indicator, brake system indicator, and VSA indicator do not go off" .
  • Do the gauge control module self-diagnostic function .
  • If the VSA modulator-control unit the symptom is still present, replace the VSA modulator-control unit .
  
With TPMS switch: TPMS cannot be calibrated
  • Symptom troubleshooting .
  • If the symptom is still present, replace the VSA modulator-control unit .
  
TPMS warning is indicated
  • Check for VSA system DTCs .
  • Check for the VSA indicator. If VSA indicator turns on, do the VSA system symptom troubleshooting "ABS indicator, brake system indicator (red), brake system indicator (amber), and VSA indicator do not go off" .
  • If the VSA modulator-control unit the symptom is still present, replace the VSA modulator-control unit .
  
Low tire pressure/TPMS indicator comes on
  • Check the tire size and tire pressure according to tire information.
  • Do the TPMS calibration .
  
Low tire pressure/TPMS indicator does not come on when the tire pressure has decreased
  • See general troubleshooting information "condition for data rejection, and case TPMS cannot operate normally" (Even if a system is normal, there is a possibility that cannot detect low tire pressure) .
  • Do the gauge control module self-diagnostic function .
  • Do the TPMS calibration .
  • If the VSA modulator-control unit the symptom is still present, replace the VSA modulator-control unit .
