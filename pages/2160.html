Navigation System Symptom Troubleshooting - Rearview camera image does not come on

NOTE: 

  • Check the vehicle 12 volt battery condition first .
  • Check the connectors for poor connections or loose terminals.
  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Shift the transmission to R position/mode.

    Does the center display image change to rearview when the transmission is shifted to R position/mode?

    YES 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.

    NO 

    The failure is duplicated, go to step 2.

  2. System Diagnostic Mode check

    -1. Shift the transmission to Neutral or P position/mode.

    -2. Go into the System Diagnostic Mode, and use the System Links to check the Rear Wide Camera icon status .

    Is the Rear Camera icon green?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Go to navigation DTC 2608 (R-Camera Diag) troubleshooting .

  3. Determine possible failure area (BACK LT line, others)

    -1. Go into the System Diagnostic Mode, and use the Vehicle Status to check the BACK signal .

    -2. Shift the transmission to R position/mode.

    Does the BACK signal change from [0] to [1] when the transmission is shifted from P position/mode to R position/mode?

    YES 

    Go to step 5.

    NO 

    Go to step 4.

  4. Open wire check (BACK LT line)

    -1. Shift the transmission to Neutral or P position/mode.

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    Audio-navigation unit connector C (32P)

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -5. Shift the transmission to R position/mode.

    -6. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      Audio-navigation unit connector C (32P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio-navigation unit connector C (32P) No. 15
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    Replace the audio-navigation unit .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the wire between the audio-navigation unit and the under-dash fuse/relay box (CVT) or the back-up light switch (M/T).

  5. Shorted wire check (CAMERA VCC, CAMERA GND, CAMERA V lines)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connectors.

    Audio-navigation unit connector C (32P)
    Rearview camera 8P connector

    -3. Check for continuity between the following test points and body ground individually.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio-navigation unit connector C (32P): disconnected
      Rearview camera 8P connector: disconnected
    Connector Terminal
    Audio-navigation unit connector C (32P) No. 13
    No. 14
    No. 29

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    There is a short to body ground in the wire(s) between the audio-navigation unit and the rearview camera. Replace the affected shielded harness.

    NO 

    The CAMERA VCC, CAMERA GND, and CAMERA V wires are not shorted to ground. Go to step 6.

  6. Shorted wire check (CAMERA lines)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio-navigation unit connector C (32P): disconnected
      Rearview camera 8P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1:Audio-navigation unit connector C (32P) Test point 2:Audio-navigation unit connector C (32P)
    No. 13 No. 14, No. 28, No. 29
    No. 14 No. 28, No. 29
    No. 28 No. 29

    Is there continuity between any of the terminals?

    YES 

    There is a short in the wires between the audio-navigation unit and the rearview camera. Replace the affected shielded harness.

    NO 

    The CAMERA wires are not shorted in the wires. Go to step 7.

  7. Open wire check (CAMERA VCC, CAMERA GND, CAMERA V lines)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio-navigation unit connector C (32P): disconnected
      Rearview camera 8P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1:Audio-navigation unit connector C (32P) Test point 2:Rearview camera 8P connector
    No. 13 No. 2
    No. 14 No. 8
    No. 29 No. 7

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The CAMERA VCC, CAMERA GND, and CAMERA V wires are OK. Go to step 8.

    NO 

    There is an open in the wire(s) between the audio-navigation unit and the rearview camera. Replace the affected shielded harness.

  8. Voltage check (CAMERA VCC line)

    -1. Reconnect audio-navigation unit connector C (32P).

    -2. Reconnect the rearview camera 8P connector.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
    Test point 1 Audio-navigation unit connector C (32P) No. 13
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there about 8.0 V?

    YES 

    Go to step 9.

    NO 

    Replace the audio-navigation unit .

  9. Rearview camera check (substitution)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Substitute a known-good rearview camera , and recheck.

    Does the symptom go away?

    YES 

    Replace the original rearview camera.

    NO 

    Replace the audio-navigation unit .
