DTC C1040-00 (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021)

DTC C1040-00  : Wheel Speed Signal Error

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • This DTC is recorded when the estimated yaw rate calculated based on the wheel speeds of the right and left wheels differs between the front and rear wheels.
  • The specific conditions under which this DTC are as follows:
    • -

      Abnormal tire diameter (low tire pressure, uneven wear, etc.)

    • -

      Driving on a road having different road conditions on the right and left sides

  • If this DTC is recorded, the integrated driver support system temporarily cancels its control to prevent the system from selecting an incorrect leading vehicle.
  • When the difference in the estimated yaw rate between the front and rear wheels returns to normal, the integrated driver support system resumes its control.
  • The functions of the integrated driver support system that will be temporarily canceled when this DTC is recorded can be confirmed with the DTC troubleshooting index .
DTC Description DTC
C1040-00 Wheel Speed Signal Error  

DTC (IDAS)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK).

    -4. Test-drive the vehicle on a straight road at speeds 25 mph (40 km/h) or more for 10 minutes or more.

    -5. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
    C1040-00 Wheel Speed Signal Error  

    Is DTC C1040-00 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Check the tire pressure and inspect for uneven wear.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting .
