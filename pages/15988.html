Side Sill Panel Removal and Installation (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Installation

Side Sill Panel (Except Type-R) 

  1. Side Sill Panel - Install
    2-door 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    4/5-door 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the side sill panel lip seal (A).
    NOTE:  If you are reusing the side sill panel lip seal, apply the adhesive primer to the area where the new double-sided adhesive tape will be applied, then attach the new double-sided adhesive tape to the side sill panel lip seal.

    Adhesive tape requirements 

    2-door: Thickness 0.4 mm (0.016 in)
      Width 6 mm (0.24 in)
      Length 518 mm (20.39 in)
    4/5-door: Thickness 0.4 mm (0.016 in)
      Width 4 mm (0.16 in)
      Length 147 mm (5.79 in)

    2. Hold up the side sill panel (A), and fit the clips (B) into the holes in the body, then push on the side sill panel until the clips snap into place.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    3. Install the clips (A) and the screws (B).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
