DTC U0056-00 (K20C1: With CAN gateway) (2020 2021)

DTC U0056-00  : CAN Gateway F-CAN ch C Bus Off

NOTE:  Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description DTC
U0056-00 CAN Gateway F-CAN ch C Bus Off  

DTC (CAN gateway)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, and wait 6 seconds.

    -5. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
    U0056-00 CAN Gateway F-CAN ch C Bus Off  

    Is DTC U0056-00 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

  2. Shorted unit check (gauge control module)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    Gauge control module connector A (32P)

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -5. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -6. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, and wait 6 seconds.

    -7. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
    U0056-00 CAN Gateway F-CAN ch C Bus Off  

    Is DTC U0056-00 indicated?

    YES (without navigation)

    Go to step 3.

    YES (with navigation)

    Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good gauge control module , then recheck. If DTC U0056-00 goes away and the gauge control module was substituted, replace the original gauge control module .

    -5. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -6. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, and wait 6 seconds.

    -7. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
    U0056-00 CAN Gateway F-CAN ch C Bus Off  

    Is DTC U0056-00 indicated?

    YES 

    Go to step 5.

    NO 

    Substitute a known-good audio unit , then recheck. If DTC U0056-00 goes away and the audio unit was substituted, replace the original audio unit .

  6. Shorted wire check (F-CAN C_H line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    CAN gateway 16P connector

    -3. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    Gauge control module connector A (32P): disconnected
    Audio unit connector E (16P) (without navigation): disconnected
    Audio-navigation unit connector E (16P) (with navigation): disconnected
    Active sound control unit 20P connector: disconnected
    CAN gateway 16P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 CAN gateway 16P connector (female terminals) No. 5:
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there 1 MΩ or more?

    YES 

    The F-CAN C_H wire is not shorted to ground. Go to step 7.

    NO 

    Repair a short in the F-CAN C_H wire between the CAN gateway and the connected units.

  7. Shorted wire check (F-CAN C_L line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    Gauge control module connector A (32P): disconnected
    Audio unit connector E (16P) (without navigation): disconnected
    Audio-navigation unit connector E (16P) (with navigation): disconnected
    Active sound control unit 20P connector: disconnected
    CAN gateway 16P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 CAN gateway 16P connector (female terminals) No. 13:
    Test point 2 Body ground

  8. Shorted wire check (F-CAN C_H line to F-CAN C_L line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    Gauge control module connector A (32P): disconnected
    Audio unit connector E (16P) (without navigation): disconnected
    Audio-navigation unit connector E (16P) (with navigation): disconnected
    Active sound control unit 20P connector: disconnected
    CAN gateway 16P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 CAN gateway 16P connector (female terminals) No. 5:
    Test point 2 CAN gateway 16P connector (female terminals) No. 13:
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there 1 MΩ or more?

    YES 

    Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good CAN gateway , then recheck. If DTC U0056-00 goes away and the CAN gateway was substituted, replace the original CAN gateway .

    NO 

    Repair a short in the F-CAN C_H wire to the F-CAN C_L wire between CAN gateway terminals No. 5 and No. 13.
