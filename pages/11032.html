Engine Oil Replacement Reminder Reset - Procedure 08

NOTE: Use this procedure if the vehicle IS  equipped with Navigation System.
  1. Turn ignition switch to ON (II) position.
  2. Press the SEL/RESET button on the dashboard until you see the engine oil life display.
  3. Press and hold the SEL/RESET button for 10 seconds. The multi-information display will ask for a confirmation.
    • If you are sure you want to reset the display, press SEL/RESET button to select OK.
    • If you do not want to reset the display, press the INFO (ARROW) button to select CANCEL.
