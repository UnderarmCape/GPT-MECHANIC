Audio/Infomation Screen Method

  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II), or press the engine start/stop button to select the ON mode
  2. Press the SETTINGS button, then rotate the Selector Knob to select vehicle settings, then select maintence items.
  3. Follow the screen prompts to reset the engine oil life.
