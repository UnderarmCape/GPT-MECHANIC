Expansion Tank Cap Test (1.5L Engine): Test

  1. Expansion Tank Cap - Test
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Wait until the engine is cool, then carefully remove the expansion tank cap (A). Wet the expansion tank cap seal with engine coolant, then install it on a commercially available pressure tester (B).

    2. Apply a pressure of 93-123 kPa (0.95-1.25 kgf/cm 2, 13.5-17.8 psi) 

    3. Check for a drop in pressure.

    4. If the pressure drops, replace the expansion tank cap.
