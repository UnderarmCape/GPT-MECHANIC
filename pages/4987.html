Engine Oil Filter Feed Pipe Removal and Installation (L15B7): Installation

  1. Oil Filter Feed Pipe - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the two 20 x 1.5 mm nuts (A) onto the oil filter feed pipe. Hold one nut with a wrench, then use a second wrench to tighten the other nut.

    2. Apply new engine oil to the oil filter feed pipe threads.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
