Numbered call-outs in figure pertain to list numbers in following procedure.

Fig 1: Exploded View Of Intermediate Shaft Components For Disassembly (K20C1 - M/T - 2017-21)

Detailed information, notes, and precautions
Replace
  1. Outer Seal - Remove
  2. External Snap Ring - Remove
  3. Intermediate Shaft - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Press the intermediate shaft (A) out of the intermediate shaft bearing (B) using the support base attachment, the support base, and a press.
    NOTE:  Be careful not to damage the bearing support ring (C) on the intermediate shaft during disassembly.
  4. Internal Snap Ring - Remove
  5. Intermediate Shaft Bearing - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Press the intermediate shaft bearing (A) out of the bearing support (B) using the 15 x 135L driver handle, the 42 x 47 mm bearing driver attachment, and a press.
