Turbocharger Hose And Hose Band Guidelines (JA1079)

Publication date: 2019-01-09

TURBOCHARGER HOSE AND HOSE BAND GUIDELINES

TURBOCHARGER HOSE AND HOSE BAND GUIDELINES

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): BJA1079, Date of Issue:  January 9, 2019
ACURA: All Models

SERVICE INFORMATION

NOTE:
  • Below are general guidelines when installing turbocharger hoses (duct) and hose bands. Always refer to the service information for details.
NOTE: Failure to follow these guidelines will result in the hoses coming off during engine operation and damage to the hose during installation. Hoses that come off during engine operation may cause severe damage to the turbocharger unit.

APPLYING LUBRICANT

Before inserting the hose to the pipe, you must apply Honda TB2720C Water Base Lubricant (P/N 08734-0013) to the inside of the hose and the outside of the joining intake pipe or throttle body. You can order Honda TB2720C through normal parts ordering.
NOTE: Do not apply the lubricant on the outside of the hose or get any lubricant inside the throttle body.

The lubricant allows easier installation of the hose to the pipe, dries quickly, and will not introduce any contaminants into the system. Never use lubricants other than Honda TB7220C as they may cause contaminants to enter the system and damage the hose. This can cause the hose to come off the pipe under high pressure.

HOSE INSTALLATION

  1. Confirm the marking (Δ triangle) either on the intake pipe or throttle body.
  2. Install the hose with the hose clamp (loosely installed) and align the paint line on the hose to the marking to the intake pipe or throttle body.
    NOTE:
    • The paint color can vary by model.
    • Before removing any hoses during a repair, make sure the paint line on the hose and the hose band is present. If the paint line is not present, make a reference line on the hose and the hose band aligned with the marking on the pipe or throttle body using a paint marker.
    • Apply Honda TB2720C lubricant right before installation of the hose. Do not allow the lubricant to dry.
  3. Firmly slide the hose up against the lip of the pipe or throttle body while aligning the marks. There should be no gaps between the edge of the hose and lip around the entire joint or throttle body. If necessary, use a mirror to confirm the condition in hard-to-see areas.
  4. Slide the hose band to position while aligning the paint mark on the hose band and the hose.
    NOTE:
    • If you are reusing the hose, position the hose band in its original position. You will typically see a depression on the hose where the hose band was originally positioned.
    • If you are using a new hose, position the hose band evenly between the paint mark while aligning the marks on the hose and hose band. In certain cases, it may be difficult to align these marks because of the access around the hose. In these cases, install the hose clamp in the same orientation as when it was removed.
  5. Tighten the hose band to the specified condition below, while aligning the paint marks and the position of the hose band.
    NOTE:
    • It will be necessary to push down on the band end to confirm the correct condition.
    • Inspect the condition by looking directly over the band end and avoid looking at it from different angles.

END
