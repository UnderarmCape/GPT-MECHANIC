DTC B2A60-57, U1387-4A, U1387-55, U3000-55 (Millimeter Wave Radar) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021)

DTC B2A60-57  : Multipurpose Camera Unit Software Failure

DTC U1387-4A  : Unmatch Combination of Millimeter Wave Radar-Multipurpose Camera Unit

DTC U1387-55  : Multipurpose Camera Unit Variant Incomplete

DTC U3000-55  : Millimeter Wave Radar Variant Incomplete

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • If a DTC is recorded again after it is cleared with the HDS, confirm the part numbers compatible with the vehicle with the parts catalogue and replace the relevant parts.
  • If DTC U1387-4A is recorded, confirm the part numbers of the multipurpose camera unit and the millimeter wave radar currently installed to the vehicle and replace the parts not compatible with the vehicle.
    • -

      B2A60-57: Replace the multipurpose camera unit .

    • -

      U1387-4A: Replace the multipurpose camera unit or the millimeter wave radar .

    • -

      U1387-55: Replace the multipurpose camera unit .

    • -

      U3000-55: Replace the millimeter wave radar .

  • The functions of the integrated driver support system that will be temporarily canceled when this DTC is recorded can be confirmed with the DTC troubleshooting index .

DTC (IDAS)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, then wait for at least 6 seconds.

    -5. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    Is DTC B2A60-57, U1387-4A, U1387-55, or U3000-55 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Confirm the parts catalogue and replace the parts related to the DTC with compatible ones.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting .
