Expansion Tank Test (K20C1) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Test

  1. Expansion Tank - Test
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Wait until the engine is cool, then carefully remove the expansion tank cap, and fill the expansion tank with engine coolant to the base of the filler neck.

    2. Attach a commercially available pressure tester (A) to the expansion tank, and apply a pressure of 112-146 kPa (1.14-1.49 kgf/cm 2, 16.2-21.2 psi).

    3. Inspect for engine coolant leaks and a drop in pressure.

    4. Remove the pressure tester, then reinstall the expansion tank cap.
