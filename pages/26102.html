LaneWatch Symptom Troubleshooting - LaneWatch camera image does not come on (Switches to the black screen)

  1. LaneWatch system check (navigation system diagnostic mode)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Do the navigation system diagnostic mode, and select the Detail Information and Setting .

    -3. Select the Unit Check icon.

    -4. Select the LaneWatch icon.

    Is the Connection OK?

    YES 

    Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Check for hard error codes (navigation DTCs) .

  2. Determine possible failure area (LWC CAM VID/LWC VID line, LWC CAM VGND/LWC VID GND line, LaneWatch camera, others)

    -1. Select the Return, then back to Detail Information and Setting menu.

    -2. Start the engine.

    -3. Select the B-CAN icon, then start diagnostic.

    Is the MICU_BCM/ICU OK or HLSW_BCM/ICU OK?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Go to step 5.

  3. Open wire check (LWC CAM VID/LWC VID line)

    -1. Stop the engine.

    -2. Disconnect the following connectors.

    LaneWatch camera 6P connector
    Audio-navigation unit connector B (24P)

    -3. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      LaneWatch camera 6P connector: disconnected
      Audio-navigation unit connector B (24P): disconnected
    Test point 1 LaneWatch camera 6P connector No. 3
    Test point 2 Audio-navigation unit connector B (24P) No. 20
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the LWC CAM VID/LWC VID wire between the LaneWatch camera and the audio-navigation unit.

  4. Open wire check (LWC CAM VGND/LWC VID GND line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      LaneWatch camera 6P connector: disconnected
      Audio-navigation unit connector B (24P): disconnected
    Test point 1 LaneWatch camera 6P connector No. 4
    Test point 2 Audio-navigation unit connector B (24P) No. 21
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Replace the LaneWatch camera .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the LWC CAM VGND/LWC VID GND wire between the LaneWatch camera and the audio-navigation unit.

  5. Body electrical DTC check

    -1. Stop the engine.

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -3. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
       

    Are any DTCs indicated?

    YES 

    Go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting .

    NO 

    Go to step 6.

  6. Open wire check (B-CAN_H, B-CAN_L line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connectors.

    Body control module connector B (36P)
    Audio-navigation unit connector A (24P)

    -3. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Body control module connector B (36P): disconnected
      Audio-navigation unit connector A (24P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Body control module connector B (36P) No. 20
    Test point 2 Audio-navigation unit connector A (24P) No. 14
    Test point 1 Body control module connector B (36P) No. 21
    Test point 2 Audio-navigation unit connector A (24P) No. 24

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Check for poor connection or loose terminal at the audio-navigation unit. If the symptom does not go away, replace the audio-navigation unit .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the B-CAN_H wire or the B-CAN_L wire between the body control module and the audio-navigation unit.
