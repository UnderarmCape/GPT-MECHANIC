CVT Differential Backlash Inspection (L15B7/L15BA/L15BY (CVT)): Inspection

  1. Differential Backlash - Inspect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the driveshaft and the intermediate shaft on the differential, then place the axles on V-blocks.

    2. Check the backlash of the pinion gears (A) using a dial indicator (B).

    3. If the backlash is out of the standard, replace the differential carrier .
    Standard:  0.05-0.15 mm (0.0020-0.0059 in)
