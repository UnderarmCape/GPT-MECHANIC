Ignition Timing Inspection (K20C2): Inspection

  1. HDS - Connect
  2. DTC - Check, refer to DTC TROUBLESHOOTING INDEX

    NOTE:  If a DTC is present, diagnose and repair the cause before continuing with this inspection.

  3. Engine - Warm Up
    1. Start the engine. Hold the engine speed at 3000 RPM with no load (M/T in neutral, CVT in P or N) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.
  4. Idle Speed - Check
  5. SCS - Short
    1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS.
  6. Ignition Timing - Inspect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Start the engine.

    2. Connect the timing light to the service loop (white tape).

    3. Aim the light toward the pointer (A) on the cam chain case. Check the ignition timing under a no load condition (headlights, blower fan, rear window defogger, and air conditioner are turned off).

    Ignition Timing 
    M/T:  0 +/-2° BTDC (WHT mark (B)) at idle in neutral 
    CVT:  0 +/-2° BTDC (WHT mark (B)) at idle in P or N 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    4. If the ignition timing differs from the specification, check the camshaft timing . If the camshaft timing is OK, update the PCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If the system works properly, and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    5. Disconnect the HDS and the timing light.
