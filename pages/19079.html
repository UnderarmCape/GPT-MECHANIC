Safety Recall: Driver's Frontal Airbag Inflator (NADI-Unaffected) (A20-082)

WARNING: This page is about the Civic EX-L, 2D Coupe, which is a different variant/trim than selected.
Publication date: 2020-10-01
Reference number: A20-082

SAFETY RECALL: DRIVER'S FRONTAL AIRBAG INFLATOR (NADI-UNAFFECTED)

SAFETY RECALL: DRIVER'S FRONTAL AIRBAG INFLATOR (NADI-UNAFFECTED)

SERVICE CAMPAIGN BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 20-082, Date of Issue:  October 01, 2020
HONDA: All Models

AFFECTED VEHICLES

The affected vehicles for this bulletin varies. Check the iN VIN status for eligibility.

BACKGROUND

A review of manufacturing records revealed that certain vehicles originally included in the Driver's Frontal Airbag Inflator (NADI) Safety Recall were NOT  originally equipped with a driver's frontal airbag module that contained the recalled inflator.

This resulted in the removal of approximately 16, 000 Honda vehicles from the recall. Vehicles that have been removed from the recall will show a completed campaign 6NA00 with a link to this service bulletin in the iN VIN status even though the vehicle was not brought into a dealer.

A notification will be sent to these customers advising them on this situation.
