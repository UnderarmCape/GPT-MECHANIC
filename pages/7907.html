DTC P0010 (K20C1) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021)

DTC P0010  : VTC Oil Control Solenoid Valve A Malfunction

NOTE:  Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
P0010 VTC Oil Control Solenoid Valve A Malfunction    

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    Clear DTC

    -3. Start the engine.

    -4. Hold the engine speed at 3000 RPM without load (in neutral) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.

    -5. Select the VTC TEST in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS, then select the IN-VTC.

    VTC TEST

    -6. Check for Pending or Confirmed DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
    P0010 VTC Oil Control Solenoid Valve A Malfunction    

    Is DTC P0010 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at VTC oil control solenoid valve A and the PCM. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

  2. VTC oil control solenoid valve A internal resistance check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    VTC oil control solenoid valve A 2P connector

    -3. At the VTC oil control solenoid valve A side, measure the resistance between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      VTC oil control solenoid valve A 2P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 VTC oil control solenoid valve A 2P connector (male terminals) No. 1 (solenoid valve side):
    Test point 2 VTC oil control solenoid valve A 2P connector (male terminals) No. 2 (solenoid valve side):
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there 7.01-7.75 Ω at room temperature?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Replace VTC oil control solenoid valve A .

  3. Open wire check (PG line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      VTC oil control solenoid valve A 2P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 VTC oil control solenoid valve A 2P connector (female terminals) No. 2:
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The PG wire is OK. Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the PG wire between VTC oil control solenoid valve A and G101.

  4. Shorted wire check (VTC line)

    -1. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector No. 1 (96P)

    -3. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      VTC oil control solenoid valve A 2P connector: disconnected
      PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    Test point 1 VTC oil control solenoid valve A 2P connector (female terminals) No. 1:
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Repair a short in the VTC wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 6 and VTC oil control solenoid valve A.

    NO 

    The VTC wire is not shorted. Go to step 5.

  5. Open wire check (VTC line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      VTC oil control solenoid valve A 2P connector: disconnected
      PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    Test point 1 VTC oil control solenoid valve A 2P connector (female terminals) No. 1:
    Test point 2 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 6
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The VTC wire is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P0010 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the VTC wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 6 and VTC oil control solenoid valve A.
