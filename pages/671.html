Left rear door will not lock or unlock (4-door)

Check Items

  • Poor ground (G601)
  • Blown No. B13 fuse
  • Blown No. B38 fuse
  • Body control module input test
  • Door switch test (check the door switch ON/OFF information with the HDS)
  • Left rear door lock actuator test
