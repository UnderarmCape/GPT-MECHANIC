Motor Test 

  1. Passenger's Door Panel - Remove
  2. Passenger's Power Window Motor - Test
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Test the motor in each direction by connecting 12 volt battery power and ground to the connector (A) according to the table. When the motor stops running, disconnect 12 volt battery power immediately.
    • If the motor does not run or fails to run smoothly, remove the passenger's door regulator , then go to step 2.
    • If the motor runs smoothly, go to the Hall IC Unit (Pulser) Test.

    		 2. Test the passenger's power window motor (A) by connecting 12 volt battery power and ground according to the table. When the motor stops running, disconnect 12 volt battery power immediately.

    3. If the motor does not run or fails to run smoothly, the passenger's power window motor is faulty; replace the passenger's door regulator

    If the motor runs smoothly, replace the wire harnesses or adjust the passenger's door glass .

    Hall IC Unit (Pulser) Test 

  3. Hall IC Unit (Pulser) - Test

    2. Connect the connector (A).

    3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    4. Do the passenger's power window switch input test terminals No. 31, No. 35, and No. 37 

    MTR VCC circuit 

    MTR PLSA and MTR PLSB circuits:

    • If the voltage is not specified, the Hall IC Unit (Pulser) is faulty; replace the passenger's door regulator .
