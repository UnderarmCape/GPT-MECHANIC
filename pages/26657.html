General Information

EDITOR'S NOTE:  This article replaces "TOP TIER Detergent Gasoline: We Recommend It" posted in June 2010. 

When it comes to meeting the needs of today's advanced engines, you can't beat TOP TIER Detergent Gasoline. It's the premier standard for gasoline performance, and we fully recommend it to help maintain vehicle performance and reliability.

Fuel that meets these high standards is guaranteed to have the proper level of detergent additives and to be free of harmful manganese-based fuel additives such as MMT. This helps avoid the build-up of deposits in the engine and emission control system.

Since TOP TIER Detergent Gasoline was first introduced, the number of licensed retail brands has steadily grown. Just look how many there are as of July 27, 2017. 

In case you're wondering, we got this list from the TOP TIER Detergent Gasoline  website at www.toptiergas.com.

Besides featuring the most current list of licensed brands, which you can download as a PDF, you can get the back-story, learn about the standards, and even get answers to frequently asked questions.

So, check out this website if you haven't already, and visit it often. And be sure to let the service advisors know about it, too, so they can direct any inquiring customers to it.
