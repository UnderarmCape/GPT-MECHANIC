Multipurpose Camera unit

The Auto High-Beam of this vehicle is controlled by the multipurpose camera unit. If an error or malfunction of the multipurpose camera unit occurs, the Auto High-Beam does not operate. At this time, a warning message is displayed on the multi-information display (MID). The operations of the Auto High-Beam corresponding to the multipurpose camera unit malfunctions can be checked in the troubleshooting list by each DTC for the multipurpose camera unit .
