Power Mirrors System Description - Control/Function (5-door) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021)

Mirror Defogger Function (With Mirror Defogger) 

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

Manual Function 

The mirror defogger is controlled by the climate control unit. When the vehicle is in the ON mode and the rear window defogger/mirror defogger switch is pressed, the climate control unit sends the defogger ON signal as a B-CAN signal to the power window master switch. The power window master switch operates the driver's side mirror defogger, and sends the defogger ON signal via the LIN to the front passenger's power window switch to operate the passenger's side mirror defogger.

Auto Mirror Defogger Function 

When the power window master switch receives a signal that the outside air temperature is 41 deg.F (5 deg.C) or below from the outside air temperature sensor, the power window master switch automatically turns on the left and right mirror defoggers within 10 seconds after the vehicle is turned to the ON mode. The mirror defogger output automatically stops in 10 minutes as a result of its timer. If the power window master switch receives a rear window defogger/mirror defogger switch input signal from the climate control unit during power mirror defogger output, the power window master switch shuts off the auto operation function immediately.

Defogger Timer Function 

The mirror defogger provides the timer control function that is controlled by the climate control unit. The timer control is operated by turning the vehicle to the ON mode, then turning on the rear window defogger/mirror defogger switch. The timer operating time varies according to ambient temperature. If the rear window defogger/mirror defogger switch is turned on when the timer is active, it toggles between ON and OFF every time the switch is pressed.
