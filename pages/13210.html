Removal and Installation

  1. Rear Door Outer Molding - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Pull away the rear door weatherstrip (A).

    2. Remove the screws (B) securing the rear door outer molding (C).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 3. Pull out the upper edge of the rear door outer molding (A) from the edge of the sash, and cut the double-sided adhesive tape (B) with a utility knife, then remove the molding.
  2. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
