TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): A17100C, Date of Issue:  October 4, 2017
HONDA: 2016-2017 Accord, Pilot; 2014-2015 Accord Hybrid, Civic Hybrid; 2017 Accord Hybrid, Clarity Electric, Clarity Fuel Cell; 2014 Accord Plug-In, 2014-2018 Civic, 2015-2017 CR-V, 2016 CR-Z, 2015-2018 Fit, 2013-2014 Fit EV, 2016-2018 HR-V, and 2017-2018 Ridgeline

AFFECTED VEHICLES

2016-17 Accord, 2014-15 and 2017 Accord Hybrid, 2014 Accord Plug-In, 2014-18 Civic, 2014-15 Civic Hybrid, 2017 Clarity Electric, 2017 Clarity Fuel Cell, 2015-17 CR-V, 2016 CR-Z, 2015-18 Fit, 2013-14 Fit EV, 2016-18 HR-V, 2016-17 Pilot, and 2017-18 Ridgeline

Although similar, the HondaLink and HondaLink EV apps available for download from the Apple Apps Store and Google Play are not the same. Furthermore, each app applies to specific models. Here's the lowdown:

HondaLink 

Applies to:  2016-17 Accord, 2014-15 and 2017 Accord Hybrid, 2014-18 Civic, 2014-15 Civic Hybrid, 2017 Clarity Electric, 2017 Clarity Fuel Cell, 2015-17 CR-V, 2016 CR-Z, 2015-18 Fit, 2016-18 HR-V, 2016-17 Pilot, and 2017-18 Ridgeline

HondaLink EV 

Applies to:  2014 Accord Plug-In and 2013-14 Fit EV

Both of these apps are periodically updated. To check for the latest version, be sure to visit the Apple App Store or Google Play online.
