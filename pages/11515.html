Engine Oil Replacement Reminder Reset - Procedure 07

NOTE: Use this procedure if the vehicle IS NOT  equipped with Navigation System.
  1. Turn ignition switch to ON (II) position.
  2. Press the SELECT button repeatedly until the engine oil life is displayed.
  3. Press the TRIP/RESET button for about 10 seconds. The engine oil life and the maintenance item codes will blink.
  4. Press the TRIP/RESET button again for 5 seconds. The maintenance item codes will disappear, and the engine oil life will reset to 100%.
