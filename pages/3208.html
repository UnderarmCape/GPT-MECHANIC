Moonroof Glass Position Adjustment: Adjustment

  1. Moonroof Glass Position - Check
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    The roof panel (A) should be even with the glass roof seal (B) to within 0+1/-1 mm (0+0.04/-0.04 in) all the way around. If not, make the following adjustment:

  2. Moonroof Glass Position - Adjust
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Slightly loosen the bolts (A) with a TORX T25 bit.

    2. Move the glass (B) up or down.

    3. Tighten all bolts securely.

    4. If necessary, repeat on the opposite side.
