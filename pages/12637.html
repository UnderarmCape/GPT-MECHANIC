CVT Differential Backlash Inspection (K20C2 (CVT)): Inspection

  1. Differential Backlash - Inspect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install both axles into the differential, then place the axles on V-blocks (A).

    2. Check the backlash of the pinion gears (B) using a dial indicator (C).

    3. If the backlash is out of the standard, replace the differential carrier .
    Standard:  0.05-0.15 mm (0.0020-0.0059 in)
