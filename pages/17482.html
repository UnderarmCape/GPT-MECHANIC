Engine Oil Jet Inspection (K20C1) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Inspection

  1. Oil Jet - Remove
  2. Oil Jet - Inspection
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Inspect it as follows.
    • Make sure that a 1.1 mm (1/32 in) diameter drill bit will go through the nozzle hole (A) (1.3 mm (0.051 in) diameter).
    • Make sure that a 1.9 mm (1/16 in) diameter drill bit will go through the oil intake (B) (2.1 mm (0.083 in) diameter).
    • Insert a 1.9 mm (1/16 in) drill bit into the oil intake (2.1 mm (0.083 in) diameter). Make sure the check ball (C) moves smoothly.
  3. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
