DTC P060C (K20C1) (17-21)

DTC P060C  : PCM Internal Control Module Malfunction

NOTE:  Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
P060C PCM Internal Control Module Malfunction    

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Record all the on-board snapshots with the HDS.

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -3. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -5. Start the engine.

    Test-drive the vehicle for several minutes in the range of these recorded on-board snapshot parameters:

    • Engine Speed
    • Vehicle Speed
    • ECT Sensor 1
    • MAP Sensor (Hi Res)
    • REL TP Sensor

    Is DTC P060C indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P060C goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the PCM. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .
