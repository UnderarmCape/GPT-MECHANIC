Fuel Line Inspection (Except K20C1) (2016 2017 2018): Inspection

WARNING: 

Flammable - Keep Fire Away

NOTE: 

  • How to read the torque specifications .
  • Refer to the Fuel and Emissions Systems Service Precautions  before disconnecting the fuel line.
  • Fuel may cause flammable gas leaks and may result in fire. Keep sparks, flame and cigarettes away.
  1. Fuel Line - Inspect

    NOTE: 

    • Check the fuel system lines and hoses for damage, leaks, and deterioration. Replace any damaged parts.
    • Check all clamps, and make sure they are properly positioned and tightened.
      • -

        Make sure the clamps is in the position of its range shown.

      • -

        Align the marks on the hoses and the pipes.

      • -

        There should be no clearance between the hoses and the pipes.

  2. Throttle Body - Move
    NOTE:

    • Do not disconnect the water bypass hoses.
    • Do not bend the water bypass hoses excessively.

  3. Intake Manifold Bracket - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the intake manifold bracket (A).

    2. Move the high pressure fuel pump cover (B).

    NOTE:  Do not disconnect the quick-connect fitting.
  4. Intake Manifold - Remove
  5. Fuel Leak - Check

    1. Apply commercially available Met-L-Check D-70 Developer to the joint of the injectors (A), the fuel rail (B), and the high pressure fuel pump (C). Set the probe of the leak detector (Snap-on Combustible Gas Leak Detector) on the joint.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Install the intake manifold and the throttle body

    with the gaskets that had used on them.

    3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, but do not operate the starter. After the fuel pump runs for about 2 seconds, the low pressure fuel line will be pressurized. Repeat this two or three times, then check for fuel leakage.

    4. Start the engine. Hold the engine speed at 4, 000 rpm for 1 minute, and stop the engine.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    If the fuel line is leaking, stop the engine immediately, and turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    5. Move the throttle body

    , then remove the intake manifold .

    NOTE:

    • Do not disconnect the water bypass hoses.
    • Do not bend the water bypass hoses excessively.

    6. Check for fuel leakage to joint of the injectors, the fuel rail, and the high pressure fuel pump.
