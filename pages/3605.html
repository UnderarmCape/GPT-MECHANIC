Brake System Description - Overview (Type-R) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021)

Vacuum Pump 

Under the certain conditions, especially high altitude, the value of the vacuum pressure supplied from the intake manifold will not satisfy the value of the vacuum pressure needed from the brake system. To compensate for the lack of the vacuum pressure, this vehicle is equipped with a vacuum pump.

The vacuum pump is driven by the camshaft and it always drives while the engine is running.
