Moonroof Glass Roof Seal Removal and Installation: Removal

  1. Moonroof Glass - Remove 
  2. Glass Roof Seal - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Place a piece of masking tape (A) on the middle of the front edge of the moonroof glass (B). Make sure that the tape is on the glass only, and is not touching the glass roof seal (C).

    2. Place a mark (D) on the masking tape that aligns with the seam (E) of the glass roof seal.

    3. Push down on the top of the original glass roof seal with your thumb until a small section separates from the moonroof glass. Once a section has started to separate, grip it and pull outward, working around the moonroof glass until the glass roof seal is completely removed (note the locations of the holes in the glass roof seal).
