How to Troubleshoot Circuits at the TCM Connector

Special Tools Required 

Male Pin Probe 07ZAJ-RDJA110 

1. Remove the intake air duct F .

2. Disconnect the connector (A) by pushing the lock (B) and pulling the lever (C) in the numbered sequence shown.

Fig 1: TCM Connector Disconnection Sequence
Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

3. When diagnosis/troubleshooting is done at the TCM connector, use the terminal test port (A) above the terminal you need to check.

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

4. Connect one side of the patch cord terminals (A) to a commercially available digital multimeter (B), and connect the other side of the terminals (C) to the pin probe male.

Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

5. Gently contact the pin probe (male) at the terminal test port from the terminal side. Do not force the tips into the terminals.

NOTICE: 

  • For accurate results, always use the pin probe (male).
  • To prevent damage to the connector terminals, do not insert test equipment probes, paper clips, or other substitutes as they can damage the terminals. Damaged terminals cause a poor connection and an incorrect measurement.
  • Do not puncture the insulation on a wire. Punctures can cause or eventually lead to poor or intermittent electrical connections.
