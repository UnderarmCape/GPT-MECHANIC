Over-Run Alternator Decoupler (OAD) Pulley Inspection (K20C2/K20C1): Inspection

NOTE:  Unless otherwise indicated, illustrations used in the procedure are for K20C2 engine.

  1. Alternator - Remove
  2. OAD Pulley Seal Cap - Remove
  3. Alternator OAD Pulley Holder - Insert
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Insert the alternator OAD pulley holder into the OAD pulley.
  4. OAD Pulley - Inspect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Place a beam type torque wrench (A) with a 17 mm socket wrench (B) on the alternator OAD pulley holder.

    2. While holding the OAD pulley (C), rotate the alternator shaft counterclockwise. Make sure the alternator shaft is locked when applying the specified torque.

    • If the alternator shaft locks, go to step 3.
    • If it does not lock, replace the OAD pulley .
    Specified Torque:
    13 N.m (1.3 kgf.m, 10 lbf.ft) 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 3. While holding the OAD pulley (A), rotate the alternator shaft clockwise. Check that the alternator shaft rotates at the specified torque or less.
    • If the alternator shaft rotates at the specified torque or less, it is normal. Install a new OAD pulley seal cap.
    • If it does not rotate at the specified torque or less, replace the OAD pulley .
    Specified Torque:
    0.8 N.m (0.08 kgf.m, 0.6 lbf.ft) max. 
  5. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE: 
    • When installing, do not distort a new OAD pulley seal cap.
    • Make sure to check that a new cap is installed securely.
