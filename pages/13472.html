Rearview camera image does not come on (Without Navigation, Color Audio Type (5-inch Screen))

NOTE: 

  • Check the vehicle 12 volt battery condition first .
  • Check the connectors for poor connections or loose terminals.
  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Shift the transmission to R position/mode.

    Does the audio display image change when the transmission is shifted to R position/mode?

    YES 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.

    NO 

    The failure is duplicated, go to step 2.

  2. In Line Diag check

    -1. Shift the transmission to Neutral or P position/mode.

    -2. Go into the System Diagnostic Mode, and use the In Line Diag to check the rearview camera connection .

    Does the Rear Camera indicate OK?

    YES 

    Go to step 7.

    NO 

    Go to step 3.

  3. Determine possible failure area (CAMERA ADPT line, others)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    Rearview camera 8P connector

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      Rearview camera 8P connector: disconnected
       
    Test point 1 Rearview camera 8P connector No. 1 (2-door)
    Rearview camera 8P connector No. 6 (except 2-door)
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there about 5.0 V?

    YES 

    Go to step 5.

    NO 

    Go to step 4.

  4. Open wire check (CAMERA ADPT line)

    -1. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      Rearview camera 8P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector C (32P) No. 16
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there about 5.0 V?

    YES 

    Repair an open in the wire between the audio unit and the rearview camera.

    NO 

    Replace the audio unit .

  5. Determine possible failure area (rearview camera, others)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Rearview camera 8P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Rearview camera 8P connector No. 5
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Replace the rearview camera .

    NO 

    Go to step 6.

  6. Open wire check (SH CAMERA line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Rearview camera 8P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector C (32P) No. 28
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    There is an open in the wire(s) between the audio unit and the rearview camera. Replace the affected shielded harness.

    NO 

    Replace the audio unit .

  7. Determine possible failure area (BACK LT line, others)

    -1. Go into the System Diagnostic Mode, and use the MICU_ICU in the Vehicle Information to check the BACK signal .

    -2. Shift the transmission to R position/mode.

    Does the C_BACKLTSW signal change from [0] to [1] when the transmission is shifted from Neutral or P position/mode to R position/mode?

    YES 

    Go to step 9.

    NO 

    Go to step 8.

  8. Open wire check (B-CAN_H line, B-CAN_L line)

    -1. Shift the transmission to Neutral or P position/mode.

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -3. Disconnect the following connectors.

    Body control module connector B (36P)
    Audio unit connector A (24P)

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Body control module connector B (36P): disconnected
      Audio unit connector A (24P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Body control module connector B (36P) No. 20
    Test point 2 Audio unit connector A (24P) No. 14
    Test point 1 Body control module connector B (36P) No. 21
    Test point 2 Audio unit connector A (24P) No. 24

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Replace the audio unit .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the wire(s) between the audio unit and the body control module.

  9. Shorted wire check (CAMERA VCC, CAMERA GND, CAMERA V lines)

    -1. Shift the transmission to Neutral or P position/mode.

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -3. Disconnect the following connectors.

    Audio unit connector C (32P)
    Rearview camera 8P connector

    -4. Check for continuity between the following test points and body ground individually.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio unit connector C (32P): disconnected
      Rearview camera 8P connector: disconnected
    Connector Terminal
    Audio unit connector C (32P) No. 13
    No. 14
    No. 29

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    There is a short to body ground in the wire(s) between the audio unit and the rearview camera. Replace the affected shielded harness.

    NO 

    The CAMERA VCC, CAMERA GND, and CAMERA V wires are not shorted to ground. Go to step 10.

  10. Shorted wire check (CAMERA lines)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio unit connector C (32P): disconnected
      Rearview camera 8P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1:Audio unit connector C (32P) Test point 2:Audio unit connector C (32P)
    No. 13 No. 14, No. 28, No. 29
    Test point 1:Audio unit connector C (32P) Test point 2:Audio unit connector C (32P)
    No. 14 No. 28, No. 29
    No. 28 No. 29

    Is there continuity between any of the terminals?

    YES 

    There is a short in the wires between the audio unit and the rearview camera. Replace the affected shielded harness.

    NO 

    The CAMERA wires are not shorted in the wires. Go to step 11.

  11. Open wire check (CAMERA VCC, CAMERA GND, CAMERA V lines)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio unit connector C (32P): disconnected
      Rearview camera 8P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1:Audio unit connector C (32P) Test point 2:Rearview camera 8P connector
    No. 13 No. 2
    No. 14 No. 8
    No. 29 No. 7

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The CAMERA VCC, CAMERA GND, and CAMERA V wires are OK. Go to step 12.

    NO 

    There is an open in the wire(s) between the audio unit and the rearview camera. Replace the affected shielded harness.

  12. Voltage check (CAMERA VCC line)

    -1. Reconnect audio unit connector C (32P).

    -2. Reconnect the rearview camera 8P connector.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector C (32P) No. 13
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there about 8.0 V?

    YES 

    Go to step 13.

    NO 

    Replace the audio unit .

  13. Rearview camera check (substitution)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Substitute a known-good rearview camera , and recheck.

    Does the symptom go away?

    YES 

    Replace the original rearview camera.

    NO 

    Replace the audio unit .
