    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:  Locate the alignment mark (A) and the joint (B) on the tailgate weatherstrip (C). Align the mark with the alignment tab (D) in the center of the tailgate opening, align the joint with the tailgate striker (E), and install the weatherstrip. Make sure the weatherstrip is seated completely and facing in the direction shown. Make sure there are no wrinkles in the weatherstrip.
