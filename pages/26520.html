TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): JA34005 - 50627-05807 Version 4, Date of Issue:  February 01, 2017
HONDA: See MODEL DESCRIPTION
Superceded Bulletin(s): 50627-05807 Version 4, Date of Issue:  July 2015
NOTE: Supersedes A/C Compressor Oil Guide, dated July 2015, to revise the information highlighted in yellow

REVISION SUMMARY

  • The 2017 Clarity, 2017 CR-V and 2017 Ridgeline were added.
  • New Models years were updated.

American Honda sells several types of refrigerant oil for A/C systems. Using the wrong refrigerant oil can cause compressor damage. This guide shows you which refrigerant oil you need for servicing A/C systems in 1994 and later Honda cars and trucks.
