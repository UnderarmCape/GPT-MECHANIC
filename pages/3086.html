Removal and Installation

  1. Upper Column Cover - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Adjust the steering column to the full tilt down position, and to the full telescopic out position.

    2. Remove the clips (A) from the upper column cover (B).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 3. Turn the steering wheel 90° clockwise, and then release the hook (A) with the trim tool.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Turn the steering wheel 180° counterclockwise, and then release the hook (A) with the trim tool.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 5. Remove the upper column cover (A).
  2. Lower Column Cover - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Turn the steering wheel to access the screws (A), then remove the screws.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Remove the lower column cover (A).
  3. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
