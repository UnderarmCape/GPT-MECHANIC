DTC P2583-64 (2/4-door) (2016 2017 2018)

DTC P2583-64  : Millimeter Wave Radar Aiming Error

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • This DTC is stored when unsuccessfully completed millimeter wave radar aiming has caused a misaligned radio wave axis and the millimeter wave radar has sent a problem detected while the vehicle is running to the multipurpose camera unit.
    • When DTC P2583-64 is stored, the DTCs in the HDS cannot be cleared. These DTCs can be cleared when millimeter wave radar aiming is completed correctly.
    • If DTC P2583-64 is output even after millimeter wave radar aiming is completed successfully, the millimeter wave radar is suspected of being faulty. Replace the millimeter wave radar.
DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
P2583-64 Millimeter Wave Radar Aiming Error    

DTC (IDAS)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -4. Test-drive the vehicle on a straight road at speeds 25 mph (40 km/h) or more for 5 minutes with the ACC activated.

    NOTE:  Drive the vehicle on the road, not on a lift.

    -5. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
    P2583-64 Millimeter Wave Radar Aiming Error    

    Is DTC P2583-64 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting . If the freeze data/on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the freeze data/on-board snapshot.

  2. Millimeter wave radar installation check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Remove the front bumper .

    -3. Check the installation of the millimeter wave radar assembly.

    Is the installation OK?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Reinstall or replace a parts that has a problem, then go to step 3.

  3. Millimeter wave radar check (aiming)

    -1. Aim the millimeter wave radar .

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -3. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -5. Test-drive the vehicle on a straight road at speeds 25 mph (40 km/h) or more for 5 minutes with the ACC activated.

    NOTE:  Drive the vehicle on the road, not on a lift.

    -6. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
    P2583-64 Millimeter Wave Radar Aiming Error    

    Is DTC P2583-64 indicated?

    YES 

    Check for loose terminals and poor connections in the millimeter wave radar 10P connector. If they are OK, replace the millimeter wave radar .

    NO 

    Troubleshooting is complete. If any other DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.
