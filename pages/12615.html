TCM Reset (K20C2, CVT model): Procedure

  1. HDS - Connect
  2. TCM - Reset
    1. Select the A/T SYSTEM with the HDS.

    2. Reset the TCM with the HDS while the engine is stopped.

    3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, then wait for 30 seconds.

    5. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode, then disconnect the HDS from the DLC.
