Pulley End Crankshaft Oil Seal Replacement - In Car (1.5L Engine): Replacement

  1. Crankshaft Pulley - Remove
  2. Pulley End Crankshaft Oil Seal - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  3. Pulley End Crankshaft Oil Seal - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Clean and dry the pulley end crankshaft oil seal housing.

    2. Apply a light coat of new engine oil to the lip of the new pulley end crankshaft oil seal.

    3. Using the driver handle, 15 x 135L and the bearing driver attachment, 40 mm to drive in the new pulley end crankshaft oil seal (A) squarely into the cam chain case to the specified installed height.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Measure the distance between the cam chain case surface (A) and the pulley end crankshaft oil seal (B).

    5. Clean the excess oil off the crankshaft, and check that the oil seal lip is not distorted.
    Oil Seal Installed Height: 
    10.2-10.5 mm (0.402-0.413 in)
