Front Lower Arm Compliance Bush Removal and Installation (Except Type-R): Installation

  1. Lower Arm Compliance Bushing - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Clean the mating surfaces of the new bushing and the lower arm.

    2. Apply a mild soap and water solution to the bushing surface.

    3. Align the raised portion (A) of the lower arm (B) and the groove (C) of the bushing (D).

    4. Press in the bushing by hand first.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 5. Support the lower arm (A) on suitable square lumber (B) as shown.

    6. Press in the bushing (C) with a suitable socket (D) and a hydraulic press.

    NOTE:  Press the bushing into the lower arm until the mark (E) meets the edge of the bushing opening.

    7. Make sure the distance between the lower arm mounting surface (F), and the bushing surface (G) is within the dimension shown.
