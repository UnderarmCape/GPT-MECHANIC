Engine Oil Pressure Switch Removal and Installation (1.5L Engine): Removal

  1. Right Front Wheel - Remove
  2. Engine Undercover Lid - Remove (With Engine Undercover Lid)
  3. Engine Undercover - Remove
  4. Drive Belt - Remove
  5. A/C Compressor - Move

    NOTE: 

    • Do not disconnect the suction hose and the discharge hose.
    • Do not bend the suction hose and the discharge hose excessively.
    • Hang the A/C compressor with a wire tie.
  6. Oil Pressure Switch - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Disconnect the connector (A).

    2. Remove the oil pressure switch (B).

    3. Remove any old liquid gasket from the oil pressure switch and the oil pressure switch mounting hole.
