Removal and Installation

  1. Mirror Holder - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Adjust the mirror fully to the upward position.

    2. Put a shop towel in the opening between the bottom edge of the mirror holder (A) and the mirror housing (B) to prevent scratches.

    3. Insert a flat-tip screwdriver under the mirror holder, and release the hooks (C).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Remove the mirror holder (A).

    5. Disconnect the connectors (B).
  2. All Removed Parts - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:  Before reinstalling the mirror holder to the inner holder (A) of the actuator, make sure the actuator rods (B) are securely mounted in the holes, and that the boots (C) properly cover each rod.
