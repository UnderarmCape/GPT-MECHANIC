Detailed information, notes, and precautions
  1. Connector (Clutch Pedal Stroke Sensor and Clutch Pedal Position Switch A) - Disconnect
  2. Lock Pin - Remove
  3. Clevis Pin - Remove
  4. Clutch Pedal Adjusting Bolt - Remove
  5. Clutch Pedal Position Switch A - Remove
  6. Clutch Pedal - Remove

    NOTE:  Make sure the harness is removed from the clutch pedal assembly before removing the clutch pedal assembly.
