New INTERCOOLER Hose Lubricant Available (A18010D)

WARNING: This page is about the Civic EX-L, 2D Coupe, which is a different variant/trim than selected.
Publication date: 2018-01-10
Reference number: A18010D

NEW INTERCOOLER HOSE LUBRICANT AVAILABLE

NEW INTERCOOLER HOSE LUBRICANT AVAILABLE

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): A18010D Version 1, Date of Issue:  January 10, 2018
HONDA: All Models

AFFECTED VEHICLES

General Information

All Honda dealers were recently sent a supply of Honda TB2720C Water Base Lubricant (P/N 08734-0013). This ThreeBond® product is the only lubricant that's factory approved for installing rubber intercooler hoses.

It dries quickly after hose installation and won't introduce contaminants into the system. It's also ideal for general coolant hose installation.

If you need more, just order through normal parts ordering channels.
