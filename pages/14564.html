Manual Transmission Removal and Installation (K20C1 (M/T)) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

NOTE: 

  • Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  • Keep all foreign particles out of the transmission.
  • Special tool Reds engine support hanger AAR-T1256 must be used with the side engine mount installed.
  1. Vehicle - Lift
  2. Engine Cover - Remove
  3. Air Cleaner - Remove
  4. Front Grille Cover - Remove
  5. 12 Volt Battery - Remove
  6. 12 Volt Battery Base - Remove
  7. High Pressure Fuel Pump Cover - Remove
  8. EVAP Canister Purge Valve - Remove
  9. Charge Air Cooler Hose - Disconnect

    NOTE:  Remove the necessary part(s).

  10. Slave Cylinder - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the clutch line bracket (A).

    NOTE:  Be careful not to bend the clutch line.

    2. Remove the slave cylinder (B).

    3. Carefully move the slave cylinder out of the way to avoid bending the clutch line.

    • Do not press the clutch pedal after the slave cylinder has been moved.
    • Do not disconnect the clutch line joints.
  11. Engine Wire Harness - Remove

    1. Disconnect the connectors (A).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Remove the harness clamps (B).

    3. Remove the engine wire harness with the harness holder bracket (C).

  12. Change Wire - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the lock pins (A).

    2. Remove the change wire clips (B).

    3. Carefully remove the change wires (C) together to avoid bending the change wires.

    NOTE:  Do not wipe off the grease of the change wire ends (D).
  13. Change Wire Bracket and Mass - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. If necessary, remove the change wire bracket (A) and the mass (B).
  14. Water Bypass Pipe - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  15. Engine Support Hanger - Install

    NOTE: 

    • Be careful when working around the windshield.
    • Be careful not to damage the hood opener cable when installing the engine support hanger at the front bulkhead.

    1. Install the universal lifting eyelet with an about 60 mm (2.36 in) commercially available spacer (A).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Install the engine support hanger onto the vehicle as shown.

    3. Attach the hook (B) to the slotted hole in the universal lifting eyelet.

    4. Tighten the wing nut (C) by hand, and lift and support the engine/transmission.

  16. Upper Transmission Assembly Mounting Bolt - Remove

    NOTE:  Refer to the Exploded View as needed during this procedure.

  17. Transmission Mount - Remove
  18. Steering Joint - Disconnect

    NOTE:  Hold the steering wheel with the steering wheel holder tool.

  19. Vehicle - Lift Up
  20. Front Wheel - Remove (Both Sides)
  21. Engine Undercover - Remove

    NOTE:  Remove the necessary part(s).

  22. Transmission Fluid - Drain
  23. Connector (Front Suspension Stroke Sensor Subharness) - Disconnect

    Left side 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Right side 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  24. Front Suspension Stroke Sensor - Remove (Both Sides)
  25. Exhaust Pipe A - Remove
  26. Stopper Link - Disconnect (Both Sides)
  27. Lower Stabilizer Link - Disconnect (Both Sides)
  28. Tie-Rod End Ball Joint - Disconnect (Both Sides)
  29. Lower Arm Ball Joint - Disconnect (Both Sides)
  30. Connector (EPS Subharness) - Disconnect
  31. MTF Cooler Pipe A and B - Remove (Transmission Side)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove MTF cooler pipes A and B with the washers (C).
    NOTE:  Turn the end of MTF cooler pipes up to prevent MTF from flowing out, then winding a shop towel.
  32. MTF Cooler - Remove 

    NOTE: 

    • Do not disconnect the MTF cooler hoses.
    • The MTF cooler is aluminum part. Be careful not to damage the MTF cooler.
  33. Torque Rod Mounting Bolt - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  34. Front Brace - Remove
  35. Lower Arm Mounting Bolt - Remove (Both Sides)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  36. Front Subframe - Support
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Set the subframe adapter (VSB02C000016) on a transmission jack (A), line up the slots in the arms with the bolt holes on the corner of the jack base, and tighten the bolts.

    2. Attach the subframe adapter to the front subframe (B).
  37. Front Subframe - Remove
  38. Transmission - Support
    1. Support the transmission with the transmission jack.
  39. Clutch Cover - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  40. Driveshaft Inboard Joint - Disconnect (Both Sides)

    NOTE:  Secure the driveshaft to the body with a nylon strap on both sides.

  41. Intermediate Shaft - Remove
  42. Lower Transmission Assembly Mounting Bolt - Remove

    NOTE:  Refer to the Exploded View as needed during this procedure.

  43. Transmission - Remove
    1. Slide the transmission away from the engine until the transmission mainshaft clears the clutch pressure plate.

    2. Slowly lower the transmission about 150 mm (5.91 in).

    3. Check once again that all hoses and harness are disconnected and free from the transmission.

    4. Lower the transmission carefully.
