VSA System Description - Agile Handling Assist Control

Overview 

Where previous VSA systems activated at the limit of a turn, the agile handling assist control activates across everyday driving situations providing a safer and more enjoyable driving experience.Agile handling assist responds to the increase/decrease in steering input and vehicle action is made smooth at the same time as it improves response to steering operation. This makes it possible to reduce the amount of steering operations and cornering with agility, invigoration, and a secure feel is realized. This steering action is made smooth with a small amount of steering operation.
