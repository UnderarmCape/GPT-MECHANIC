Starting System Symptom Troubleshooting Index

Without keyless access system 

Symptom  Diagnostic procedure  Also check for 
Engine does not crank
  • Check for loose 12 volt battery terminals or connections.
  • Test the 12 volt battery for a low state of charge .
  • Check for PGM-FI DTCs .
  • Check the starter .
  • Check the relay circuit board .
  • Check the starter system circuit troubleshooting .
  • M/T: Check clutch pedal position switch A .

    CVT: Check the transmission range switch .

  • Check the ignition switch and its related circuit .
  • M/T: Poor ground at G305CVT: Poor ground at G201
  • Seized engine
Engine cranks, but does not start
  • Check for PGM-FI DTCs .
  • Check the PGM-FI main relays .
  • Check the fuel pressure .
  • Check for a plugged or damaged fuel line .
  • Check for a plugged fuel filter .
  • Check the throttle body .
  • Check for low engine compression .
  • Check the camshaft timing .
  • Do the PCM reset in the PGM-FI System to cancel ALL INJECTORS STOP with the HDS.
  • Fuel level in tank
  • Weak or fouled spark plugs
Engine is hard to start Do the symptom troubleshooting for engine will not start, engine starts but stalls immediately, or engine is hard to start . Weak or fouled spark plugs
Engine cranks slowly
  • Check for loose 12 volt battery terminals or connections.
  • Test the 12 volt battery for a low state of charge .
  • Check the starter for binding .
  • Check for excessive drag in the engine.
