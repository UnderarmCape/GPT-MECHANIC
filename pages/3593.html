Handling the brake fluid

NOTICE: 

Do not spill brake fluid on the vehicle; it may damage the paint. If brake fluid does contact the paint, wash it off immediately with water.

  • Use recommend new genuine Acura or Honda Brake Fluid from an unopened container. Using a non-Acura or non-Honda brake fluid can cause corrosion and shorten the life of the system.
  • Do not reuse the drained brake fluid.
  • Make sure no dirt or other foreign matter gets in the brake fluid.
