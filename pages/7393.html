DTC P0201 (K20C1) (17-21)

DTC P0201  : No. 1 Cylinder Injector Circuit Malfunction

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • If DTC P0560, P068A, and/or P068B are stored at the same time as DTC P0201, troubleshoot those DTCs first, then recheck for DTC P0201.
DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
P0201 No. 1 Cylinder Injector Circuit Malfunction    

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Start the engine (If the engine does not start, crank the engine for 20 seconds).

    -4. Check for Pending or Confirmed DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC
    P0201 No. 1 Cylinder Injector Circuit Malfunction    

    Is DTC P0201 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the injector and the PCM. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

  2. Open wire check (INJ H1 line, INJ L1 line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector No. 1 (96P)

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    Test point 1 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 17
    Test point 2 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 18

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The INJ H1 wire and the INJ L1 wire are not open. Go to step 5.

    NO 

    Go to step 3.

  3. Open wire check (INJ H1 line)

    -1. Disconnect the following connector.

    No. 1 injector 2P connector

    -2. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
      No. 1 injector 2P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 No. 1 injector 2P connector (female terminals) No. 2:
    Test point 2 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 17
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The INJ H1 wire is OK. Go to step 4.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the INJ H1 wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 17 and the No. 1 injector.

  4. Open wire check (INJ L1 line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    No. 1 injector 2P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 No. 1 injector 2P connector (female terminals) No. 1:
    Test point 2 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 18
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The INJ L1 wire is OK. Replace all injectors as a set .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the INJ L1 wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 18 and the No. 1 injector.

  5. Shorted wire check (INJ H1 line to power)

    -1. Disconnect the following connector.

    No. 1 injector 2P connector

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -3. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
    PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    No. 1 injector 2P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 17
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there any voltage?

    YES 

    Repair a short to power in the INJ H1 wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 17 and the No. 1 injector.

    NO 

    Go to step 6.

  6. Shorted wire check (INJ L1 line to power)

    -1. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
    PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    No. 1 injector 2P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 18
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there any voltage?

    YES 

    Repair a short to power in the INJ L1 wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 18 and the No. 1 injector.

    NO 

    Go to step 7.

  7. Shorted wire check (INJ H1 line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    No. 1 injector 2P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 17
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Repair a short in the INJ H1 wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 17 and the No. 1 injector.

    NO 

    The INJ H1 wire is OK. Go to step 8.

  8. Shorted wire check (INJ L1 line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    PCM connector No. 1 (96P): disconnected
    No. 1 injector 2P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 PCM connector No. 1 (96P) No. 18
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Repair a short in the INJ L1 wire between PCM connector No. 1 terminal No. 18 and the No. 1 injector.

    NO 

    The INJ L1 wire is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC P0201 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .
