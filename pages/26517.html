A/C REFRIGERANT TYPE GUIDE

A/C REFRIGERANT TYPE GUIDE

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): AJA11219, Date of Issue:  June 01, 2019
HONDA: All Models

REVISION

2019 Fit refrigerant was changed to R-1234yf.

AFFECTED VEHICLES

American Honda sells two different types of refrigerant for A/C systems. Be careful with what type of refrigerant is put in the vehicles. This guide shows you which refrigerant you need for servicing A/C systems in 2000 and newer Honda vehicles.
