Detailed information, notes and precautions
Replace
  1. Fuel Pressure - Relieve
  2. EVAP Canister Purge Valve and Purge Joint - Remove
  3. Throttle Body - Move

    NOTE: 

    • Do not disconnect the water bypass hoses.
    • Do not bend the water bypass hoses excessively.
  4. Intake Manifold - Remove
  5. Joint Pipe Bracket - Remove
  6. High Pressure Fuel Pump Cover - Remove
  7. Harness Holder - Move
  8. Quick-Connect Fitting - Disconnect
  9. Fuel Joint Pipe - Remove
  10. High Pressure Fuel Pump and Pump Lifter - Remove
    1. Loosen the bolts alternately, and remove the high pressure fuel pump and the pump lifter.
  11. Fuel Feed Pipe - Remove (If Necessary)
  12. High Pressure Fuel Pump Cam - Check
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Rotate the crankshaft and check the high pressure fuel pump cam (A) for pits, scores, or excessively wear. If needed, replace the exhaust camshaft .
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 2. Install the pump lifter (A) to the high pressure fuel pump cam.

    3. Set the gauge (B) on the pump lifter and hold it.

    4. Rotate the crankshaft 720° and measure the four cam lobes height. If the cam lobes are excessively worn, replace the exhaust camshaft .

    NOTE:  The high pressure fuel pump cam has four cam lobes.

    Cam Lobe Height Standard (New): 4.0 mm (0.157 in)
