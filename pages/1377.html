Millimeter Wave Radar Mounting Position Inspection (4-door, Japan Production) (2018): Inspection

NOTE: 

  • If millimeter wave radar aiming cannot be completed, follow the procedure below to check that the millimeter wave radar is installed properly and the radar bracket is not deformed.
  • Based on the check results, replace faulty parts. After that, perform millimeter wave radar aiming again.
  1. Radar Cover - Remove 
  2. Millimeter Wave Radar Mounting Position - Inspect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 NOTE:  Make sure the steering wheel is pointed straight ahead, and do not turn it after setting up the vehicle.

    1. Put the level on the aiming pad of the millimeter wave radar.

    NOTE:  Use the level included in the alignment set.

    2. Confirm if the bubble in the level is between the lines. Check visually using a mirror (A).

    NOTE:

    • To correctly confirm the bubble position in the level, hold the mirror above the level so that the level in the mirror is seen as a rectangle.
    • If the bubble is not within the lines, adjust the mounting position using the vertical adjustment bolt (B).
    • If you cannot adjust the millimeter wave radar horizontally, check for damage on the millimeter wave radar bracket or the vehicle frame.
