Wireless Charger Symptom Troubleshooting - Wireless charger indicators do not come on (green and amber) (5-door) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021)

  1. Open wire check (+B BACK UP line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    Wireless charger 7P connector

    -3. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition OFF (LOCK) mode
    Wireless charger 7P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Wireless charger 7P connector No. 7 
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Repair an open or high resistance in the wire.

  2. Open wire check (GND line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition OFF (LOCK) mode
    Wireless charger 7P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Wireless charger 7P connector No. 1 
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Replace the wireless charger .

    NO 

    Repair an open or high resistance in the ground wire or poor ground (G503).
