DTC U0100-00 (Without LKAS (EPS Control Unit))

DTC U0100-00  : F-CAN Malfunction (EPS Control Unit-PCM)

NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
U0100-00 F-CAN Malfunction (EPS Control Unit-PCM)    

DTC (EPS)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    Clear DTCs

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode, then start the engine.

    -4. Wait for 10 seconds or more.

    -5. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
    U0100-00 F-CAN Malfunction (EPS Control Unit-PCM)    

    Is DTC U0100-00 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. If the freeze data/on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the Freeze data/on-board snapshot.

  2. Open wire check (F-CAN_H, F-CAN_L lines) 1

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Short the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connectors.

    EPS control unit connector B (6P)
    PCM connector A (50P) *1
    PCM connector No. 2 (58P) *2

    *1: Except Type-R

    *2: Type-R

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    EPS control unit connector B (8P): disconnected
    PCM connector A (50P) *1: disconnected
    PCM connector No. 2 (58P) *2: disconnected
    Test point 1 EPS control unit connector B (6P) No. 3
    Test point 2 PCM connector A (50P) No. 37 *1
    PCM connector No. 2 (58P) No. 15 *2
    Test point 1 EPS control unit connector B (6P) No. 1
    Test point 2 PCM connector A (50P) No. 36 *1
    PCM connector No. 2 (58P) No. 27 *2

    *1: Except Type-R

    *2: Type-R

    Is there continuity?

    YES (CVT)

    Go to step 3.

    YES (M/T)

    The F-CAN_H and the F-CAN_L wires are OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the F-CAN_H and/or the F-CAN_L wire between EPS motor/control unit and the PCM.

  3. Open wire check (F-CAN_H, F-CAN_L lines) 2

    -1. Disconnect the following connector.

    TCM 50P connector

    -2. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    EPS control unit connector B (6P): disconnected
    PCM connector A (50P): disconnected
    TCM 50P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 EPS control unit connector B (6P) No. 3
    Test point 2 TCM 50P connector No. 3
    Test point 1 EPS control unit connector B (6P) No. 1
    Test point 2 TCM 50P connector No. 11

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The F-CAN_H and the F-CAN_L wires are OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good TCM , then recheck. If this DTC goes away and the TCM was substituted, replace the original TCM .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the F-CAN_H and/or the F-CAN_L wire between EPS motor/control unit and the TCM.
