Removal and Installation

  1. Front Door Sash Outer Trim - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Pull away the front door weatherstrip (A), then remove the screw (B) securing the front door outer molding (C).

    2. Pull out the glass run channel (D) as needed.

    3. Cut the double-sided adhesive tapes (E) with a utility knife.

    4. Remove the upper edge of the front door sash outer trim (F) from the front door outer molding.

    NOTE:  Pull the front door outer molding only as needed to remove the upper edge of the front door sash outer trim. Excessive pulling will cause the molding to get damaged resulting in replacement.

    5. Release the hook (G), then remove the front door sash outer trim.

    6. Remove any remaining double-sided adhesive tapes from the front door sash outer trim and the front door sash, then clean the surfaces with a sponge dampened with isopropyl alcohol. After cleaning, keep oil, grease, and water from getting on the clean surface.
  2. All Removed Parts - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:  If you are reusing the original front door sash outer trim (A), apply the adhesive primer to the areas where the new double-sided adhesive tapes (B, C) will be applied, then attach the new double-sided adhesive tapes to the front door sash outer trim along the alignment lines (D) as shown.

    Adhesive tape 

    (B): Thickness 0.8 mm (0.031 in)
      Width 10 mm (0.39 in)
      Length 280 mm (11.02 in)
    (C): Thickness 0.8 mm (0.031 in)
      Width 15 mm (0.59 in)
      Length 280 mm (11.02 in)
