Removal and Installation

NOTE: How to read the torque specifications .

2/4-door 

  1. Rear Shelf - Remove
  2. Rear Left Seat Belt Buckle - Remove (Rear Center Seat Belt Assembly) 
  3. Rear Seat Belt Assembly - Remove
    Fig 1: Rear Seat Belt Lower Anchor Bolt With Torque Specifications (2/4-Door)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    1. Remove the lower anchor bolt (A).
    Fig 2: Rear Seat Belt (Left/Right) Assembly With Torque Specifications (2/4-Door)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    Fig 3: Rear Seat Belt (Center) Assembly With Torque Specifications (2/4-Door)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    2. Remove the seat belt assembly (A).
  4. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.

5-door (Rear Left/Right Seat Belt) 

  1. C-Pillar Trim - Remove
  2. Rear Left/Right Seat Belt Assembly - Remove
    Fig 4: Rear Seat Belt Lower Anchor Bolt With Torque Specifications (5-Door - Rear Left/Right Seat Belt)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    1. Remove the lower anchor bolt (A).
    Fig 5: Rear Left/Right Seat Belt Assembly Components With Torque Specifications (5-Door - Rear Left/Right Seat Belt)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    2. Remove the rear left/right seat belt assembly (A).
  3. Webbing Guide/Anchor Wire - Remove

    1. Cut the tie laps (A).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Remove the webbing guide (B) and the anchor wire (C).

  4. All Removed Parts - Install

    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.

    NOTE:  If you are installing a new rear seat belt (A) to replace the part of the following.

    • Webbing guide (B)
    • Tie lap (C)
    • Washer (D)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
