Brake Booster Vacuum Pump Removal, Installation, and Test (Type-R) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Inspection

  1. Vacuum Pump - Test
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Disconnect the vacuum hose (A).

    2. Connect it to the vacuum gauge and the tube joint adapter, then connect the plug (B) as shown.

    NOTE:  Wrap the thread seal tape around the threads of the vacuum gauge, when connecting the vacuum gauge to the tube joint adapter.

    3. Start the engine, and wait for 90 seconds. The vacuum should be above 80 kPa (600 mmHg, 23.6 inHg).

    • If the vacuum is OK, the test is complete.
    • If the vacuum is out of specification, replace the vacuum pump.
