Aftermarket Roof Racks Can Cause Roof Damage (A16090C)

WARNING: This page is about the Civic EX-L, 2D Coupe, which is a different variant/trim than selected.
Publication date: 2016-09-01
Reference number: A16090C

AFTERMARKET ROOF RACKS CAN CAUSE ROOF DAMAGE

AFTERMARKET ROOF RACKS CAN CAUSE ROOF DAMAGE

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): A16090C, Date of Issue:  September, 2016
HONDA: All Models

AFFECTED VEHICLES

General Information

Do you have a vehicle in your shop for any of these concerns?

  • Dents in the roof
  • Scratches in the roof paint
  • Cracks in the moonroof glass (if equipped)

If the vehicle has an aftermarket roof rack, it could be the culprit. The only roof racks that we approve of are listed in the applicable Accessories and Touch-Up Paint  parts information bulletins (PIBs).

Keep in mind any damage caused by an aftermarket roof rack is not  our responsibility and is not  covered by warranty.
