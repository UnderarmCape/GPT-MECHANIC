Mainshaft Disassembly, Reassembly, and Inspection (Except K20C1 (M/T)): Disassembly: Procedure

Detailed information, notes, and precautions
Replace
  1. Ball Bearing - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Support 6th gear (A) on steel blocks, and press the mainshaft out of the angular ball bearing (B) using an attachment (C) and a press (D).
    NOTE:  Do not use a jaw-type puller; it can damage the gear teeth.
  2. 6th Gear - Remove
  3. Needle Bearing - Remove
  4. 6th Gear Distance Collar - Remove
  5. Synchro Ring - Remove
  6. Synchro Spring - Remove
  7. 5th/6th Synchro Sleeve - Remove
  8. 5th/6th Synchro Hub - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Support 5th gear (A) on steel blocks, and press the mainshaft out of the 5th/6th synchro hub (B) using an attachment (C) and a press (D).
    NOTE:  Do not use a jaw-type puller; it can damage the gear teeth.
  9. Synchro Spring - Remove
  10. Synchro Ring - Remove
  11. 5th Gear - Remove
  12. Needle Bearing - Remove
  13. 4th/5th Gear Distance Collar - Remove
  14. Needle Bearing - Remove
  15. 4th Gear - Remove
  16. Synchro Ring - Remove
  17. Synchro Spring - Remove
  18. 3rd/4th Synchro Sleeve - Remove
  19. 3rd/4th Synchro Hub - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Support 3rd gear (A) on steel blocks, and press the mainshaft out of the 3rd/4th synchro hub (B) and 3rd gear using an attachment (C) and a press (D).
    NOTE:  Do not use a jaw-type puller; it can damage the gear teeth.
  20. Synchro Spring - Remove
  21. Synchro Ring - Remove
  22. 3rd Gear - Remove
  23. Needle Bearing - Remove
  24. Angular Ball Bearing - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Support the angular ball bearing (A) on steel blocks, and press out the mainshaft using an attachment (B) and a press (C).
