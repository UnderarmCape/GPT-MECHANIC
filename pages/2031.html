No sound is heard from all the speakers (display is normal) (With Stereo Amplifier, Without Navigation)

NOTE: 

  • Check the vehicle 12 volt battery condition first .
  • If the center display unit displays an error message, follow the instructions .
  • Before doing this troubleshooting, go to Audio unit power will not turn on (No information display) troubleshooting .
  • Check the connectors for poor connections or loose terminals.
  1. Operation check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Check that the volume level is not set to the MIN level.

    Is it at the MIN level?

    YES 

    Raise the volume level, and recheck the function.

    NO: USA and Canada production

    Go to step 4.

    NO: Except USA and Canada production

    Go to step 2.

  2. Audio system check

    -1. Check if the center display unit keeps indicating audio mute indication.

    Is audio mute indication displayed?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Go to step 4.

  3. Open wire check (ACC line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    Audio unit connector A (24P)

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -4. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      Audio unit connector A (24P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector A (24P) No. 13
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    Replace the audio unit .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the wire.

  4. System Diagnostic Mode check

    -1. Go into the System Diagnostic Mode, and use the System Links to check the AMP icon status .

    Is the AMP icon green?

    YES 

    Go to step 5.

    NO 

    Go to step 6.

  5. Problem verification

    -1. Go into the System Diagnostic Mode, and use the speaker check mode .

    Did all speakers produce a tone?

    YES 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose connections at the audio unit, the stereo amplifier, and speakers. If the tone is weak or distorted, go to Audio system sound is weak or distorted (display is normal) troubleshooting .

    NO 

    The failure is duplicated, go to step 16.

  6. Fuse check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Check the following fuse.

    USA and Canada production 

    Fuse No. A20

    Except USA and Canada production 

    Fuse No. A1-4

    Is the fuse OK?

    YES 

    Go to step 7.

    NO: USA and Canada production

    Replace the fuse, and recheck. If the fuse blows again, check for a short in the No. A20 fuse circuit.

    NO: Except USA and Canada production

    Replace the fuse, and recheck. If the fuse blows again, check for a short in the No. A1-4 fuse circuit.

  7. Open wire check (+B AUDIO AMP line)

    -1. Disconnect the following connector.

    Stereo amplifier connector A (18P)

    -2. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Stereo amplifier connector A (18P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Stereo amplifier connector A (18P) No. 3
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    The +B AUDIO AMP wire is OK. Go to step 8.

    NO 

    Repair an open in the wire.

  8. Determine possible failure area (SWD+B line, others)

    -1. Disconnect the following connector.

    Stereo amplifier connector C (16P)

    -2. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -3. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      Stereo amplifier connector A (18P): disconnected
      Stereo amplifier connector C (16P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Stereo amplifier connector C (16P) No. 14
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    The SWD+B wire is OK. Go to step 11.

    NO 

    Go to step 9.

  9. Open wire check (SWD+B line)

    -1. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
      Stereo amplifier connector A (18P): disconnected
      Stereo amplifier connector C (16P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector A (24P) No. 21
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there battery voltage?

    YES 

    Repair an open in the wire.

    NO 

    The SWD+B wire is not open. Go to step 10.

  10. Shorted wire check (SWD+B line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connectors.

    Audio unit connector A (24P)
    Active sound control unit 20P connector ('20-21Si)

    -3. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Stereo amplifier connector A (18P): disconnected
      Stereo amplifier connector C (16P): disconnected
      Audio unit connector A (24P): disconnected
      Active sound control unit 20P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector A (24P) No. 21
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there 1 MΩ or more?

    YES 

    Replace the audio unit .

    NO 

    Repair a short to body ground in the wire.

  11. Open wire check (GND line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Reconnect stereo amplifier connector A (18P).

    -3. Reconnect stereo amplifier connector C (16P).

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -5. Measure the voltage between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle ON mode
    Test point 1 Stereo amplifier connector A (18P) No. 1
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there less than 0.2 V?

    YES 

    The GND wire is OK. Go to step 12.

    NO 

    Repair an open or high resistance in the wire or poor ground (G504).

  12. Shorted wire check (RS485+ line, RS485- line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connectors.

    Audio unit connector E (16P)
    Stereo amplifier connector C (16P)

    -3. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio unit connector E (16P): disconnected
      Stereo amplifier connector C (16P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector E (16P) No. 7
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector E (16P) No. 8
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there 1 MΩ or more?

    YES 

    The RS485+ and the RS485-  wires are not shorted to ground. Go to step 13.

    NO 

    There is a short to body ground in the wire(s). Replace the affected shielded harness.

  13. Shorted wire check (RS485+ line to RS485- line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio unit connector E (16P): disconnected
      Stereo amplifier connector C (16P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector E (16P) No. 7
    Test point 2 Audio unit connector E (16P) No. 8

    Is there 1 MΩ or more?

    YES 

    The RS485+ and the RS485-  wires are not shorted. Go to step 14.

    NO 

    There is a short in the wires. Replace the affected shielded harness.

  14. Shorted wire check (AMP RS485 SH line to another lines)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio unit connector E (16P): disconnected
      Stereo amplifier connector C (16P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector E (16P) No. 6
    Test point 2 Audio unit connector E (16P) No. 7
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector E (16P) No. 6
    Test point 2 Audio unit connector E (16P) No. 8

    Is there 1 MΩ or more?

    YES 

    The AMP RS485 SH wire is OK. Go to step 15.

    NO 

    There is a short in the wires. Replace the affected shielded harness.

  15. Open wire check (RS485+ line, RS485- line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio unit connector E (16P): disconnected
      Stereo amplifier connector C (16P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector E (16P) No. 7
    Test point 2 Stereo amplifier connector C (16P) No. 15
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector E (16P) No. 8
    Test point 2 Stereo amplifier connector C (16P) No. 7

    Is there 1.0 Ω or less?

    YES 

    The RS485+ and the RS485-  wires are OK. Go to step 19.

    NO 

    There is an open in the wire(s). Replace the affected shielded harness.

  16. Shorted wire check (SPDIF2 SIG line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connectors.

    Audio unit connector M (4P)
    Stereo amplifier connector D (2P)

    -3. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio unit connector M (4P): disconnected
      Stereo amplifier connector D (2P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector M (4P) No. 1
    Test point 2 Body ground

    Is there 1 MΩ or more?

    YES 

    The SPDIF2 SIG wire is not shorted to ground. Go to step 17.

    NO 

    There is a short to body ground in the wire(s). Replace the affected shielded harness.

  17. Shorted wire check (SPDIF2 SIG line to SPDIF2 SH line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio unit connector M (4P): disconnected
      Stereo amplifier connector D (2P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector M (4P) No. 1
    Test point 2 Audio unit connector M (4P) No. 3

    Is there 1 MΩ or more?

    YES 

    The SPDIF2 SIG and the SPDIF2 SH wires are not shorted. Go to step 18.

    NO 

    There is a short in the wires. Replace the affected shielded harness.

  18. Open wire check (SPDIF2 SIG line)

    -1. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      Audio unit connector M (4P): disconnected
      Stereo amplifier connector D (2P): disconnected
    Test point 1 Audio unit connector M (4P) No. 1
    Test point 2 Stereo amplifier connector D (2P) No. 1

    Is there 1.0 Ω or less?

    YES 

    The SPDIF2 SIG wire is OK. Go to step 19.

    NO 

    There is an open in the wire(s). Replace the affected shielded harness.

  19. Stereo amplifier check

    -1. Substitute a known-good stereo amplifier .

    -2. Reconnect all the connectors, and recheck.

    Does the symptom go away?

    YES 

    Replace the original stereo amplifier.

    NO 

    Replace the audio unit .
