Engine Oil Pressure Test (K20C1) (2017 2018 2019 2020 2021): Test

NOTE:  If the low oil pressure indicator stays on with the engine running, check the engine oil level . If the oil level is correct:

  1. Rocker Arm Oil Pressure Sensor - Remove
  2. Engine Oil Pressure - Test

    1. Install the oil pressure hose, the pressure gauge adapter, the A/T pressure adapter, the A/T pressure test hose, and the A/T low pressure gauge w/panel as shown, then install the rocker arm oil pressure sensor to the pressure gauge adapter.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Start the engine. Shut it off immediately if the gauge registers no oil pressure. Repair the problem before continuing.

    3. Allow the engine to reach operating temperature (fan comes on at least twice). The pressure should be:

    Engine Oil Temperature:  176 deg.F (80 deg.C) 

    Engine Oil Pressure 

    At Idle:  100 kPa (1.02 kgf/cm 2, 14.5 psi) min. 
    At 3, 000 rpm:  400 kPa (4.08 kgf/cm 2, 58.0 psi) min. 

    4. If oil pressure is out of specifications, inspect these items:

    • Oil filter blocked or plugged.
    • Oil strainer blocked or plugged.
    • Inspect the oil pump relief valve .
  3. All Special Tools - Remove
  4. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal.
