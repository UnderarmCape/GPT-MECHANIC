DTC C0061-62, C0061-F0 (VSA Control Unit)

DTC C0061-62  : Lateral Acceleration Sensor Failure (Signal Compare Failure)

DTC C0061-F0  : Lateral Acceleration Sensor Malfunction

NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
C0061-62 Lateral Acceleration Sensor Failure (Signal Compare Failure)    
C0061-F0 Lateral Acceleration Sensor Malfunction    

DTCs (VSA)

  1. DTC check

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    Test-drive the vehicle under these conditions:

    • Vehicle speed at 31 mph (50 km/h) or more for 10 seconds or more.
    • Change lanes a few times.

    NOTE:  Drive the vehicle on the road, not on a lift.

    -5. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC Freeze Frame
    C0061-62 Lateral Acceleration Sensor Failure (Signal Compare Failure)    
    C0061-F0 Lateral Acceleration Sensor Malfunction    

    Is DTC C0061-62 or C0061-F0 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Replace the SRS unit .

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting . If the freeze data/on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the Freeze data/on-board snapshot.
