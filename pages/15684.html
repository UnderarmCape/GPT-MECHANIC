Removal and Installation

SRS components are located in this area. Review the SRS component locations and the precautions and procedures before doing repairs or service.

NOTE:  Do not turn the vehicle to the ON mode, while removing or installing the driver's seat position sensor.

  1. Driver's seat - Move
    1. Move the driver's seat to gain access to the driver's seat position sensor.
  2. Driver's Seat Position Sensor - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. From under the driver's seat, disconnect the connector (A).

    2. Remove the nut (B), then remove the driver's seat position sensor (C).
  3. All Removed Parts - Install
    1. Install all of the removed parts in reverse order.
    NOTE:  Be sure to install the harness so it is not pinched or interferes with other parts.
  4. Driver's Seat Position Sensor - Operation Check
  5. SRS Operation - Confirm
    1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, and check that the SRS indicator comes on for about 6 seconds and then goes off.
