VSA System Description - VSA Modulator (2019 2020 2021)

The VSA modulator unit consists of the inlet solenoid valve, the outlet solenoid valve, the VSA NO (normally open) solenoid valve, the VSA NC (normally closed) solenoid valve, the reservoir, the pump, and the pump motor. The hydraulic control has four modes; pressure intensifying, pressure retaining, pressure reducing, and pressure intensifying mode.
