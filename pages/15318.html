Manual Transmission Symptom Troubleshooting Index (M/T)

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Symptom Diagnostic procedure Also Check for
Hard to shift into 1st gear
  • Check the clutch .
  • Check and/or replace the MTF .
  • Check the change lever assembly.
  • Check the 1st synchro ring and 1st gear .
  • Check the 1st/2nd synchro sleeve and hub .
  
Hard to shift into 2nd gear
  • Check and/or replace the MTF .
  • Check the clutch .
  • Check the change lever assembly.
  • Check the 2nd synchro ring and 2nd gear .
  • Check the 1st/2nd synchro sleeve and hub .
  
Hard to shift into 3rd gear
  • Check and/or replace the MTF .
  • Check the clutch .
  • Check the change lever assembly.
  • Check the 3rd synchro ring and 3rd gear .
  • Check the 3rd/4th synchro sleeve and hub .
  
Hard to shift into 4th gear
  • Check and/or replace the MTF .
  • Check the clutch .
  • Check the change lever assembly.
  • Check the 4th synchro ring and 4th gear .
  • Check the 3rd/4th synchro sleeve and hub .
  
Hard to shift into 5th gear
  • Check and/or replace the MTF .
  • Check the clutch .
  • Check the change lever assembly.
  • Check the 5th synchro ring and 5th gear .
  • Check the 5th/6th synchro sleeve and hub .
  
Hard to shift into 6th gear
  • Check and/or replace the MTF .
  • Check the clutch .
  • Check the change lever assembly.
  • Check the 6th synchro ring and 6th gear .
  • Check the 5th/6th synchro sleeve and hub .
  
Hard to shift into reverse
  • Check the clutch .
  • Check and/or replace the MTF .
  • Check the change lever assembly.
  • Check the reverse shift fork and reverse idler gear .
  • Check reverse gears.

Symptom Diagnostic procedure Also Check for
Noise from the transmission
  • Check and/or replace the MTF .
  • Check the MTF level (unfilled or in surplus the transmission with the MTF).
  • Check the transmission gears.
  • Check the transmission bearings.
  • Check the differential carrier, the final driven gear, and the carrier bearings.
  
Shift lever does not operate smoothly
  • Check and/or replace the MTF .
  • Check the change wire and their joints .
  • Check the shift lever housing with the shift lever shaft.
  • Check the change lever assembly.
  
Transmission jumps out of gear
  • Check and/or replace the MTF .
  • Check the detent balls and springs for proper size and/or damage .
  • Check the teeth of the synchro rings and the gears .
  • Check for bent, deform, or damage of the shift forks.
