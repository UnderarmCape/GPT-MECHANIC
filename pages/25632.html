Wiper Blade Removal and Installation: Removal

Windshield 

  1. Locking Back Wiper Arms - Procedure
    1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    2. Turn and hold the wiper/washer switch to the MIST position, and then turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    3. Release the wiper/washer switch.

    NOTE:  The wiper arm stops at the lock back position.

    4. Lift the wiper arm(s) off the windshield.
  2. Windshield Wiper Blade - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Lift the wiper arm(s) off the windshield, raising the driver's side first, then the passenger's side.

    2. Pull up the tab (A).

    3. Slide the windshield wiper blade assembly (B) off the windshield wiper arm (C).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Find the end of the blade holder labeled "SET" (A), and then pull back the wiper blade (B).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 5. Pull out the wiper blade (A) through the wiper blade opening.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 6. If necessary, remove the end cap (A) by pressing tab (B).
    NOTE:  When removing it, take care not to damage the rubber holder (C).

Rear Window (With Rear Window Wiper) 

  1. Rear Window Wiper Blade - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Lift the wiper arm off the rear window.

    2. Pull up the tab (A).

    3. Slide the rear window wiper blade assembly (B) off the rear window wiper arm (C).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. Find the end of the blade holder labeled "SET" (A), and then pull back the wiper blade (B).
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 5. Pull out the wiper blade (A) through the wiper blade opening.
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 6. If necessary, remove the end cap (A) by pressing tab (B).
    NOTE:  When removing it, take care not to damage the rubber holder (C).
