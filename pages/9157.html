DTC P0602 (K20C2)

DTC P0602  : PCM Programming Error

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • This DTC is indicated when the PCM update procedure is not completed.
  • Do not turn the vehicle to the other than ON mode while updating the PCM. If you do, the PCM can be damaged.
