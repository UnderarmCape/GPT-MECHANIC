DTC U0122, U0128, U0131, U0151, U0155, U129E (K20C1: Without CAN gateway) (17-21)

DTC U0122  : F-CAN Malfunction (PCM-VSA Modulator-Control Unit)

DTC U0128  : F-CAN Malfunction (PCM-Electric Parking Brake Control Unit)

DTC U0131  : F-CAN Malfunction (PCM-EPS Control Unit)

DTC U0151  : F-CAN Malfunction (PCM-SRS Unit)

DTC U0155  : F-CAN Malfunction (PCM-Gauge Control Module)

DTC U129E  : F-CAN Malfunction (PCM-Power Control Unit (PCU))

NOTE: 

  • Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .
  • According to the detected DTC(s), check for the power circuit and the ground circuit of the control unit which cannot communicate with the PCM.
  • Before disconnecting the SRS unit connector, do the 12 volt battery terminal disconnection procedure , and wait for at least 3 minutes.
  • Refer to the DTC shown on the display, then inspect the connectors and terminals based on the instructions.

DTC (PGM-FI)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    Clear DTC

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -5. Check for Pending or Confirmed DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description Confirmed DTC Pending DTC

    U0128 F-CAN Malfunction (PCM-Electric Parking Brake Control Unit)

    Are any DTCs indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the transmitting control units and the PCM. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

  2. Open wire check (F-CAN_H line, F-CAN_L line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the receiving control unit connector(s).

    NOTE:  Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute when removing the PCM connector(s).

    PCM connector No. 2 (58P)

    -3. Disconnect the transmitting control unit connector(s).

    Refer to the DTC shown on the display, then disconnect the connector(s) based on the instructions (see table).

    -4. Check for continuity between the receiving control unit and the transmitting control unit on the F-CAN_H circuit and the F-CAN_L circuit.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    The F-CAN_H wire and the F-CAN_L wire are OK.

    Refer to the DTC shown on the display, then substitute or replace the control unit based on the instructions (see table).

    DTC Operation for transmitting control unit
    U0122 Replace the VSA modulator-control unit .
    U0128 Replace the VSA modulator-control unit .
    U0131 Replace the EPS control unit .
    U0151 Substitute the SRS unit . If DTC U0151 goes away after substitution, replace the original SRS unit .
    U0155 Substitute a known-good gauge control module , then recheck. If DTC U0155 goes away after substitution, replace the original gauge control module .
    U129E Substitute a known-good body control module , then recheck. If DTC U129E goes away after substitution, replace the original body control module .

    NO 

    Repair an open in the F-CAN_H wire and/or the F-CAN_L wire between the receiving control unit and the transmitting control unit.
