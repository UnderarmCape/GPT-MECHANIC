BACKGROUND

A table of contents section has been added to the current service manual to help you navigate and find information more quickly and efficiently. This job aid shows you how to navigate the new table of contents.

NOTE: The new table of contents only applies to the 3rd generation service manual, not the 2nd generation.

3 rd  Generation  - Supported

2 nd  Generation  - Not Supported
  • The table of contents works only on browsers other than Internet Explorer. Internet Explorer is NOT  supported.

OVERVIEW

Once you click on a service manual procedure in service information, a table of contents appears on the left-hand side. The procedure you selected is highlighted in gray within the table.

TABLE OF CONTENTS STRUCTURE

The information is organized in major and sub-categories. Click on one of the major sections to view the sub-categories and additional information. To view a procedure, simply click on it.

This new enhancement includes access to general and specific DTC troubleshooting. The troubleshooting is organized by system under the main category, Control System DTCs.

Control System DTCs also contain the DTC troubleshooting index and any available "How to Troubleshoot" information.
NOTE: Some main categories other than the Control System DTC will have a sub-category called Troubleshooting. This sub-category will not have information regarding specific DTC troubleshooting and will only contain symptom-based troubleshooting, general circuit troubleshooting and the troubleshooting index.

This new enhancement also adds body repair information. Depending on the model and model year, this includes information from the body repair basics (BRB), paint repair basics (PRB), and the model-specific body repair manual (BRM).

NOTE: Some older models may not have the body repair basics and paint repair basics as an option in the major categories. Refer to the sub-categories in Body Repair  for more information.

Some information may contain a number within parentheses. This indicates the applicable model year(s). In the example below, Standard and Service Limits (17-18) applies to the 2017-18 models and Under-Hood Emission Control Label (20) applies only to the 2020 model year.

GENERAL OPERATION

Displaying the Table of Contents 

The table of contents is displayed by default. You can hide the table by clicking the MENU icon. Click the icon again to restore the table.

Forward and Back Buttons 

Like with your internet browser, forward and back buttons are available so you can go to the next or previously viewed screen.

Printing 

Click on the PRINT icon to print the procedure. Once the PRINT icon is clicked, it will go directly to the print menu pop-up window.

NEW ICONS

New icons have been added next to each step of a procedure to indicate where the information is.

The separate procedure will open in a new window.

The removal or installation procedure is part of an existing image on the right.

Further details will open in a new window.

The step is shown on the right.

USING WITH THE 4TH GENERATION SERVICE MANUAL

NOTE: The following only applies to 2022 and later Civic models.

The exploded view diagrams of the 4th generation service manual can be found two ways, through the Visual Index or the Exploded View sub-categories under Visual Index and Exploded Views.

The exploded view will show all of the exploded views in alphabetical order.
