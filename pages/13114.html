Rear Right Seat-Back (2/4-door, Split Fold Down Seat) 

  1. Rear Right Seat-Back - Remove
    Fig 1: Rear Right Seat-Back Components With Torque Specifications (2/4-Door, Split Fold Down Seat)
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    1. Fold down the rear right seat-back (A).

    2. Turn over the rear right seat-back covers (B), then remove the bolts (C).

    3. Remove the rear right seat-back.

    Fig 2: Rear Seat Pivot Bracket With Torque Specifications (2/4-Door, Split Fold Down Seat)
