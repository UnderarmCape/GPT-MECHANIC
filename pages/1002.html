DTC U0047-00 (L15B7/L15BA/L15BY: With CAN gateway) (2020 2021)

DTC U0047-00  : CAN Gateway F-CAN ch B Bus Off

NOTE:  Before you troubleshoot, review the general troubleshooting information .

DTC Description DTC
U0047-00 CAN Gateway F-CAN ch B Bus Off  

DTC (CAN gateway)

  1. Problem verification

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -2. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, and wait 6 seconds.

    -5. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
    U0047-00 CAN Gateway F-CAN ch B Bus Off  

    Is DTC U0047-00 indicated?

    YES 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

    NO 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. If the on-board snapshot of this DTC is recorded, try to reproduce the failure under the same conditions with the on-board snapshot .

  2. Shorted unit check (PCM)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector A (50P)

    -4. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    -5. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -6. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -7. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, and wait 6 seconds.

    -8. Check for DTCs with the HDS.

    DTC Description DTC
    U0047-00 CAN Gateway F-CAN ch B Bus Off  

    Is DTC U0047-00 indicated?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If DTC U0047-00 goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .

  8. Shorted unit check (SRS unit)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Do the 12 volt battery terminal disconnection procedure , and wait for at least 3 minutes.

    -6. Clear the DTC with the HDS.

    -7. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -8. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode, and wait 6 seconds.

  9. Shorted wire check (F-CAN B_H line)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Disconnect the following connector.

    CAN gateway 16P connector

    -3. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
      PCM connector A (50P): disconnected
      EPS control unit connector B (6P): disconnected
      Millimeter wave radar 8P connector: disconnected
      Multipurpose camera unit 12P connector: disconnected
      VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector: disconnected
      Adaptive damper control unit 24P connector (with adaptive damper system): disconnected
      SRS unit connector A (39P): disconnected
      CAN gateway 16P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 CAN gateway 16P connector (female terminals) No. 6:
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there 1 MΩ or more?

    YES 

    The F-CAN B_H wire is not shorted to ground. Go to step 10.

    NO 

    Repair a short in the F-CAN B_H wire between the CAN gateway and the connected units.
