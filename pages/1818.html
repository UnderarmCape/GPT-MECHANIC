Resetting the Parking and Back-Up Sensor Control Unit: Procedure

Reset the parking and back-up sensor control unit after doing any of the following actions:

  • When you replace the parking and back-up sensor.
  • When you change the parking and back-up sensor mounting location from the original location.

NOTE:  Before performing the resetting procedure, make sure that each parking and back-up sensor is connected.

  1. Parking and Back-Up Sensor Control Unit - Reset

    Resetting Procedure 

    1. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    2. Press the parking and back-up sensor button 10 times, within 10 seconds.

    NOTE:  The parking and back-up sensor switch indicator will blink.

    3. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    4. Press and hold the parking and back-up sensor button.

    5. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode.

    6. Wait about 10 seconds.

    NOTE:  If the resetting procedure is successful, the parking and back-up sensor buzzer will sound for 2 seconds.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    7. Press the parking and back-up sensor button.

    8. Make sure that the system works normally.
