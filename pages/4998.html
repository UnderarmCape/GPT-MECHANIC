Engine Oil Filter Removal and Installation (K20C2): Installation

  1. Engine Oil Filter - Install
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Apply a light coat of new engine oil to the oil filter rubber seal.

    NOTE:  Use only filters with a built-in bypass system.

    2. Install the oil filter by hand.

    3. After the rubber seal seats, tighten the oil filter clockwise with the oil filter wrench.
    Tighten:  3/4 Turn Clockwise 
    Tightening Torque:  12 N.m (1.2 kgf.m, 9 lbf.ft) 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 4. If four numbers or marks (1 to 4 or ↓ to ↓↓↓↓) are printed around the outside of the filter, use the following procedure to tighten the filter.
    • Spin the filter on until its seal lightly seats against the oil pan, and note which number or mark is at the bottom.
    • Tighten the filter by turning it clockwise three numbers or marks from the one you noted. For example, if mark ↓ is at the bottom when the seal is lightly seated, tighten the filter until the mark ↓↓↓↓ comes around to the bottom.

  2. Engine Oil Filter After Install - Check
    1. After installation, fill the engine with engine oil to the specified level , run the engine for a minimum of 3 minutes, then check for any leaks.

  4. Maintenance Minder - Reset (With Maintenance Minder System)
    1. USA models: If the Maintenance Minder required to replace the oil filter, reset the Maintenance Minder with the gauge (see "Resetting the Maintenance Minder").

    2. Canada models: If the Maintenance Minder required to replace the oil filter, reset the Maintenance Minder with the gauge (see "Resetting the Maintenance Minder"). If the Maintenance Minder did not require to replace the oil filter, reset the Maintenance Minder with the HDS (see "Resetting the Individual Maintenance Items").
