Fuel Tank Unit Removal and Installation (L15BY) (2018 2019 2020): Removal

WARNING: 

Flammable - Keep Fire Away

NOTE:  Refer to the Fuel and Emissions Systems Service Precautions before disconnecting the fuel line.

  1. Fuel Pressure - Relieve
  2. Rear Seat-Back and Rear Seat-Cushion - Remove
  3. Fuel Line Quick-Connect Fitting - Disconnect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the access panel (A) from the floor.

    2. Disconnect the connector (B).

    3. Disconnect the quick-connect fitting

    (C) from the fuel tank unit.
  4. Fuel Tank Unit Cam Lock Ring - Unlock
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Set the fuel sender wrench as shown.

    2. Turn the fuel tank unit cam lock ring (A) counterclockwise.

    NOTE:  Try to keep the fuel sender wrench from floating.
  5. Fuel Tank Unit Cam Lock Ring - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
  6. Fuel Tank Unit - Remove
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Remove the fuel tank unit (A) with the O-ring (B).
    NOTE:  Be careful not to bend or twist the fuel level sensor arm (C) excessively.
