Reverse Lockout System Symptom Troubleshooting - Driver cannot select reverse gear when vehicle speed is 9 mph (15 km/h) or less (L15B7/L15BA/K20C1/K20C2 (M/T))

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

NOTE: 

  • Do not release the clutch pedal if you can select reverse gear while the wheels are driven forward.
  • Refer to How to Troubleshoot Circuits at the PCM connectors in the Fuel and Emissions Systems section before troubleshooting this circuit .
  • Information marked with an asterisk (*1) applies to the L15BA/L15B7 engine, an asterisk (*2) applies to the K20C1 engine, and an asterisk (*3) applies to the K20C2 engine.
  1. Problem verification (without engine running)

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Try to shift the transmission into reverse.

    Can you shift the transmission into the reverse?

    YES 

    Go to step 3.

    NO 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 2.

  2. Reverse lockout solenoid check

    -1. Do the reverse lockout solenoid test .

    Is the reverse lockout solenoid OK?

    YES 

    The reverse lockout solenoid is OK. Check the change lever assembly, or inspect the internal transmission.

    NO 

    Repair or replace the reverse lockout solenoid .

  3. Problem verification (with engine running)

    -1. Start the engine, and try to shift the transmission into reverse.

    Can you shift the transmission into reverse?

    YES 

    Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.

    NO 

    The failure is duplicated. Go to step 4.

  4. Shorted wire check (RVSLCK line *1*2/RVS LCK *3 line):

    -1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF (LOCK) mode.

    -2. Jump the SCS line with the HDS, and wait more than 1 minute.

    -3. Disconnect the following connector.

    PCM connector No. 2 (58P) *2
    PCM connector E (80P) *1*3

    -4. Check for continuity between test points 1 and 2.

    Test condition Vehicle OFF (LOCK) mode
    PCM connector No. 2 (58P) *2: disconnected
    PCM connector E (80P) *1*3: disconnected
    Reverse lockout solenoid 2P connector: disconnected
    Test point 1 Reverse lockout solenoid 2P connector (female terminals) No. 2:
    Test point 2 Body ground
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Is there continuity?

    YES 

    Repair a short to ground in the RVSLCK wire *1*2/RVS LCK *3 wire between the reverse lockout solenoid and the PCM.

    NO 

    The RVSLCK wire *1*2/RVS LCK wire *3 is OK. Check for any authorized service information related to the DTCs or symptoms you are troubleshooting, or substitute a known-good PCM , then recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away and the PCM was substituted, replace the original PCM .
