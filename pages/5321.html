EVAP Canister Purge Valve Removal, Installation, and Inspection (L15B7/L15BA/L15BY): Inspection

WARNING: 

Flammable - Keep Fire Away

NOTE:  Refer to the Fuel and Emissions Systems Service Precautions before disconnecting the fuel line.

Non-Return Valve Inspection (USA/Canada/California models) 

  1. EVAP Canister Purge Valve Hose - Disconnect
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Disconnect the EVAP canister purge valve hoses (A).
  2. HDS - Connect
  3. Non-return Valve - Inspection
    Non-Return Valve A (Intake Manifold Side) 
    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    Non-Return Valve B (Turbocharger Side) 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 1. Select the PGM-FI system with the HDS.

    2. Select the INSPECTION MENU, then select EVAP TEST, and follow the screen prompts.

    3. Select the SINGLE SOLENOID, then select PCS (CPV) ON, and follow the screen prompts.

    4. Connect the vacuum pump (A) to the EVAP canister purge valve (B) as shown.

    5. Apply vacuum to the valve.

    • If it holds vacuum, replace the EVAP canister purge valve.
    • If it doesn't hold the vacuum, go to next step.
    Non-Return Valve A (Intake Manifold Side)

    Non-Return Valve B (Turbocharger Side) 

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 6. Connect the vacuum pump (A) to the EVAP canister purge valve (B) as shown.

    7. Apply pressure to the valve.

    • If it holds pressure, the EVAP canister purge valve is OK.
    • If it doesn't hold the pressure, replace the EVAP canister purge valve.
