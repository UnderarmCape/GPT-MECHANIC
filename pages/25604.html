Side Sill Panel Removal and Installation (2-door) (2016): Removal

Side Sill Panel 

  1. Side Sill Panel - Remove

    1. Remove the clips (A) and the screws (B).

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    2. Open the front door.

    3. Slide the side sill panel (A) forward to remove it.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.

    4. Remove the clips (B) from the body by turning them 45°, then install them to the side sill panel.

    Courtesy of HONDA, U.S.A., INC.
    		 5. If necessary, remove the side sill panel lip seal (A).
